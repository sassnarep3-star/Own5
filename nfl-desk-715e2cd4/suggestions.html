How to use this section

Read the recommendation column, then the cost column. The suggestions with the highest decision value are P01 (buy a licensed feed: it unblocks the entire staking layer), P02 (derive depth from snap counts rather than 32 club pages), P03 (publish designations as a delta series) and P05 (add a second season, the only route to a defensible skill claim). P04 and P11 are removals: they take away a display that currently implies knowledge the desk does not have.

Every numeric value on this site is either computed in-repo from an official nfl.com page or left blank and flagged. Market prices are blank by policy P-01. No backtest figure appears on the leaderboard.  ·  source
