Why win rate and ROI are not ranking metrics here

Ranking by realised return over a season cannot distinguish a good process from a lucky one: at the power published on the removal-of-luck page, a 55% bettor and a 52.4% break-even bettor are statistically indistinguishable until roughly 5,773 bets. A league table built on 200 bets would mostly rank luck. Ranking is therefore by benchmark-relative Brier skill with a game-cluster lower bound and a multiplicity adjustment for the number of trials, and staking is scored from its own observations.

Combos stay behind gate G8

Multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid under dependence. A game parlay, same-game parlay or prop combo may only be ranked when a directly validated joint probability and the operator's void and push rules are both available. Neither is available, so S4-S6 carry no numbers at all.
