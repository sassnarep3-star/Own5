What is not computable, and why

A parlay needs the correlation between its legs. Cross-game legs and margin/total pairs can be estimated from official finals, and are. Player-prop pairs, same-team pairs and same-game-parlay legs cannot be estimated at all without a prop market or a play-by-play source, so no such number appears anywhere on this site (F-02). That absence is the binding constraint on S4, S5 and S6.

Every numeric value on this site is either computed in-repo from an official nfl.com page or left blank and flagged. Market prices are blank by policy P-01. No backtest figure appears on the leaderboard.  ·  source
