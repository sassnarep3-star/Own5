How the luck panel is used

A leaderboard entry is only interesting once it clears the sample size in the first table. Until then every strategy on this site is tagged as noise-eligible, and the only honest ranking statement is "insufficient evidence". That is why the leaderboard has no numbers in it.

Every numeric value on this site is either computed in-repo from an official nfl.com page or left blank and flagged. Market prices are blank by policy P-01. No backtest figure appears on the leaderboard.  ·  source
