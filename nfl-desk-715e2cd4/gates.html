Demotion is a gate too

Gate G7 is the only one that can take something away. Its numeric triggers are on the leaderboard page: a rolling 200-bet benchmark-relative lower bound of zero, mean closing-line value at or below zero over 100 bets, two consecutive calibration bins outside their intervals, or a parser drift alarm. A live strategy that fires any of them returns to paper the same day.

Every numeric value on this site is either computed in-repo from an official nfl.com page or left blank and flagged. Market prices are blank by policy P-01. No backtest figure appears on the leaderboard.  ·  source
