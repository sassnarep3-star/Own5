Why the fair total appears twice

Requirement R13 asks for two estimators until one wins a walk-forward comparison. Estimator A is the sum of the two fitted expected points. Estimator B is the mean of the two clubs' own average combined game total. On the Week 10 holdout both lost to a constant league-mean total, so neither has won. The gap column is published so the disagreement is visible rather than averaged away.

Every numeric value on this site is either computed in-repo from an official nfl.com page or left blank and flagged. Market prices are blank by policy P-01. No backtest figure appears on the leaderboard.  ·  source
