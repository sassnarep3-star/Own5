Snapshot diff first, then the capture log that makes the diff trustworthy.

A diff needs two dated snapshots, and there is one. The desk keeps every snapshot under data/snapshots/<date>/ and renders the change instead of a static table. On the first capture there is nothing to compare, and this page says so rather than inventing a change (F-11). The capture log below is append-only and hash-checked: a re-read with identical bytes is refused as a no-op, and a second change to the same URL on the same day must be logged as a revision, never overwritten.
