What this desk is, and what it will not do

What this desk does
  • Originates its own line for all 13 Week 11 fixtures, side first, then total, then moneyline.
  • Scores the frozen model out of sample on a chronological holdout and publishes the failures.
  • Computes every correlation from official numbers only and prints n next to each one.
  • Tracks 66 requirements against the brief, each with its blocking gate and its clearing number.
  • Publishes 16 reviewable suggestions and 15 questions that must be answered before decisions.
What this desk refuses to do
  • Invent a market price, a depth rank, a starter, a snap share or a win rate.
  • Put a backtest figure on the leaderboard (policy LB-01).
  • Multiply marginal probabilities to price a parlay (gate G8).
  • Infer depth from jersey number, experience or salary (rejected in writing).
  • Say "not listed" when the truth is "on injured reserve" (F-13).
