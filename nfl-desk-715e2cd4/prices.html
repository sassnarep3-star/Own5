Policy first, schema second, honest zero coverage third.

No operator page was fetched, because policy P-01 forbids scraping odds before that operator's terms are read. The store's schema, coverage and policy are published so the gap is visible rather than hidden.

Snapshot schema

Each future price row carries: snapshot_id, game_id, market_type, selection, phase (open/close), line, price_american, operator, jurisdiction, captured_at_utc, source_url, extract_sha256, status. Opening and closing are separate rows, never one "current" number.
