Bet Markets & Grading Rules — Verified Research (T3)

Definitions and grading rules below are quoted/paraphrased from official sportsbook operator pages and regulator-approved house rules — not from third-party blogs. Machine-readable: data/bet-types.csv, data/bet-combos.csv.

Where lines come from: the NFL's Official Sports Betting Partners (Caesars, DraftKings, FanDuel) all use the NFL's official league data feed NFL.com ↗. Live NFL lines are published on the operators' own pages, e.g. DraftKings NFL ↗ and FanDuel NFL ↗. Line values are dynamic and are not snapshotted in this repo (flag F02).

Market definitions

  • Point spread — wager on margin of victory; favorite gives points (minus), underdog gets points (plus); half-point spreads prevent a push. DraftKings NFL page ↗
  • Moneyline — pick the outright winner; margin irrelevant. DraftKings NFL page ↗
  • Total (over/under) — combined points of both teams. FanDuel notes these markets are powered by official NFL data. DraftKings ↗ · FanDuel ↗
  • Parlay — 2+ legs combined into one wager; every leg must win (minimum two legs). FanDuel parlay guide ↗
  • Same Game Parlay (SGP) — multiple bets from the same game (props, spread, totals); the book builds correlation assumptions into the odds, so check the combined price. SGP+ stacks multiple SGPs across games. FanDuel NFL guide ↗
  • Teaser — adjust spread/total by a set number of points (typically 6) across ≥2 legs for reduced odds; most common NFL margins of victory are 3 and 7 — the key numbers teasers cross. FanDuel NFL guide ↗
  • Player props — player/team performance (passing yards, rushing yards, TDs, sacks...). DraftKings ↗
  • Live (in-play) — wagers after kickoff across spread/ML/total/period markets/eligible props. DraftKings ↗
  • Round robin — multiple smaller parlays built from one selection pool. FanDuel NFL guide ↗

Grading rules (what actually settles a bet)

  • Overtime counts for full-game spread, moneyline and totals (graded on final official score inclusive of OT). FanDuel grading FAQ ↗ · FanDuel house rules ↗ · BetMGM rules ↗
  • Push: whole-number spread or total landing exactly on the number → wager refunded; in a parlay the leg is excluded and the parlay re-priced. FanDuel house rules ↗ · FanDuel NFL guide ↗
  • Tie after OT: moneyline wagers void (stake refunded); spread/total settled on posted numbers (push if exactly on the line). FanDuel grading FAQ ↗
  • Postponed before kickoff → all wagers void; relocated but played on the scheduled date → action (graded on the official league box score, not the venue). FanDuel grading FAQ ↗
  • Minimum play: pro football results are official after 55 minutes unless an official result is declared earlier. BetMGM regulator-approved house rules (MA) ↗
  • Player props: void if the player is not listed as active by the official league scoring source. BetMGM rules ↗; official stats via Elias Sports Bureau NFL stat corrections ↗
  • Correlated legs: parlays containing selections where one outcome directly affects another are not permitted unless explicitly offered (e.g., as an SGP); if accepted in error the book may void the bet or settle affected selections as singles. Books also reserve the right to refuse or limit any parlay. FanDuel house rules ↗
