Discrimination and rationale

Use a consistent, lawful calculation and neutral criteria; avoid decisions based on protected characteristics, retaliation, or tenant complaints. A documented maintenance, insurance, tax, or operating-cost change may explain an owner’s business decision, but does not itself authorize an increase beyond applicable rent limits. Do not represent “raising both” as a defense: evaluate each tenancy’s lawful rent history independently.

For a defensible file, preserve rent ledger, lease/addenda, service records, license confirmation, calculations, notice, and delivery evidence. Consider a tenant-side or landlord-tenant attorney review before notice where roommate succession or banked rent is involved.
