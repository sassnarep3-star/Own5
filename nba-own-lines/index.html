What “remove luck” means here

Random variation cannot be deleted from sport. This desk freezes rules before outcomes, requires prospective samples, compares against baselines, applies exact null tests and corrects for multiple comparisons. Until those gates pass, skill, profit and rewards remain unclaimed.
