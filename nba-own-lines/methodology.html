Methodology

From official rows to reproducible research lines

The calculations are deliberately simple enough to audit line by line. Assumptions are disclosed rather than filled in as facts.

Line-origination pipeline

All arithmetic is implemented in model.py

Spread = −rounded projected margin for the home team; total = rounded sum of projected scores; nearest-half rounding is independent.
