Correlation analysis

Measure association. Do not confuse it with an edge.

Pearson r ranges from −1 to +1. These contemporaneous descriptive statistics do not establish prediction, causation, profitability or threshold-specific joint-hit rates.

Multiplicity result: 0 of 16 player tests has Holm-adjusted p < 0.05. Two strong raw associations (Luka PTS×3PM and Shai PTS×AST) do not survive the family-wise correction at that threshold. This is why raw correlation alone cannot qualify a strategy.
