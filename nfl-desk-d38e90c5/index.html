Key findings

  • Walk-forward out-of-sample performance on n = 73 predictions: MAE 10.2379 points, Brier 0.229097 against a 0.244324 base-rate baseline, log loss 0.647968 against 0.681751, winner hit rate 0.6027. A transparent baseline, not a proven edge.
  • NFL.com publishes no betting prices and no operator price feed was reachable from this environment, so every market line, price, difference, CLV and market ROI stays blank and is published on the Flagged for review page.
  • Replacement depth is tracked per player (starter, second, third, fourth string) on offense and on defense, with the named replacement and a depth-deficiency flag.
  • All six strategies are PAPER: the demotion gate fails closed, so nothing is promoted while a required criterion cannot be evaluated.
