Recommended path

  1. Now (done in this session): publish a complete, self-contained, officially sourced desk that

fails closed on prices, separates signal from luck, tracks replacement depth, publishes n with every correlation, and keeps a live demotion gate.

  1. Next (highest value, low effort): SUG-03 (estimate HFA), D-04/D-02 (weekly archives and a

growing calibration sample), D-05/D-06 (close the flagged data gaps).

  1. Then (highest value, higher effort): SUG-01 (price feed) and SUG-04 (player tables). These two

unlock the three strategies that are currently blocked and turn the desk from a model study into a betting record with measurable ROI and CLV.

  1. Separately (repo-wide, other sessions): SUG-07 and SUG-08, coordinated through

COORDINATION.md and finished in a final integration session.

What we are deliberately not doing

  • Not importing third-party price scrapes to fill the market columns.
  • Not promoting any strategy to live while a required criterion is not evaluable.
  • Not editing files owned by other sessions' claims.
