Executive summary - NFL line origination and strategy desk (session d38e90c5)

Status: published as a standalone GitHub Pages desk. Every published table row carries an official source link. Values that cannot be confirmed from an official source are blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page.

Data as of 2026-11-14 (official NFL.com). 137 completed games transcribed from the official Week 1-9 schedule pages plus the completed Week 10 Thursday game; 26 upcoming games (Week 10 and Week 11) with kickoff times, networks and venues; the complete official Week 10 injury report (347 rows); and the official depth chart of all 32 clubs (31 transcribed, Houston published as an image only).

What this desk does

  1. Originates its own NFL lines with a transparent model built only from official numbers:

alternating least-squares offensive and defensive ratings, small-sample shrinkage, and a walk-forward logistic calibration. 297 markets originated (99 spreads, 99 totals, 99 moneylines).

  1. Publishes in the required order - side/spread first, total second, moneyline third - with

model fair line, market line and difference as three separate columns.

  1. Keeps a price-snapshot store with an opening and a closing record for every originated market

(594 records, append-only).

  1. Keeps a verified results feed (137 official final scores) so backtests and ROI can be computed

the moment a price source exists.

  1. Separates signal quality from staking performance on two leaderboards, so a lucky staking run

cannot hide a weak signal (and a strong signal is not credited for a lucky run).

  1. Tracks replacement depth, not just "did a replacement happen": every official injury row is

matched to the club's official depth chart and labelled 1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th string on offense and on defense.

  1. Computes correlations only from officially sourced numbers and publishes n with every value.
  2. Runs a fail-closed demotion gate: every strategy is currently PAPER, because no required

criterion may be silently skipped.

  1. Researches single bets, parlays and player-prop combinations, and records what blocks each

one.

Headline findings

#FindingEvidence
1The model is a usable but unremarkable baseline: 73 walk-forward out-of-sample predictions, MAE 10.24 points, Brier 0.2291 vs a 0.2443 base-rate baseline, log loss 0.6480 vs 0.6818, winner hit rate 60.3%, calibration slope 0.869.data/backtest_predictions.csv, data/leaderboard_signal.csv
2NFL.com publishes no betting prices. nfl.com/betting/ returns 404 and no operator price feed was reachable from this environment. Every market line, market price, difference, CLV and market ROI is therefore blank and flagged.data/flagged_for_review.csv, https://www.nfl.com/betting/
326 of the 28 clubs playing in Week 10 have at least one starter Out on the official injury report; the substitution depth behind each absence is published per player.data/depth_detail.csv, data/depth_team_summary.csv
4Offensive starters out in Week 10 show essentially no relationship to the season point differential (Pearson r = 0.034, n = 28); defensive starters out show a weak negative relationship with points allowed per game (r = -0.1587, n = 28). One injury week cannot separate injury effect from team quality.data/correlations.csv
5Staking results priced at the model's own fair price are pure luck (expected profit is zero by construction). The leaderboard therefore reports them separately from signal quality and the gate never lets them promote a strategy.data/leaderboard_staking.csv, data/gate_decisions.csv
6The Houston Texans publish their depth chart as an image only, so all 16 Texans depth values are blank pending manual review.data/flagged_for_review.csv, https://www.houstontexans.com/team/depth-chart

What is deliberately blank

Market line, market price, difference, closing-line value, market ROI, player-prop lines and the player-prop backtest. They are not estimated, not filled from a third-party scrape, and not carried over from an older desk. Each one has an entry on the Flagged for Review page with the manual-review link and the action needed to close it.
