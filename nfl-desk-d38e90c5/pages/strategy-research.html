Strategy research: single bets, parlays and player-prop combinations

Every strategy below states its selection rule, the price it is priced at, the measured result and the gate status. The full gate table is in data/gate_decisions.csv; the leaderboards are in data/leaderboard_signal.csv and data/leaderboard_staking.csv.

Pricing rule for this desk. No primary market price is available, so every strategy that can be measured is priced at the model's own fair price. At a fair price the expected profit is exactly zero, so the realised result of any of these strategies is luck, not skill. That is the reason the two leaderboards are separate and the reason a staking run can never promote a strategy.

S1 - Model spread favourite (single bet)

  • Rule: take the side the model prefers when |model margin| >= 3.5 points.
  • Price basis: the model's own fair price for that side (cover probability from the model's

margin distribution).

  • Measured: 16 bets, 50.0% hit rate, -2.19 units realised, ROI -13.7% per bet at the model's own

fair price.

  • Reads: the loss is not luck - it is a signal defect, and the leaderboard exposes it. The

model's cover probability is derived from the margin noise scale that was calibrated on win/loss outcomes, and it is overconfident for cover outcomes, where the model's margin error (MAE 10.24 points) is large relative to a 3.5-point threshold. The honest use of this strategy is as a calibration exercise, not as a recommendation.

S2 - Model moneyline favourite (single bet)

  • Rule: back the model side when the calibrated win probability is at least 0.60.
  • Price basis: model fair price.
  • Measured: 37 bets, 70.3% hit rate, +0.12 units realised - a result that sits inside the

95% luck band of +/-7.90 units, so it is indistinguishable from luck. That is exactly the case the gate is built to catch, and it is why the strategy stays in PAPER.

S3 - Two-leg parlay of model favourites (parlay)

  • Rule: in each week, pair the two most confident model sides into a two-leg parlay.
  • Price basis: the combined model fair price (product of the leg probabilities).
  • Measured: 17 parlays, 41.2% hit rate, -3.32 units realised, also inside the luck band.
  • Reads: parlay construction adds variance without adding skill when the legs are priced at fair

probabilities. The realised loss is larger than either single-bet strategy over a comparable number of bets because parlay outcomes are rare events.

  • Research note: parlay construction adds variance without adding skill when the legs are priced

at fair probabilities. A parlay only becomes interesting when the legs are priced below their model probabilities, which requires a real price feed. The repo's third-party price work (nfl-desk-26d4cafb/data/oos_week9.csv, labelled "ESPN/DraftKings -- THIRD PARTY") shows what that looks like, but those numbers are not official and are therefore not used here.

S4 - Value versus the market line (single bet, blocked)

  • Rule: bet where the model line differs from the market line by at least a point.
  • Status: blocked. NFL.com publishes no market line, and no operator price feed was reachable from

this environment. The market line, market price, difference, CLV and ROI columns are blank and flagged. This is the single highest-value unlock in the whole desk: it converts the pack from a model study into a betting record.

S5 - Replacement-depth adjusted line (single bet, prospective)

  • Rule: shift the model line when a starter is Out and the next in line at that position is a

third- or fourth-string reserve.

  • Status: paper track from Week 10. The official NFL injury report is a current week page -

there is no official archive of historical Week 1-9 injury reports on nfl.com - so a walk-forward backtest of a depth adjustment is not possible with official inputs today.

  • What exists already: 347 official injury rows, 32 official depth charts, and a per-player

replacement tier for every row that the club charts cover. From Week 10 forward the desk can freeze each week's injury report and build the missing history (see discovery step D-04).

  • Interim evidence: the Week 10 cross-section (C4, C5 in data/correlations.csv) shows no

relationship between offensive starters out and season point differential (r = 0.034, n = 28) and a weak negative relationship for defensive starters out versus points allowed per game (r = -0.1587, n = 28). One week of injuries cannot separate injury effect from team quality.

S6 - Player-prop combinations (player prop, blocked)

  • Rule: combine player-level official box scores (passing, rushing, receiving yards) with the

replacement-depth state, then look for prop lines that sit below the model probability.

  • Status: blocked on two independent requirements:
  1. Player-level official data: the NFL Game Center exposes a Game Book PDF (for example the

official Week 10 Washington-New York book at https://static.www.nfl.com/image/upload/v1794570519/gamecenter/ab5828d9-4feb-11f1-abca-2c54536568a9.pdf). Parsing that into a machine-readable player table is a defined discovery step (D-03), not a guess. The NFL.com team-stat pages were rejected as a source because their multi-value tiles cannot be paired with their labels without ambiguity - the values are left blank rather than inferred.

  1. Prop prices: no official prop line exists, so no prop edge can be computed.
  • Research note: prop strategies also inherit the same luck problem. A prop bet at the model's

own fair price has zero expected profit; only a price feed turns it into an edge.

Validation gates (fail-closed)

Ten criteria per strategy, published row by row in data/gate_decisions.csv:

#CriterionType
1Every input traces to an official NFL or official club sourceprovenance
2No look-ahead: a prediction uses only earlier weeksstructural
3Minimum out-of-sample sample size (n >= 30)signal
4Brier score at or better than the league base-rate baselinesignal
5Log loss at or better than the league base-rate baselinesignal
6Calibration slope inside the 0.8 - 1.2 bandsignal
7Realised staking result is separated from signal qualityluck control
8Staking profit is never used alone to reward a strategyluck control
9Market ROI and closing-line value against a real pricemarket (not evaluable today)
10Break-even hit rate at a real offered pricemarket (not evaluable today)

Decision rule. Any FAIL -> PAPER. Any NOT_EVALUABLE -> PAPER (fail closed). Only a strategy whose every live criterion is PASS may be LIVE_ELIGIBLE. A strategy that is already live is moved back to PAPER by the same rule - that is the demotion gate, and it is exercised on every build.

Current outcome: all six strategies are PAPER, because criteria 9 and 10 cannot be evaluated without a price source, and S4/S5/S6 cannot even measure their signal yet. No strategy is promoted on the strength of a staking run.
