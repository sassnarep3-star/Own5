Injuries, positional depth and depth deficiencies

Inputs: the official NFL.com Week 10 injury report (347 rows, 28 clubs - the four clubs on a Week 10 bye do not publish an injury list that week) and the official depth chart of all 32 clubs (31 transcribed; Houston publishes an image only).

What is published

data/depth_detail.csv has one row per official injury row, with:

FieldMeaning
team, player, listed_position, injuryexactly as published on the official injury report
practice_statusverbatim official practice designation
game_statusverbatim official game designation (blank means no designation was published)
absence_classOut, Doubtful, Questionable or No designation published
unit, depth_positionOffense / Defense / Special Teams and the club's own position label
depth_slot, depth_tier1 = first string; tier reads 1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th string
replacement_player, replacement_depth_tierwho is next in line at that position and their tier
depth_deficiencyset when a starter is Out and the next in line is a 3rd or 4th string reserve
source_url, depth_source_urlthe official injury page and the official club depth chart

data/depth_team_summary.csv aggregates per club: starters out on offense, starters out on defense, doubtful and questionable starters, the count of depth deficiencies, a severity score (offensive starter 3.0, defensive starter 2.5, other starter 1.0) and the injured starter names.

Week 10 shape

  • 347 official rows: 67 Out, 10 Doubtful, 85 Questionable, 185 with no published designation.
  • 61 rows have no entry on the club's published depth chart, so their depth tier is blank:
  • 16 Houston rows - the club publishes its chart as an image only;
  • 45 rows at other clubs - practice participants, players on other units' lists, or players the

club has not placed on its chart. Each is flagged, not inferred.

  • Blank official game-status cells are recorded as "No designation published" and are never

treated as healthy.

Reading the tracker correctly

  • A Out designation on a third-string reserve is a much smaller event than an Out on the first

string, and the tracker keeps them separate on purpose.

  • A deficiency flag means the club has published no adequate backup at that position, not that the

team will necessarily play worse; the correlation table shows how little a single week's absences explain.

  • Cross-club depth charts are not a substitute for a game-specific inactive list: an injury report

row is the official record of availability, the depth chart is the official record of order.
