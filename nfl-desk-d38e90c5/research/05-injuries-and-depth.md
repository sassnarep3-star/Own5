# Injuries, positional depth and depth deficiencies

Inputs: the official NFL.com Week 10 injury report (347 rows, 28 clubs - the four clubs on a Week 10
bye do not publish an injury list that week) and the official depth chart of all 32 clubs
(31 transcribed; Houston publishes an image only).

## What is published

`data/depth_detail.csv` has one row per official injury row, with:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `team`, `player`, `listed_position`, `injury` | exactly as published on the official injury report |
| `practice_status` | verbatim official practice designation |
| `game_status` | verbatim official game designation (blank means no designation was published) |
| `absence_class` | `Out`, `Doubtful`, `Questionable` or `No designation published` |
| `unit`, `depth_position` | Offense / Defense / Special Teams and the club's own position label |
| `depth_slot`, `depth_tier` | 1 = first string; tier reads 1st / 2nd / 3rd / 4th string |
| `replacement_player`, `replacement_depth_tier` | who is next in line at that position and their tier |
| `depth_deficiency` | set when a starter is Out and the next in line is a 3rd or 4th string reserve |
| `source_url`, `depth_source_url` | the official injury page and the official club depth chart |

`data/depth_team_summary.csv` aggregates per club: starters out on offense, starters out on defense,
doubtful and questionable starters, the count of depth deficiencies, a severity score (offensive
starter 3.0, defensive starter 2.5, other starter 1.0) and the injured starter names.

## Week 10 shape

* 347 official rows: 67 `Out`, 10 `Doubtful`, 85 `Questionable`, 185 with no published designation.
* 61 rows have no entry on the club's published depth chart, so their depth tier is blank:
  * 16 Houston rows - the club publishes its chart as an image only;
  * 45 rows at other clubs - practice participants, players on other units' lists, or players the
    club has not placed on its chart. Each is flagged, not inferred.
* Blank official game-status cells are recorded as "No designation published" and are **never**
  treated as healthy.

## Reading the tracker correctly

* A `Out` designation on a third-string reserve is a much smaller event than an `Out` on the first
  string, and the tracker keeps them separate on purpose.
* A deficiency flag means the club has published no adequate backup at that position, not that the
  team will necessarily play worse; the correlation table shows how little a single week's absences
  explain.
* Cross-club depth charts are not a substitute for a game-specific inactive list: an injury report
  row is the official record of availability, the depth chart is the official record of order.

## Known irregularities

| Item | Detail | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Kansas City injury rows | the official capture for Kansas City was truncated at a page-chunk boundary, so the Chiefs block may be incomplete | re-capture and re-transcribe; flagged |
| Tennessee injury rows | delivered in two non-contiguous page segments and assembled by hand | re-capture and confirm row order; flagged |
| Houston depth chart | published only as a raster image | manual review of the image, then hand transcription; all Texans depth values are blank meanwhile |
| Players on two clubs' charts | some official club charts place a player on a chart that does not match the club named on the injury report (for example Kenneth Walker appears on the Chiefs chart while the injury report lists him with Tennessee) | depth matching therefore uses the club named on the injury report; unmatched rows are flagged |
