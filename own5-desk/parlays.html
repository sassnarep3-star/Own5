Cross-game discipline

Two distinct games and no shared team are necessary but not sufficient for independence. Residual dependence must be tested. Pushes and voids remove legs only under the supplied settlement contract.

Same-game discipline

Shared pace, script and target share can change joint probability. The engine requires an operator-specific repricing function for SGP void/push cases; it never defaults to multiplying prices.
