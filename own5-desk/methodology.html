Methodology

How independent lines are formed, how dependence is handled and why qualification is fail-closed.

Line originator

attack_i = (PF_i + k·μ) / (n_i + k) defense_i = (PA_i + k·μ) / (n_i + k) λ_home = attack_home · defense_away / μ λ_away = attack_away · defense_home / μ

μ uses total team games, not matchups. Fair spread/total are distribution medians; ties and pushes retain explicit mass.

Skill before rewards

Forecast skill, economic skill, settlement integrity, dependence and reproducibility are separate gates. A profitable-looking sample is not enough.

Unknown ≠ pass. Synthetic tests ≠ leaderboard results. Official inputs ≠ NFL endorsement of a model.
