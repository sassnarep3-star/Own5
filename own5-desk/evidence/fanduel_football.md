# FanDuel official football help — retrieved 2026-11-02 UTC
Source: https://support.fanduel.com/s/article/Betting-football-with-FanDuel
These are operator-specific statements, not universal sportsbook rules.
NFL player props are settled as normal if the player is active and plays at least one snap. If the player is inactive, or active but does not play a snap, applicable player prop bets are void.
For touchdown scorer bets, the selected player or defense must score the touchdown by running the ball into the end zone or catching a touchdown pass. A player who throws a touchdown pass is NOT considered the touchdown scorer.
Yards and receptions recorded during a two-point attempt do not count toward standard yardage or reception totals.
Football bets are settled using official game results and statistics. Some markets have specific settlement rules, so review the market details in your betslip before placing a bet.
