## Scoring
Source: https://static.www.nfl.com/image/upload/fl_attachment/league/tqivdkzt9mu6wdgsh1ku.pdf
Official-domain search extraction of the 2026 PDF, Rule 11 Section 1 Article 2:
(a) Touchdown: 6 points
(b) Field goal: 3 points
(c) Safety: 2 points
(d) Try after touchdown: 1 point (field goal or safety) or 2 points (touchdown)
## 2025 overtime change
Source: https://operations.nfl.com/rules-officiating/featured-rules
NFL teams adopted to align the postseason and regular season overtime rules by granting both teams an opportunity to possess the ball regardless of the outcome of the first possession, subject to a 10-minute overtime period in the regular season.

## GSIS provenance and corrections
Source: https://www.nflgsis.com/Help/About.html
GSIS is used league-wide to capture play and statistical information from every NFL stadium.
Corrections to existing game information will be made regularly in conjunction with Elias Sports Bureau, the official statistician of the NFL.

## Totals
Source: https://support.nfl.com/hc/en-us/articles/35869727578900-What-is-a-total
A total is a bet on the number of points scored in the game by both teams combined, including overtime.
## Moneyline example (not a point-spread definition)
Source: https://support.nfl.com/hc/en-us/articles/35869774194324-What-is-the-point-spread
In a moneyline wager, the bettor focuses just on who will be the winner of the match.
If the game ends in a tie, the bettor's wager is returned with no loss or profit.
