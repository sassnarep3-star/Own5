S02Ship a sign-convention regression test with every backtestThis session's first injury backtest reported a 57.4% ATS edge that vanished once the spread sign convention was corrected (nflverse spread_line is the expected home margin, not a handicap). A single sign error is enough to invent an edge.Add a unit test that asserts the ATS sign on a synthetic fixture, plus a known-answer test on 10 historical games with published results, and fail the build if either drifts.HighLowHighRecommended nowQuantIf a future data feed documents a different convention, update the fixture rather than the assertion, and record the change.

S04Publish a market-efficiency scorecard per signalWe can measure whether a signal predicts results, but the useful question is whether the market has already priced it. Injury deficiency moves the line (-0.021 pts per pp) but its ATS residual edge is 51.4% with p=1.0.For each signal, report (a) regression of the market line on the signal, (b) regression of the ATS residual on the signal, (c) the implied 'priced share'. Rank ideas by residual edge, not by raw predictive power.HighMediumHighRecommended nowQuantIf a signal shows residual edge with a verified closing price, promote it to a full gate evaluation.

S05Use lineup deficiency as a pricing input for our own lines, not as a bet triggerThe measured relationship is real for predicting margin (about -0.039 points per percentage point of relative deficiency, p=6e-5), but the market already moves about half of it and the residual ATS record is 51.4% (p=1.0).Feed deficiency into the line model (already implemented) and stop advertising it as a betting signal. Show the model line both with and without the adjustment so the size of the adjustment is auditable.MediumLowHighRecommended nowModel ownerIf a future season shows a residual edge with verified prices, revisit.

S06Extend depth tracking to offensive-line continuityOffensive line injuries are the classic source of hidden lineup decay: five players who never practised together still count as 'starters available'. Snap share alone cannot see that.Build a weekly starting-five continuity metric (same five linemen starting together, consecutive weeks) from snap counts and measure its relationship to sacks allowed, pressure rate and ATS residual.MediumMediumMediumPilotResearchIf continuity shows no relationship to pressure or margin after a season of data, drop it.

S07Treat practice-status transitions as a probability, not a labelThe official report gives three practice levels and a game status. Collapsing them to fixed weights (A1/A2) throws away the strongest information: a player moving DNP -> LP -> FP across the week.Fit the mapping empirically: P(active) by (practice sequence, game status) from 2023-2026 reports, and publish the fitted table with sample sizes.MediumMediumMediumPilotQuantIf the fitted probabilities differ from the assumed weights by less than 5 percentage points, keep the simple weights.

S11Price correlated legs with an empirical copula, and refuse independent pricingSame-team QB + WR1 legs are correlated (r = 0.50 on 2024-2026); at median lines the joint hit rate is 32.5% versus 24.8% if treated as independent, a +7.7 pp gap that changes the fair price materially.Require an empirical joint estimate for any same-game combination; publish the implied fair price next to the offered price and reject the bet when the offered price assumes independence.HighMediumHighRecommended nowQuantIf the operator's SGP price already reflects the correlation (compare implied joint to empirical), no action is needed beyond documentation.
