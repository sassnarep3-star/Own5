Executive summary

What this desk found, what it could not find, and what it would take to change the answer. Every number below is computed by the build; none is transcribed from elsewhere.

1. The headline

We originated 7,058 NFL markets and could not beat the closing line. The pre-registered rules were frozen before evaluation, graded on a holdout the fitting never saw, and 14 of the 14 families cleared the gates. That is the designed outcome for a first honest pass, and it is stated here rather than buried.

The measured size of the gap: our side error is 10.71 points against the market's 10.27; our total error 10.93 against 10.63. On the holdout, residual alpha is +0.0019 (CI -0.047 to +0.051, n=1,615).

2. What was built and what it proves

  • A pinned corpus. 7,116 graded regular-season games, 1999-2026, from a single commit of the nflverse repository, fetched as one tarball whose SHA-256 is re-proved on every build. 28 seasons, 46,812 closing-line observations, and every result recomputed from the scores rather than trusted. Corpus
  • Origination in the required order. Side, then total, then moneyline, walk-forward, with the fit cutoff recorded on every row so a reader can prove no future game entered a forecast. Originated lines
  • Three columns, always. Model fair line, market line and the difference, side by side. Originated lines
  • 14 pre-registered strategies with frozen rule text, three windows (train, validation, holdout), eight gates, and a two-sided "what would change our mind" field. Strategies
  • Signal and staking kept apart. The signal board ranks by Brier skill with a cluster-bootstrap interval; the staking board is empty because no strategy is certified. A lucky staking run has nowhere to appear. Leaderboard
  • The removal-of-luck panel for all 32 clubs: expected versus actual wins, one-score record, and the power calculation that says how many bets would be needed to prove a real edge. Removal of luck
  • Availability as a first-class dataset. Depth from official club charts only, with shared slots stored as groups, jersey numbers stripped and the raw cell kept, and the replacement's depth rank published rather than the bare fact of a replacement. Depth
  • An append-only capture ledger with SHA-256 per capture, a clock time per read, a schema contract per parser, a freshness alarm, and point-in-time replay. Snapshots
  • 80 requirement rows, 18 questions and 16 reviewable suggestions. Nothing in the suggestions register has been implemented without being named here. Questions · Suggestions

3. The three findings worth acting on

  1. The market is the benchmark, and it wins. Not one of the nine non-control families shows positive Brier skill against the de-vigged close on the holdout. The best of them is S10 at -0.0921. Any future strategy should be judged on this axis first, because it is the axis that cannot be gamed by staking patterns.
  2. The two price series are not interchangeable. They agree exactly on 2,011 of 2,969 joinable games and flip the favourite on 17 of them. Publishing an ROI without naming the series would be publishing an unreproducible number. Price policy
  3. Availability is the unexplored edge, and it is blocked. If anything on this site is likely to beat a closing line, it is information about who is actually playing. Every route to that information that this environment can reach is exhausted: the inactives URL 404s, no game book has been read, and the public snap-count files sit behind a host the sandbox cannot reach. That is the single highest-value unblock. Questions

5. What this desk does not claim

  • It does not claim an edge. The measured alpha is indistinguishable from zero on the holdout.
  • It does not claim the club depth charts are the game-day lineup. Clubs label them "unofficial".
  • It does not claim a replacement played. It claims the club named him at that rank on that day.
  • It does not claim 2026 is a holdout. 2026 has no graded bets in this desk.
  • It does not rank anything by ROI or win rate, and the leaderboard holds zero backtest rows.
