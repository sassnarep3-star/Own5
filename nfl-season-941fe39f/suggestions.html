Where to start, if you only read three

  1. S-02 — an opening-line history. The largest single constraint on the project. Without it, a third of the brief cannot be answered at all.
  2. S-05 — a second clock time for every availability unit. Cheap, and it turns four "cannot replay" rows into replayable ones.
  3. S-01 — one index for the eleven desks. A reader cannot currently tell which desk is current, and four desks publish different counts for the same requirement.
