Price policy

The brief asks for the price-data policy to be decided before any backtest is published. Here it is, in three rules, each with the measurement that forced it.

The policy

  1. One series, named in every table. The backtest uses games.spread_line_home and games.total_line from the pinned corpus and nothing else. The separate closing_lines.csv file is used only for the comparison below.
  2. A price is a snapshot with a clock time, or it does not exist. A row without a clock time is not a price. This is why no opening price is published: there is no opening snapshot, only a closing one.
  3. Two-sided or dropped. A bet is graded only where both sides of the market carry a price at the same snapshot, so the implied probability can be de-vigged from the pair rather than assumed.

Consequence, stated plainly: closing-line value cannot be signed anywhere in this project, because CLV needs an open and a close from the same book. Gate G3 is recorded as failed rather than skipped and no ROI claims a beat-the-close property.

What would change the policy

A source that publishes an open and a close from the same book, with a timestamp on each. With that, rule 2 becomes satisfiable and G3 becomes measurable. Until then the policy is not a preference; it is the only defensible reading of the data that exists.
