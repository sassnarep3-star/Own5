How the pairing is chosen

Each position group has a declared set of opponents it lines up against: receivers face corners and safeties, tackles face edge rushers and interior linemen, running backs face linebackers and interior linemen. That mapping is a modelling choice and is published in tools/season_desk_941fe39f/availability.py.

A pairing appears only when at least one side has a named replacement. If the starter is out and the club named nobody at rank 2, the matrix stays silent, because the alternative is to invent a replacement.

A group at rank 1 (two players in one cell) is treated as two available starters, so a team with a shared slot is not marked deficient just because its chart lists two names.

What would make this decisive

Three things, in order of value: an official inactive list (so "out" becomes "did not dress"), official snap counts (so "backup" becomes "played 12% of snaps"), and charts for the other 27 clubs. The first two are blocked by source availability; the third is a capture-cadence problem that this desk's ledger is already built to absorb.
