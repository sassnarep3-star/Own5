The three that did not reproduce

  • Baltimore publishing Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley at quarterback. The 2026-11-17 chart lists Jacoby Brissett, Joe Fagnano and Kedon Slovis, and Brissett's arrival is dated 11/06 in the league's transaction log. F-06.
  • Washington leaving a depth slot empty. No offence or defence row on the 2026-11-17 page has an empty rank-1 cell. F-07.
  • The Detroit designations being blank. All three read as Out. Published as reproduced, not as a failure.
