A positive difference on the side means we rate the home team more strongly than the market does. It is a disagreement, not an edge: the holdout says these disagreements carry no demonstrated information.

The chain, enforced in code

Each market is produced in a fixed order and the order is recorded, so a reader can check that the moneyline was derived from the side rather than priced independently:

  1. Side. margin = HFA + rating(home) - rating(away), a weighted ridge fit on games strictly before the target week's first kickoff.
  2. Total. Two scoring-rate equations per game, home and away, fitted together.
  3. Moneyline. P(home win) = Phi(margin / sigma), where sigma is the probit maximum-likelihood fit of market spread to result on the same fit window.

Nothing is priced from the moneyline backwards, and no market is priced before the one above it.

Alpha r is the correlation between (our line - market line) and (result - market line). A positive value with an interval above zero would be the first evidence of a real edge; no season shows that cleanly.

Residual, not level

The brief asks for the residual against the market to be published on a schedule. The residual is the difference column on every row, and its distribution is on the Model page. A useful property of publishing the residual rather than only the pick: a residual of zero is a legitimate and common outcome, and it means we agreed with the market.
