Reading them

Three of the seven have intervals that exclude zero, and each says something usable:

  • Wind and total: r = -0.095, n = 5,026. More wind, fewer points. The interval is -0.122 to -0.068. It is small, and it is already in the closing total, which is why it is not a strategy on its own. It is the one fixture fact that shows a real marginal signal.
  • Rest difference and margin: r = 0.034, n = 7,116. Real, tiny, and priced.
  • Closing spread and margin: r = 0.432, n = 7,116. The market's own accuracy, published as the benchmark a model would have to beat.

The two that do not exclude zero are worth naming for what they rule out: temperature has no detectable marginal relationship with the total in this sample, and the closing spread is essentially uncorrelated with the closing total (r = -0.011), which is why the side and total models can be fitted separately.

What is withheld, and why

No correlation on this page is computed from a source that is not the pinned corpus or an official capture. Where a value would need a feed this environment cannot reach — same-game prop correlation, for instance — nothing is published: there is no number, only a question (Q-12).
