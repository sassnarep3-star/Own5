The plain answer to the question this project asks

Can a model we build beat the NFL closing line? On this evidence: no, not yet, and the desk can say by how much.

Across the 1,615 games of the 2020-2025 holdout, the correlation between our disagreement with the closing spread and the result relative to that spread is +0.0019 (95% CI -0.047 to +0.051). The interval straddles zero, so the honest reading is that the residual carries no demonstrated information on the holdout. The full 1999-2025 sample gives +0.0245 (CI +0.001 to +0.048), which is small enough that any publication of it as an edge would be overclaiming.

Our mean absolute error on the side is 10.71 points against the market's 10.27, and on the total 10.93 against 10.63. The market is better than us at both. That is the finding, and it is what the gates were built to detect.

Source for every number above: the pinned corpus in Corpus. The arithmetic is in tools/season_desk_941fe39f/model.py and re-runs in about 25 seconds with no network.

What is blocking the most value, in order

  1. No opening price. Every price in the corpus is a single snapshot, so closing-line value cannot be signed and no move-based strategy can be graded. 19,344 markets carry a close and 0 carry an open. See Price policy.
  2. No official inactive list and no game book. The league's inactive URL returns HTTP 404 from this environment, and no game book has been read. A replacement can be named from a club chart but never confirmed as the player who took the snaps. See Availability.
  3. No official snap counts. Depth is therefore club-published order, which is better than nothing and worse than playing time. See Depth.
  4. One club publishes its chart only as an image. Houston contributes zero depth rows and is flagged rather than transcribed. See Flagged.
  5. No player-level game statistics. Player props and same-game parlays cannot be priced at all, so their joint-evidence gate cannot be tested. See Corpus.

Verification you can re-run

Four independent checks are run on every build and none may fail silently:

  • Results. The 145 official 2026 finals this repository already held match the pinned corpus game for game: 145 of 145.
  • Standings. The corpus re-derives each club's 2026 record and points; every difference is explained, game by game, by a game that finished between the two captures (24 exact, 8 reconciled).
  • Price. The corpus carries two price series. They agree on 2,011 of 2,969 joinable games, which is why the desk uses one series only and says so on every table.
  • Schedule. All 7 checkable claims in the league's dated schedule release match the live schedule facts.
