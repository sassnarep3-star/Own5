Leaderboard

Two boards, kept apart on purpose. The signal board ranks by a proper score with an interval. The staking board is empty, and stays empty until a strategy is certified, so a lucky staking run cannot stand in for a bad signal.

Board 2 — staking

Empty. By rule. The staking board admits only strategies that hold a certification, and 0 do. The rule is written on Gates and the reason is the brief's own: a lucky staking run must never be able to mask a bad signal.

What would fill it: one family clearing all nine gates on the holdout. The closest is S10, blocked by G3_CLV;G3B_ALPHA;G4_BSS;G5_CALIBRATION;G6_MULTIPLICITY;G8_DECAY.

Each control has no model, so its Brier skill against the market is exactly zero by construction and its ROI is the vig. That is the point: it is the number a real strategy has to clear, and none does.

Demotion gate

A strategy that is live is sent back to paper when any of six triggers fires. None can fire today because nothing is live, but the thresholds are frozen now so that they cannot be chosen after the fact:

triggercondition
D1rolling 100-bet Brier skill against the close below 0
D2calibration slope outside [0.8, 1.25] on the live sample
D3mean residual alpha below 0 over the live sample with an interval excluding 0
D4price source changes book, or the capture cadence breaks for 7 days
D5any gate that was cleared now fails on a fresh validation window
D6the rule text is edited without a new pre-registration commit
