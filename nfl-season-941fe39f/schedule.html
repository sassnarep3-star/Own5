Why this pairing matters

A live schedule page is a moving target: games get flexed, kickoff times move, and a venue can be reassigned. The dated announcement is the fixed record of what was promised. Publishing them side by side means a reader can see whether a fixture was moved rather than having to infer it from a missing row.

The desk checks the seven claims on every build. If the league changes a fixture, the check fails and the build stops, which is the drift alarm working as intended.
