Model

One model, frozen. Two estimators for the total, published side by side. Four constants that are choices rather than league values, published with their values. An ablation section that says plainly what cannot yet be ablated.

Model card

Target: sidemargin = HFA + rating(home) - rating(away), weighted least squares, weights 0.5^(days/365), ridge 2.0 on ratings, 1.0 on home field
Target: totaltwo equations per game, home = off(home) + def(away) + hfa and away = off(away) + def(home), ridge 1.0 on every rate
Target: moneylinePhi(margin / sigma), sigma by probit maximum likelihood on the same window; the sigma for every market is a column on its originated row (11.52 points on the full 2026 week-11 fit)
Identifiabilitythe ridge is required: early in a season every club has one home and one away game, so home field cannot be separated from strength without it
Fit windowevery regular-season game strictly before the target week's first kickoff. The number of games in each fit and the cutoff date are columns on every originated row.
What is not in itinjuries, depth, weather beyond what is in the corpus's own columns, travel, rest beyond the corpus field. The ablation section explains why.

A model that is unbiased but noisier than the market cannot beat it: the market's error is the floor you have to beat, not the average you have to match. This desk measures the gap rather than assuming it away.

Two totals, both published

The brief asks that every model total be published twice until one wins. Two estimators exist in the code: A, the scoring-rate regression above, and B, a simple league-mean-plus-team-total-offset estimator. Both are computed; the table compares them on the holdout on the same games.

The walk-forward comparison on the holdout is on the Corpus page with its MAE. Neither is collapsed to one yet, which is the state the brief asks for.

Ablations: injuries and personnel

The brief asks for ablations rather than fixed penalties: run the identical model with and without the availability features and report the difference with uncertainty.

This desk cannot yet run that ablation, and says so. The availability features cover 4 clubs and 12 players in this capture. An ablation on 12 rows is not an ablation; it is a coincidence with a confidence interval. The design is registered on Strategies and the blocking question is Q-17 on Questions.

What is published is the fixture-context ablation: the corpus's own published schedule facts (rest, roof, surface, wind, temperature, division, neutral site) are available for every game, and their marginal correlation with the result is on Correlations. Wind is the one with an interval that excludes zero.
