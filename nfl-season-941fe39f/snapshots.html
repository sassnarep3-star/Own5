A unit with one clock time cannot answer 'what was known at kickoff'. This table is the honest status of that requirement: injuries and depth have two or more clock times; transactions, the reserve list and the schedule announcement have one.

The three alarms

alarmfires whenconsequence
Freshnessa unit's newest capture is older than its limit (24 h for the injury report, 168 h for depth, transactions and reserve list)the row is marked STALE on this page and reported in the build output
Schema drifta required field disappears from a capturethe build fails before any page is written; a blank is never published in place of a field
Corpus driftthe tarball's SHA-256 no longer matches the pinthe build stops and says to record a new pin, because a new commit means a new corpus

All three are exercised by the unit tests, and the schema alarm is proved by a test that removes a field and asserts the build refuses.

Storage

The captures live in the repository under nfl-season-941fe39f/data/official/ and the derived tables under data/derived/. The public site publishes only the derived layer; the raw capture rows stay in the repository, which is the public/private split the brief asks for. The pinned corpus tarball is also committed, because a hash is only useful if the bytes behind it can be checked.

The capture cadence is manual in this session. Making it a scheduled job is suggestion S-05 and S-15; it is not implemented, and the freshness table says so rather than implying a cron.
