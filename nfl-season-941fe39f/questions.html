The five that matter most

  1. Q-01 — is there an opening price anywhere? Without one, CLV does not exist and no move-based strategy can be graded.
  2. Q-02 — which price series is the closing market? The two series disagree on more than a fifth of joinable games.
  3. Q-04 — who actually started? The club chart is an expectation; the game book and the inactive list are the record.
  4. Q-06 — how deep is the replacement? Depth rank is not playing time; snaps are.
  5. Q-17 — can the model be improved enough to beat the close? Only if a feature arrives that the close does not already contain, and the only candidate is availability.
