Executive Summary

Full text also in the repo: EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md

Objective

Build the research foundation, validation machinery, and competition structure for originating NBA betting lines — pricing markets independently and betting only when our price beats the available price — across singles (sides/totals/moneyline), player props, and parlays / correlated prop combos (SGP).

What was produced

  1. Strategy catalog — 8 candidate signal families: rest/schedule spots, official-injury-report latency, home/travel context, streak persistence, pace-interaction props, usage-vacuum props, CLV-based origination, correlated-SGP construction. None is claimed profitable; all are candidates pending validation.
  2. Nine validation gates (G0–G8): pre-registration, parameter budget, walk-forward out-of-sample, de-vigged CLV with bootstrap CI, deflated-Sharpe/PBO multiplicity control, Monte-Carlo permutation nulls, economic realism, parlay correlation audit, fractional-Kelly staking.
  3. Correlation framework: a 10-pair direction taxonomy, a ban on negatively correlated legs, and tooling (analysis/prop_correlations.py) to compute coefficients from official box scores. Numeric r values are intentionally blank — never inferred.
  4. Luck-removal methodology: grade on de-vigged closing-line value (signal in ~200–500 bets) instead of W/L (needs thousands); empirical-Bayes shrinkage; permutation nulls; mandatory falsification criteria.
  5. Leaderboard competition: divisions per bet class; score = 70% shrunken CLV + 20% flat-stake yield + 10% process compliance; Gold/Silver/Bronze tiers; anti-gaming rules. Board currently empty by design.
  6. This dashboard site, with a dedicated Flagged for Review page.

Key verified findings

  • Back-to-backs are the most consistent schedule depressant: 44% win rate vs ~50% null across 2019/20–2024/25 (arXiv:2606.27957); winning likelihood rises significantly from B2B to one rest day (Esteves et al. 2021).
  • Official injury-report timing creates a defined reaction window: statuses due 5 p.m. local the day before; 1 p.m. game-day for back-to-back second games; game-day reports 11 a.m.–1 p.m. (official.nba.com).
  • Short-horizon persistence exists: P(win | previous win) = 54.5% vs 50% null (arXiv:2606.27957) — an edge only if the closing line misses it.
  • Home advantage is real but shrinking: ~64% (1980s) → ~57% (2010s) across major sports (Pollard & Gómez 2021, as cited); recent NBA-specific percentages are third-party and flagged, not asserted.
  • CLV is the accepted skill metric: de-vig first, benchmark a sharp close, expect signal in hundreds of bets (industry convention, flagged as such).

Honest limitations

  • No numeric prop correlations yet — blank until computed from official NBA box scores (build sandbox had no network route to stats.nba.com).
  • No performance claims — no bets placed; leaderboard empty until strategies pass gates.
  • Industry thresholds (e.g., "+1–2% CLV is strong") are conventions, listed on Flagged for Review.
  • Pre-existing irregularity: GitHub Pages was enabled on this repo before any content existed (see COORDINATION.md / R1).
