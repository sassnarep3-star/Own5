Core principle: strategies are graded on de-vigged closing-line value, not wins and losses — the accepted way to measure skill and remove luck from the evaluation.
Transparency: values that could not be confirmed from an official or primary source are left blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page. The leaderboard is empty because no strategy has passed the gates; nothing here claims betting profits.
Sibling project: this repository also hosts an NFL Research Desk built by a parallel session (see COORDINATION.md); this NBA lab is independent of it.
