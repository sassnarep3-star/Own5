Bet Types — Singles, Parlays, Player-Prop Combos

1 · Single bets (sides / totals / moneyline)

The cleanest laboratory: one market, one price, direct CLV measurement. Applicable strategies: S1 rest spots, S2 injury latency, S3 home/travel, S4 streaks, S7 origination.

2 · Parlays (cross-game)

Legs in different games are genuinely independent — joint probability is the product of the legs, and the book compounds its vig on every leg. Five 50% legs ⇒ 3.125% (0.5⁵). A cross-game parlay is only +EV when each leg is individually +EV; parlays never create edge, they only compound it (and compound variance). (nba-prop-bets INDUSTRY)

3 · Same-game parlays & player-prop combos

Within one game, legs are correlated — positively or negatively — and books apply an imperfect correlation adjustment. The only durable SGP edge is modeling the joint distribution better than the book (strategy S8, gate G7).
