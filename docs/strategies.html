Design rules common to all strategies

  • Evaluate against the closing line, never raw outcomes — a real basketball effect the market already prices is worth nothing (see Skill vs Luck).
  • Inputs must come from official sources at bet time (e.g., the official injury report); unconfirmable inputs ⇒ no bet.
  • Every bet is logged pre-game with timestamp, line, price, stake, and strategy version hash.
Every table row carries a source link and tier: OFFICIAL NBA league source · PRIMARY peer-reviewed / preprint / primary author · INDUSTRY betting-industry convention (listed on Flagged for Review). Unconfirmable values are left blank — never inferred. No performance claims are made anywhere on this site.
