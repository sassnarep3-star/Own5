SF RENT REVIEW/Fairness & rights
Snapshot · Nov 6, 2026
CONSISTENT ≠ AUTOMATICALLY LAWFUL

Fairness is more than matching numbers.

An increase to one unit is not automatically discriminatory, and increasing both does not guarantee protection from a complaint. Apply and document neutral criteria, and seek professional review.
