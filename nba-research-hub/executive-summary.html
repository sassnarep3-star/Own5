Executive Summary

Concise summary of what was verified, calculated, and blocked. This is an exploratory retrospective audit, not prospective preregistration or betting advice.

What is solid

  • Official NBA team snapshots for all 30 teams (2025-26 and 2026-27 through Nov 5) and 125 selected player-game rows were directly checked and linked.
  • Nine November 6 research spreads/totals/scores are reproducible from disclosed formulas; market and availability fields remain blank/held.

What is not established

  • No predictive series passes the frozen skill gate; all are under 100 observations and lack adjusted significance.

  • Cross-game parlay dependence is unmeasured; non-significant lag-1 correlations do not prove independence.
  • Repository line inventory is content hashing/linking, not independent verification of every inherited factual claim.
