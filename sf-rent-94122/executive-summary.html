Executive summary

Scope. Two units in one 1940 building in San Francisco's Outer Sunset (ZIP 94122), both reported as rent controlled. Unit 1: initial lease February 1, 2025 at $3,995/month (three occupants, anonymized); continuing tenancy April 1, 2026 at $3,995/month (two occupants). Unit 2: lease started June 1, 2019 at $3,150/month (two occupants). All tenant identities are anonymized on this site; dates and amounts are retained exactly as provided and flagged as user-provided/unverified.

Law. Because the building predates June 13, 1979, the SF Rent Ordinance applies (unit-level confirmation still required). The only ordinary in-tenancy increase is the annual allowable increase: 1.6% for effective dates 3/1/2026–2/28/2027 (60% of Bay Area CPI-U), once per 12 months on the anniversary date, calculated on base rent only, no rounding up. S16 ↗ Anything beyond the annual amount requires a Rent Board petition/passthrough. S17 ↗ State AB 1482 (5% + CPI, max 10%; 8.8% in the SF region for 8/1/2026–7/31/2027) does not apply because the local ordinance is stricter; Costa-Hawkins guarantees vacancy decontrol only. Notice. Written notice ≥30 days before the effective date (90 days if the increase exceeds 10% alone or combined in the same year; +5 days if mailed), stating dollar amount, percentage, effective date, and any banked portion, using the rate in effect on the effective date; a current rent increase license (Housing Inventory) must be on file. No increase during a fixed-term lease unless the lease allows it. S01 ↗ S16 ↗ S05 ↗ S09 ↗ S15 ↗

Reasonableness. The defensible, "normal" increase for both units is the published annual allowance applied uniformly on each unit's own anniversary date — not a market-based figure (market comps for 94122 could not be verified from an official source and are left blank). Raising one unit is lawful; raising both is not required and is not a safe harbor. Uniform, documented treatment avoids disparate-treatment risk under the federal Fair Housing Act.
