Compliant rent-increase checklist (rent-controlled units)

  1. Confirm coverage of each unit (Property Information Map / Rent Board counselor). S06 ↗ S18 ↗
  2. Confirm the lawful base rent and rent ledger, including every prior increase and any banked amounts (banked only after ≥24 months without an increase; added, not compounded; not carried to new tenants). S16 ↗
  3. Confirm the anniversary date and that at least 12 months have passed since the last increase. S01 ↗
  4. Hold a current rent increase license (Housing Inventory reporting) before the increase takes effect. S05 ↗
  5. Use the rate in effect on the effective date — 1.6% for 3/1/2026–2/28/2027. S01 ↗ S02 ↗
  6. Calculate on base rent only; do not round up. S01 ↗
  7. Serve written notice ≥30 days ahead (90 days if >10% alone or combined in the same year; +5 days if mailed), stating dollar amount, percentage, effective date, and banked portion. S01 ↗ S16 ↗ S09 ↗
  8. Never raise rent mid-term on a fixed-term lease unless the lease allows it. S15 ↗
  9. Never charge for additional occupants. S01 ↗
  10. For anything beyond the annual amount, file the Rent Board petition/passthrough first. S17 ↗
  11. Keep records (ledger, lease, license, calculation, notice, delivery proof) and apply the same policy uniformly to all units; have a tenant-rights attorney review before serving. S13 ↗ S07 ↗ S08 ↗
