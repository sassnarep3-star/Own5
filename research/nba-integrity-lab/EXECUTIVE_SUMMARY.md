## Findings

- Nine **model-calculated**, unvalidated November 6 research spreads and totals use 18 rows of [NBA advanced team statistics](https://www.nba.com/stats/teams/advanced?Season=2026-27&SeasonType=Regular%20Season) and the [NBA 4:00 p.m. ET injury report](https://ak-static.cms.nba.com/referee/injury/Injury-Report_2026-11-05_04_00PM.pdf). They are not official NBA lines or bookmaker quotes. The model has no injury adjustment, calibrated probability or market comparison.
- The injury snapshot marks 17 of 18 team entries **NOT YET SUBMITTED**. New Orleans lists players, but this is not a completeness or present-tense availability determination. All nine games have at least one unsubmitted team report. The NBA says reports update throughout the day; consult the [current report index](https://official.nba.com/nba-injury-report-2026-27-season/).
- We separately reviewed the displayed date, matchup, game ID, result, minutes, points, made threes, rebounds and assists for 25 selected 2025–26 Anthony Edwards appearances against the [NBA player log](https://www.nba.com/stats/player/1630162/boxscores?Season=2025-26). In this selected sample, Pearson points × made threes is **0.555**. Points ≥25 and made threes ≥3 occurred together 12/25 times (**48.0%**) versus **40.8%** from the sample marginals (phi **0.315**). These are our calculations, not NBA-published correlations; they are exploratory, conditional on playing and not generalizable on their own.
- In a hypothetical independent, no-push, constant −110 sequence, the break-even probability is 110/210 and the exact one-sided probability of 30 or more wins in 50 at that null is about **0.1745**. A 2,243-bet example with true win probability 55%, one test and one-sided α=0.05 has approximately **79.2% exact binomial power**. The [NIST binomial reference](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda366i.htm) describes the distribution; actual betting data can violate these assumptions. No calculation can eliminate luck.
- All seven candidate strategies are **blocked**: no verified executable sportsbook prices, matched settlements, preregistered prospective holdout, dependence validation, independently replicated skill or funded prize pool. No rank, ROI, p-value or cash amount is asserted. Badge tiers and a log-score comparison are proposals, not awarded prizes or an operational payout service. [Operator definition of same-game parlay](https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/same-game-parlay).
- A static HTML link-presence audit of the [session-start GitHub tree](https://github.com/sassnarep3-star/Own5/tree/54e8dee596d49731c3da535a1f77b01cd0c295d5) covers **139 pages and 2,852 data rows**. **369 rows** lack a link to the conservative allowlist, including NBA and NFL pages. This is a location list for review, not proof that the remaining rows are correct or that every legitimate source was included. Markdown, CSV/JSON, dynamically rendered tables and most historical claims were not independently verified.

It calls Edwards a top-two scorer, although the [NBA leaders table](https://www.nba.com/stats/leaders?Season=2025-26) ranks him third. See the [older summary](https://github.com/sassnarep3-star/Own5/blob/54e8dee596d49731c3da535a1f77b01cd0c295d5/research/nba-signal-lab/EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md#L50-L70), [older power calculation](https://github.com/sassnarep3-star/Own5/blob/54e8dee596d49731c3da535a1f77b01cd0c295d5/nba-signal-lab/compute.py#L350-L395), [older line calculation](https://github.com/sassnarep3-star/Own5/blob/54e8dee596d49731c3da535a1f77b01cd0c295d5/nba-signal-lab/compute.py#L239-L251), the [operator’s spread-sign explanation](https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/page/nba-finals-odds), and this desk’s review page.

## Operational limitations

The published site is a frozen snapshot and does not automatically refresh NBA inputs or acquire bookmaker data. Current odds, current availability, future settlements, prospective registration and reward funding remain unverified. The 20-game shrinkage and zero home-court parameters are project choices made after seeing the data, not official NBA values or a trained model. User-facing browsing requires no manual data entry; future automatic ingestion and an actual competition service are not implemented.
