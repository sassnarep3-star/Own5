## Reward tiers

| Tier | Rank rule | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | Top 3 strategies by risk-adjusted return (Sharpe-style: ROI / stdev of bet outcomes) after gates | Featured placement + largest research allocation |
| Tier 2 | Next 4 validated strategies | Standard research allocation |
| Tier 3 | Remaining validated strategies | Honorable mention |

Ranking metrics (graded bets, win rate, ROI, risk-adjusted return, max drawdown, CLV) are computed only from graded bets that passed the gates; CLV remains flagged (F01) because no official source publishes closing lines.
