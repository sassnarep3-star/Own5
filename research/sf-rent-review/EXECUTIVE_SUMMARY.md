# Executive summary — SF rent review

**Evidence snapshot:** November 6, 2026. The [dashboard](../../sf-rent-review/index.html) contains the source-linked tables and a [review register](../../sf-rent-review/review.html). This is information, not an individualized legal determination or service-ready notice.

## Decision in brief

For a tenancy actually subject to San Francisco’s rent-increase limits, the [Rent Board’s annual allowance](https://www.sf.gov/news--annual-rent-increase-3126-22827-announced) is **1.6% of lawful base rent** for an ordinary annual increase effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027. The amount is an allowance, not a requirement or an observed “normal” rate. Recheck the [rate schedule](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) for later effective dates.

A considerate and easy-to-explain starting policy is **no increase or an ordinary-only increase up to the applicable annual allowance**, if each unit independently qualifies. A lower increase is allowed; a properly [banked increase](https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases) or other separately authorized amount may be possible, but no such amount is established here. The recommended approach is not a finding that a higher legally authorized amount is impermissible, nor a guarantee against a claim.

## Before action

Verify coverage, current lawful base rent, prior notices and anniversary, lease terms, any applicable subsidy, and a current [Housing Inventory license](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory). Review potential fair-housing, retaliation and habitability issues with qualified counsel. Do not assume a banked balance, legal clearance, or an official verification of owner-reported facts. Public tables intentionally leave the dates, amounts and other property-specific values blank. No notice was issued. The [Rent Board](https://www.sf.gov/speak-rent-board-counselor) provides free information, not legal advice; [tenant resources](https://www.sf.gov/tenant-and-landlord-resources) provide independent help.
