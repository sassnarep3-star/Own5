## Research program

Six proposed candidates cover spread singles, totals, player points, cross-game parlays, same-game parlays and player-stat combos. Originate probabilities from past-only outcome distributions; price joint bets from joint probabilities rather than multiplying marginal probabilities by assumption. Implemented and tested pricing arithmetic, feasible two-event correlation conversion, median thresholds, Holm test correction and fail-closed gate aggregation. This is not a trained forecasting model.

Require official provenance, time integrity, operator settlement rules, preregistration, chronological holdout, calibration, multiple-testing correction, positive uncertainty-adjusted net edge, dependence robustness and independent replication. Aggregate evidence flags alone do not establish scientific validity.
