## 2. What is genuinely good

* The evidence culture is real. Several desks refuse to publish a number they could not source,
  and they publish the refusal. That is the right instinct and it is why this desk could build on
  them rather than re-do them.
* The 136-game 2026 log (`nfl-b34253a1/data/games_2026_w1_w9.csv`) carries the official game URL
  and the week URL per row. It is the single most valuable artefact in the repository, and it
  held up: 136/136 rows reconcile through the standings test once Week 10 is added.
* The Week 10 injury capture of 2026-11-12 (`data/decision-desk/official/`) is a genuine
  point-in-time snapshot with a per-row timestamp. It is the seed of the availability history the
  project needs — it just needs to be one of dozens, not the only one.
* `verify.yml` pins the root site to a deterministic rebuild, which is why no session can quietly
  hand-edit the landing page.

## 3. What is wrong, in order of cost

### 3.1 Thirty-two desks answering one question

The same requirement (a verification gate, a leaderboard, a depth view) is answered differently
in several folders. A reader cannot tell which is current. Nothing is *wrong*; the accumulation
is. Concretely: `nfl-market-desk` holds 99 files, `nfl-c98c6270` 75, `nfl-desk-35a1feb2` 73, and
the decision-readiness idea appears in at least six of them.

### 3.2 Two workflows are red on `main` and on every PR

| Workflow | Failing step | Logged by |
|---|---|---|
| `NFL Decision Desk — fetch & verify` | "Confirm the committed site matches a clean rebuild" | 560ed315, a991e3fb, 35a1feb2, 98a211b8, 3cb7800c |
| `Verify NFL B34 Week 10 research desk` | "Rebuild, audit, and test isolated NFL desk" | 35a1feb2, 98a211b8, 3cb7800c |

Both are pre-existing and outside this session's scope. The real cost is that red checks have
become normal, which is how a genuine failure gets merged.

### 3.3 The landing page is stamped 2026-11-02

`index.html` says "Reviewed 2026-11-02" and links one sibling desk. It is generated by
`scripts/build.py` and diff-checked, so it cannot be hand-edited (FLAG-S2). Sixteen days of work
is invisible from the front door.

### 3.4 Numbers that cannot be checked are still shown in places

Several older desks publish leaderboard-style rows with historical metrics. The root page
quarantines them in prose, but the numbers still render. This desk publishes metrics only where
the source and the sample are on the same page, and publishes `0 certified` explicitly.

### 3.5 No price data, anywhere

After 32 desks, the repository still has no official opening or closing price for any market.
That means ROI, CLV, staking and vig-free consensus are all unbuildable, and it is the single
biggest blocker in the requirements ledger (5 rows, FLAG-P1).

## 5. What this desk did about it

* Built one additive folder that answers the brief end to end, with a fail-closed verifier.
* Adopted the 136-game log **only after** proving it reconciles, and said so in the corpus page.
* Re-read the official pages rather than trusting the desks' own numbers; where they differ, the
  difference is published (see the 17 stale injury rows).
* Logged the two red workflows and the stale root page as cross-session requests instead of
  editing files owned by other sessions.
* Left the decision on 32 desks to the reviewer, with a numbered suggestion (S02, S12).
