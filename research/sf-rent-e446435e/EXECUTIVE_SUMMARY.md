## Answers to each question

1. **Reasonable/normal under local/state/federal law?** Local: 1.6% annual (+ properly banked
   only); state AB 1482 strictly looser and inapplicable; federal: no cap, anti-discrimination
   only. ZIP 94122 HUD FY2027 SAFMR benchmarks (1BR $2,770; 2BR $3,410; 3BR $4,460; 4BR $5,020;
   S-24) are market context — bedroom counts are unknown (R44-F07) and benchmarks never justify
   an increase above the Rent Board ceiling.
2. **Corporations vs. individuals?** Institutions disclose standardized renewal programs in SEC
   10-Ks (S-26, S-27), are carved out of AB 1482's single-family exemption (S-15), and face
   Sherman Act §1 plus SF §37.10C bans on algorithmic pricing (S-25, S-13). Individual SF owners
   commonly bank CPI years and catch up later — but any "typical %" claim is private behavior
   with no official source, so it is blank + flagged (R44-F11).
3. **Correct notice + reasons?** Written, itemized ($ and % for annual vs. banked), served by
   personal delivery or first-class mail **+5 days in CA**; 30/35 days (≤10%) or 90/95 days
   (>10%); license active; 12-month spacing; fixed-term clause honored; email alone invalid
   (S-03, S-14, S-07). Lawful reasons: CPI annual, banked catch-up, Board-granted petitions.
   Never: retaliation, discrimination, coercion, or "market rent" alone (S-17, S-18, S-20).
4. **Tenant-lawyer scrutiny?** Assume full review: free counsel (Right to Counsel/EDC
   415-659-9184; Rent Board counseling 415-252-4600; S-28, S-29), tenant petitions, and the
   null-and-void penalty. The review-passing pattern is a written uniform building-wide formula,
   perfect service/itemization, and contemporaneous records (R44-F12, F15).
5. **One unit or both?** Lawful-in-principle to raise one unit where objective timing locks
   differ (here: Unit 1's agreement/bank timing vs. Unit 2's bank) — but only under a uniform
   written policy applied as each unit's lawful window opens, or selective action invites
   disparate-treatment/retaliation inquiries (S-18, S-20, S-17; R44-F12).
