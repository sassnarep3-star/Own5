## Q1 — Reasonable/normal increases for these units under local, state, federal law?

**Local (controls).** For covered tenancies the Rent Board publishes one annual percentage per
Mar–Feb window; for effectiveness Mar 1, 2026–Feb 28, 2027 it is **1.6% of lawful base rent**
(S-01, S-02). That is the *normal* increase: the annual CPI allowance, plus only *properly
banked* prior years (S-03(a)(2), S-05) or Board-granted petitions (R44-F19). Increases are
capped at one per 12 months (S-04), percents add without compounding, and any non-anniversary
effective date changes the anniversary with possible bank forfeiture (S-06, R44-F17).
Coverage for a 1940 ≤4-unit building follows Prop I / §37.12 regardless of owner occupancy
(S-11), subject to unit-by-unit confirmation (R44-F01).

**State (floor beneath local).** AB 1482 allows 5% + CPI to max 10% (S-15) but expressly yields
to stricter local rent control (S-32), so it never authorizes more than the 1.6%-plus-banked
SF ceiling here. State law's binding contributions are instead procedural and protective:
§827 notice/service/timing (S-14), the 180-day retaliation bar (S-17), FEHA fair-housing rules
(S-18, S-19), and Costa-Hawkins full-vacancy decontrol (S-31).

**Federal (no cap; anti-discrimination).** No general federal private-market rent cap exists
(S-22). 42 U.S.C. §3604(b) bars discriminatory rental rates, terms, and conditions (S-20,
S-21). CARES §4024(c) adds a 30-day eviction-notice rule only for federally backed/assisted
"covered dwellings" (S-22; status here unknown, R44-F14).

**Market context (never a lawful basis above the ceiling).** HUD FY2027 ZIP 94122 SAFMRs:
1BR $2,770; 2BR $3,410; 3BR $4,460; 4BR $5,020 (S-24; 0BR unconfirmed). Unit bedroom counts are
unknown, so per-unit applicability is blank (R44-F07). Both reported rents sit in the same
order of magnitude as these benchmarks; that observation supports "normal business practice"
narrative only — the lawful ceiling remains the Rent Board percentage.

## Q4 — How corporations vs. individual owners handle increases

- **Institutions (verified context).** Large multifamily owners/REITs publish standardized
  renewal programs and same-store rent metrics in SEC 10-Ks (venues verified: Essex S-26,
  AvalonBay S-27); they are excluded from AB 1482's single-family exemption where corporate/
  REIT-owned (S-15(d)(5)); and automated renewal pricing faces Sherman Act §1 exposure (DOJ
  RealPage suit, S-25) plus SF's §37.10C algorithmic-device ban with tenant-org standing since
  Oct 6, 2025 (S-13). Specific corporate "normal %" figures are portfolio data, not norms for
  this property → blank (R44-F11).
- **Individual SF owners (rules verified; norms unverified).** Small owners lawfully use the
  same annual/banked mechanics, often deferring years and catching up when taxes/insurance/
  utilities rise — but any claim about what individuals "normally" charge is private behavior
  with no official source → blank (R44-F11). The documented individual-owner failure modes are
  procedural: unlicensed increases (void), defective itemization (void), short notice, and
  >10% catch-ups without 90/95-day service (S-03, S-07, S-14).

## Q5 — Correct notice procedure and reasonable reasons

Serve **written** notice stating **annual vs. banked dollars and percents separately** (S-03(b)),
delivered by **personal delivery or first-class mail (+5 days in CA)** (S-14); **30/35 days**
for ≤10% cumulative in 12 months, **90/95 days** for >10%; active §37.15 license (S-07);
12-month spacing (S-04); fixed-term clause honored (R44-F03); e-mail/portal alone never
suffices (S-14); keep proof of service + rent ledger (R44-F13).
**Reasonable (lawful) reasons:** annual CPI allowance; banked catch-up; Board-granted petition
amounts. **Unlawful reasons:** retaliation within 180 days of protected activity (S-17);
discrimination on protected characteristics or source of income (S-18…S-20); coercion/
harassment (S-12); "market rent" or tenant behavior as standalone justifications for covered
units (S-03, S-04).

## Q6 — Surviving tenant-advocate / free-counsel scrutiny

Assume the increase will be reviewed: SF funds Tenant Right to Counsel (EDC 415-659-9184),
the Rent Board counsels at 415-252-4600, and BASF mediates at 415-782-8940 (S-28, S-29);
tenants may petition and void defective increases (S-03(b)(5), S-09). The review-passing
pattern: (1) written uniform building-wide formula applied as each unit's window opens;
(2) perfect service + itemization + license; (3) contemporaneous business-reason records;
(4) 180-day retaliation clearance; (5) counsel pre-review (R44-F12, F15). No review has
occurred — that fact is published, not hidden.

## Q7 — One unit or both?

Legally permissible to raise one unit where **objective, non-discriminatory timing locks**
differ — as here (Unit 1's agreement-type/bank timing vs. Unit 2's mature bank) — **provided**
a written uniform policy (e.g., "each unit receives the standard annual CPI, plus lawful bank
at its anniversary window") is adopted and each unit is moved strictly as its lawful window
opens (S-03, S-14; R44-F12). Without that paper trail, selective action invites
disparate-treatment (S-18, S-20), retaliation (S-17), and harassment (S-12) inquiries even when
the dollars are within the ceiling. Raising both under the same formula is the lowest-scrutiny
path; raising one is defensible only with documented locks + uniform policy + counsel review.
