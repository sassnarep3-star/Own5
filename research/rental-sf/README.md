# research/rental-sf — Rental Increase SF research

Anonymized research on lawful rent increases for two SF Outer Sunset (94122) tenancies in a
1940-built two-unit building, and the law governing them. General information, **not legal advice**.

- `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md` — one-page brief
- `ANALYSIS.md` — full findings, unit-by-unit math, Q&A (one/both units, process, reasons)
- `SOURCES.md` — official source registry with retrieval date
- `FLAGGED.md` — every irregularity/unknown (F-01…F-15)
- `VERIFICATION.md` — verification log

Anonymization: tenant/owner names from the source brief are replaced by placeholders
(Household 1 / Household 2; unit anniversaries kept). Dates and amounts are preserved.
Site rendering: `rental-sf/`.
