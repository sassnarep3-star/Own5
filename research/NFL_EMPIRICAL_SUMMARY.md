# Executive summary

**Decision:** no strategy qualifies for deployment or rewards. This is a source-backed historical research prototype, not current NFL lines or a profitable betting system. Chance cannot be removed; the design instead requires evidence that a signal survives chronological testing, uncertainty, multiple comparisons, calibration and market verification.

- **Coverage.** The [NFL Football Operations file](https://github.com/nfl-football-ops/Big-Data-Bowl/blob/9ad0b4b2ea36697e29e44e2399512cc1ce70358c/Data/games.csv) supplies 91 final results from Weeks 1–6 of 2017. Walk-forward evaluation covers 44 games in Weeks 4–6; the prior weeks supply history. All 91 local game rows were compared with the pinned official file. Two official NFL player-log pairings contribute 31 matched game rows from 2024 and 2025; three additional Kelce rows lack a matching Mahomes log and are excluded, not imputed ([source register](SOURCES.md)).
- **Single-game signals.** On the 44-game historical evaluation, the 50/50 reference has Brier **0.2500**; weekly Elo has **0.2518**, and the prior-margin model **0.2622**. Lower is better. Neither candidate beats the reference on this sample. A simple score-blend total has MAE **11.80** points versus **12.00** for the prior-league-mean baseline, too little evidence to claim an edge. The definitions and [NOAA scoring reference](https://vlab.ncep.noaa.gov/web/mdl/ndfd-verification-score-definitions) are documented in [methodology](NFL_EMPIRICAL_METHODS.md).
- **Dependence matters.** In the 2017 game sample, the sample phi for home win and game total over 44.5 is **+0.165** (91 games), with observed joint frequency **28.6%** versus **24.5%** from multiplying sample marginals. This threshold is illustrative, not a verified market line. The estimated relationship is unconditional and too small/old a sample for a future parlay price ([NFL data](https://github.com/nfl-football-ops/Big-Data-Bowl/blob/9ad0b4b2ea36697e29e44e2399512cc1ce70358c/Data/games.csv); [NIST correlation definition](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/correlat.htm)).
- **Player combos.** In 17 matching 2024 games, [Burrow](https://www.nfl.com/players/joe-burrow/stats/logs/2024/) passing yards and [Chase](https://www.nfl.com/players/ja-marr-chase/stats/logs/2024/) receiving yards have sample Pearson r **+0.752**, but the two illustrative over-threshold indicators have phi **−0.236**. In 14 matching 2025 games, [Mahomes](https://www.nfl.com/players/patrick-mahomes/stats/logs/2025/) and [Kelce](https://www.nfl.com/players/travis-kelce/stats/logs/2025/) yield r **+0.341**, and illustrative-over phi **+0.091**. These are descriptive, not causal or prospective; their small samples and selected thresholds are unsuitable for pricing.
- **Competition.** Research-credit rewards of 100/60/30 for the top three are a proposed non-cash policy, awarded only after every gate passes. No cash payout is implemented. Historical descriptive rank does not confer eligibility; no credits have been awarded.
- **Review flags.** The historical sample is narrow; pregame quotes, official player-prop market thresholds, settlement terms, injuries and forward-locked evidence are absent; no ROI or live edge can be verified. The upstream game repository did not expose a license in API metadata; redistribution permission is unverified. The website's Flagged for Review page lists these without blocking publication.

The scores and coefficients above are calculated by this project from the linked official inputs; they are not official NFL predictions or sportsbook offers. See [full methodology](NFL_EMPIRICAL_METHODS.md) for formulas, assumptions, validation policy and reproducibility.
