## Irregularities and limits

- The Week 12 injury URL response is a week mismatch; the reason for that mismatch is not established.
- The official Game Book has a reuse notice. Permission was not sought or assumed.
- The Texans chart is image-only in the retrieved page; no human/visual transcription was attempted.
- The Packers chart normalization issue is identified from a current official club page; no player depth rank is inferred.
- The NFL team directory establishes that a directory route exists for all 32 clubs. It does not guarantee a machine-readable depth chart or stable source schema for each club.
- The FanDuel page is an operator example and does not establish the user's jurisdiction, local access, price feed or rules for another operator.
- The attempted defensive-snap URL returned 404; no conclusion is drawn about other NFL snap sources.
- The page retriever provided a calendar date but no exact UTC retrieval time. This report records no fabricated time or raw-source hash.
