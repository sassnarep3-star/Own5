Executive summary

  • The evidence is reconciled, but no edge is certified. Fifteen official-source integrity checks pass across 30 teams, 125 selected player-games, two scheduled games and 12 archived FanDuel prices. The settled-bet ledger is empty, so ROI, CLV, Deflated Sharpe, PBO, ranks and rewards remain blank.
  • Own lines are experimental. The neutral-baseline model projects Orlando 111.50 at Phoenix 111.66 (fair PHX -0.16, total 223.16) and Miami 115.34 at Golden State 115.41 (fair GSW -0.07, total 230.75). These are point estimates, not calibrated probabilities or recommendations. Missing availability, roster drift and the uncalibrated margin distribution are flagged.
  • Dependence matters and aggregation can mislead. Pooled points–threes correlation is 0.689. Points–assists falls from 0.193 pooled to 0.044 after player centering. Edwards PTS≥25 + 3PM≥3 hits 0.5574 jointly versus an independence product of 0.4407 (lift 1.2646). Positive dependence means multiplying marginals understates the joint hit probability; it does not validate a parlay price.
  • Luck cannot be eliminated by a hot streak. At a hypothetical -110 break-even, detecting a true 55% hit rate with 80% exact power requires 2248 independent bets unadjusted, or 4055 under a 5%/8 Bonferroni screening scenario. Eight candidates are registered; every strategy fails the outcome, dependence, calibration and replication gates.
  • The repository audit is broad but scoped. Every baseline file and line was structurally scanned: 64,598 lines, 70 Python files, 66 CSVs and 139 HTML files. Syntax/JSON/JS checks pass. The audit found 9 inherited directory links without an index page and 26 findings; shared broken links are repaired only in final integration and reverified.
Decision: publish the research and blocked leaderboard; do not place bets or award rewards. The next evidence required is a point-in-time historical game/market ledger, complete availability, exact-leg dependence validation and a frozen prospective replication window.
