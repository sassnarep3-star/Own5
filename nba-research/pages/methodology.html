Method in plain language

  1. Freeze evidence. Transcribe official tables with row links; keep unknowns blank; record retrieval limits.
  2. Originate a transparent baseline. Shrink small-sample team ratings, match pace and opponent defense, and compare point estimates with archived prices. Do not invent probabilities.
  3. Measure dependence honestly. Report per-player and centered correlations, exact joint hit rates and conditional intervals. Never treat lag-1 scoring as parlay independence.
  4. Separate skill from luck as far as evidence allows. Use exact binomial power, Holm adjustment, Deflated Sharpe with the full trial family, CSCV/PBO, market calibration and prospective replication. No ledger means no skill claim.
  5. Fail closed. A strategy ranks only after all ten gates pass. Rewards are non-cash badges until funding is verified.
