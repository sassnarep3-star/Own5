Every flag carries the page it was observed on. A flag with an empty source link is a defect in the register itself and is treated as one.

How a flag gets closed

blocking
needs an input that does not exist here (a price, an inactive list, a second capture). Stays open until the input arrives.
review
two sources disagree, or a value is ambiguous. Closed by re-reading the official page, not by choosing the convenient number.
corrected, kept visible
an error this desk made, fixed in place with the correction recorded (OWN-20 is the worked example).

Paper research only. No bet is placed, no stake is live, no reward is issued. Every numeric cell is computed from a committed data file in nfl-market-desk/data/; every source link opens the page it came from. Cells that could not be confirmed against an official page are left blank and marked UNVERIFIED rather than estimated.
