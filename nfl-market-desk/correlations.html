What the numbers do and do not mean

  • n=32 is one season slice of 32 clubs. It is enough to report a coefficient and its p-value; it is not enough to support a player-level claim, which is why no player correlation appears here.
  • Points-for and win percentage correlating at r=0.725 is a sanity check on the data join, not a discovery.
  • Parlay and prop-combo dependence needs paired player outcomes and prop prices. Neither exists in this repository (OWN-17), so no parlay correlation is published and gate G7 blocks both combo families outright.
