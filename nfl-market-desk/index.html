The one-line reading

Of the 7 bet families this desk pre-registered, one clears its own signal test (S5, rest edge, hit rate 0.2917 against a price-implied null, n=24, Holm p=0.0397) — and it cannot be certified, because certification needs a closing price this repository does not contain. The best staking ROI on the board belongs to a family with 3 graded bets, which is exactly the number the brief told us never to rank by.

What this desk does not establish, stated up front

  • No price we can certify. nfl.com publishes no spread, total or moneyline, and no sportsbook page was reachable from the capture environment. The only prices in this repository come from a third-party mirror with no book, no jurisdiction and no timestamp. Those prices are labelled AGGREGATOR and are barred from certification and from any closing-line claim.
  • The model does not beat the market on the validation window. Weeks 5–8: model Brier 0.2427 against the market's 0.2192 on the same 57 games. It does beat it on the final holdout (weeks 9–10: 0.1524 vs 0.1570 on 24 games), but a 24-game holdout is not evidence of an edge and we do not present it as one.
  • No bet is live and no reward is issued. Every family is registered PAPER. Five of the eleven gates are not met, and the two that matter most — a certifiable price and a prospective sample — cannot be met from inside this repository.
  • Depth coverage is 11 of 32 clubs and every one of those charts is undated or one capture old. The replacement matrix is therefore a partial view, labelled as such.

What changed in this build

  • Price store opened. 195 snapshot rows now exist for the 39 originated Week-11 markets: 78 carry a number (our own fair line, and the aggregator reference), 117 are deliberately empty with a flag, because an opening and a close cannot be observed without a second clock.
  • Flags consolidated. The inherited register from PR #79 used the same identifiers (F-01…F-14) as this desk for entirely different findings. Both are now in one register with namespaces: INH- for the inherited fourteen, OWN- for this desk's twenty, 34 in total.
  • Two ledger counts corrected. The Week 11 slate note said 14 games (the page lists 13) and the injury capture note said 254 rows (the committed file holds 347). Both are corrected in place with the correction left visible as OWN-20, not overwritten.
  • The total market got an honest reference. A draft build derived a "market-implied margin standard deviation" from a symmetric price and produced 42.9-point standard deviations. That is an artefact, not a market number, so the field is now blank by construction and the total's market reference is the de-vigged two-way total price instead.

How to read this site

Each table row carries a source link. A cell that says UNVERIFIED is a cell where a value ought to be, must be shown, and could not be confirmed against an official page — it is not zero and it is not an estimate. Tiers are stated on every value: OFFICIAL a league or club page, AGGREGATOR a third-party mirror with undocumented provenance, DERIVED computed here from published inputs.

Paper research only. No bet is placed, no stake is live, no reward is issued. Every numeric cell is computed from a committed data file in nfl-market-desk/data/; every source link opens the page it came from. Cells that could not be confirmed against an official page are left blank and marked UNVERIFIED rather than estimated.
