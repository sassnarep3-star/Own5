Headline findings (all recomputable in-repo)

  • Prop legs are not independent: same-game 2-leg combos hit 10–16% more often than the independence product; the 3-leg combo PTS≥25 & AST≥5 & REB≥5 lifts 1.27× (phi table). Parlays/SBGS must be priced with measured phi, not multiplied marginals.
  • Cross-game legs behave: lag-1 PTS autocorrelation is ≈0 for both tracked players (both 95% CIs contain 0), supporting independent-game parlay assumptions.
  • Skill detection is brutally expensive: distinguishing a true 55% bettor from break-even (52.38% at −110) at 80% power needs ~2,243 graded bets. A 30–20 hot streak (60%) carries exact p=0.17 — indistinguishable from luck.
  • PTS×3PM is the strongest stat coupling (r=0.69 [0.58, 0.77]) while PTS×WIN is weak (r=0.09 [−0.09, 0.26]) — player-prop volume legs correlate far more than player-outcome legs.
