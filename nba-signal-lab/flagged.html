Irregularities observed at the source

  • https://www.nba.com/games?date=2026-11-04 renders only the calendar + game count without JavaScript; individual matchups are not in the static HTML.
  • cdn.nba.com/static/json/staticData/scheduleLeagueV2_1.json returns HTTP 500.
  • standings?Section=margins renders situational splits (3pts or less, score 100+, …), not PF/PA margins; PA/g is derived from PTS/g − +/−/g with 1dp rounding (±0.1 documented).
  • Player box-score pages render at most ~50 rows; remaining rows retrieved via the official DateFrom/DateTo filters (row counts then matched the official "Rows" totals exactly).
  • Team schedule pages label tip times "PDT" on nba.com; times are transcribed exactly as listed ("as listed" column) — no timezone conversion was inferred.
