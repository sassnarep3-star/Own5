Expected value & de-vig

For unit stakes without partial settlement: EV = p(win) × decimal odds + p(push) − 1. Proportional no-vig probabilities normalize inverse prices from a complete, synchronized, mutually exclusive no-push market. This is a convention, not ground truth. See the methodology for derivations and limitations.
