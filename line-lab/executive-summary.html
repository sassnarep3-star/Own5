Executive summary

What was built. A complete, source-linked NFL line-origination & betting-strategy project for the2026 season: verified data spine (standings, full game log, upcoming lines), a line-generation model with published divergence vs market, a gate-based strategy competition (singles / parlays / props), luck-isolation statistics, and this site. Everything below is reproducible from the repo with zero manual input.

Data & verification

  • Standings: all32 teams read from nfl.com (2026-11-02) — W-L-T, PF, PA, streaks.
  • Scores: 120 final games (Weeks1–8, incl. the Week5 NYG-WSH tie) + Week9 schedule; 58 verified directly on nfl.com, the rest via PFR and exact standings reconciliation (32/32 teams pass) — verification.
  • Lines: 16 upcoming boards (spread/total/ML + opens) from ESPN/DraftKings; Super Bowl futures for the displayed top16 only.
  • Irregularities flagged: count_flags() items — including an NFL.com broadcast-label oddity on the Week1 Wednesday kickoff, ESPN omitting ties from two records, ambiguous open-spread attribution on3 Week9 boards, and4 hard data gaps (historical closings, ATS history, turnovers, props) — all published on Flagged for Review.

Model & market

  • Line origination: mean-reverted Pythagorean strength + margin blend, home adjustment 1.75 pts (research band1.5–2.0; see Analysis §5). Divergences ≥2 spread pts / ≥3 total pts vs the Week9 board are marked as candidates — not as bets — pending the CLV gate.
  • Market context: closing-line research shows the NFL market is near-unbiased (mean error +0.06 pts over6,983 graded games; blind favorites cover ~47–49%) — so we pre-declare that beating closes, not win-rate, is the promotion currency.

Strategy competition status (honest readout)

  • S1 Pythagorean walk-forward singles: 61/103 = 59.2% (p=0.076) — promising, not yet proven at α=.05. Stage: conditional.
  • S2 Home-field singles: rejected —2026 home win% 49.6% (below .500).
  • S3 Totals fixed-number: best fixed system beat its -110 baseline in backtest but market-line gate pending (no historical closings) → conditional.
  • S4 Momentum: rejected — margin autocorrelation 0.0248 ≈0.
  • S5 Parlays/SGP + S6 Props: pre-declared gates + cited correlation values; blocked until prop/CLV sources come live. No numbers were invented.
  • S7 Week9 origination: live candidates logged; CLV logging starts with Week9 closes.

Luck removed how?

Exact binomial tests and Wilson intervals on every record; walk-forward design to prevent look-ahead; Pythagorean residual ladders to identify record-vs-margin luck (e.g., top over-achiever CLE +25.2%); one-score games shown to behave like coin flips (p=0.90); and CLV as the primary future promotion metric (outcome-independent skill proxy).
