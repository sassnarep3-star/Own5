How legs would be priced

  • Singles use the own line vs a market line; the trigger is a fixed disagreement threshold, not a feeling.
  • Same-game combos must use the empirical joint rate from the correlation page, never the product of marginals (G08).
  • Cross-game parlays may use the product only while the lag-1 independence check stays near zero, and only if the offered price beats it by the stated margin.
  • Every rule cites a required sample size (≥2,248 graded bets for a 55% edge) so a hot week cannot be mistaken for an edge.
