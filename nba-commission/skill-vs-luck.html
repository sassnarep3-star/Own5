A 55–45 run is not skill. A bettor with exactly the break-even rate goes 55–45 over 100 bets with probability 0.618 — i.e. routinely. Reaching 55% over 200 bets happens 25.1% of the time for such a bettor. Conversely, a genuine 55% edge is detected only 17.8% of the time over those same 200 bets.

Deflation before ranking

The leaderboard never displays a raw win rate. It displays (wins + K*p0) / (n + K) with K = 25, p0 = break-even rate — a 55–45 record deflates to 0.5448, barely above break-even, which is the honest reading of a 100-bet sample. Ranking also requires the minimum sample in the power table.
