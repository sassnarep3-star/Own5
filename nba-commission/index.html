Key findings

  • Net rating is the dominant team signal this season: net points/game vs win% r = 0.9206 (95% CI 0.839 to 0.962, n=30).
  • Same-game prop legs are strongly dependent. PTS × 3PM r = 0.717 (n=137); the PTS≥25 + 3PM≥3 combo hits 65.7% vs 56.5% if the legs were independent (lift ×1.16). Pricing such a combo off the product of marginals understates it.
  • Cross-game legs look close to independent: lag-1 PTS correlation is +0.056 (Edwards) and -0.041 (Dončić) — but n is small and this cannot prove independence.
  • A 55% true edge needs ≈2,248 graded bets to be certified at α=0.05 with 80% power against a −110 break-even of 0.5238. Nothing short of that is evidence of skill.
  • The 2026-11-05 home/road split does not measure home advantage (62–59 home, two-sided p = 0.856), so HFA = 2.5 pts stays a flagged model assumption (F02) with 0.0 and 3.0 runs shown per game.
  • Own lines are fragile to the model prior. 4 of 11 games change favourite across the seven variants, and the widest single-game split is 12.0 points of spread (OKC @ MEM: -4.8 to +7.2) — published per game, not hidden.
