Anti-gaming rules

Design intent. A 50/30/20 split of an unfunded pool with a hard sample floor means the only way to reach the top of this board is to survive hundreds of graded bets with a positive de-vigged closing-line value — which is what skill looks like when luck is subtracted, and what almost nothing survives.
