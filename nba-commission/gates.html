What would have to change

  • G04 / G05 need a settled sample in a second, non-overlapping window — 2,248 graded bets for a 55% edge at 80% power.
  • G06 / G07 need a timestamped, de-viggable price from a named operator, captured before tip-off, plus that operator’s published settlement rules for props (F10).
  • G09 needs the whole candidate family declared up front and Holm/Bonferroni-adjusted (α = 0.00625 for 8 tests).
  • G12 needs a frozen prospective window with a stopping rule agreed before it starts.
