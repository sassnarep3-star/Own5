Formula (reproducible)

  • off_i = PTS/g_i − league_avg, def_i = PA/g_i − league_avg, both blended with the 2025-26 value: est = w·current + (1−w)·prior, w = GP/(GP+K).
  • home_pts = league_avg + (est_off_home + est_def_away)/2 + HFA/2; away_pts = league_avg + (est_off_away + est_def_home)/2 − HFA/2.
  • spread = home_pts − away_pts, total = home_pts + away_pts, each rounded to the nearest 0.5 for publication.
  • No market input, no odds, no moneyline. Win probabilities are deliberately not published (F08).
