Method

Correlation values are computed in-repo by tools/compute_correlations.py (Pearson r) from the official standings snapshot data/standings-2026-wk8.csv, which was captured from nfl.com/standings ↗ on 2026-11-02 (2026 season through Week 8, all 32 teams). The script first asserts internal consistency of the official data (NET = PF − PA for every team; PCT = (W + 0.5·T)/GP; total wins = total losses) — all checks pass. Input source for every row: https://www.nfl.com/standings/ ↗.

Interpretation

  • Net points per game vs. winning percentage, r = 0.839 — the strongest verified single-team predictor; the backbone of STR02 (net-points mismatch).
  • Offense and defense are essentially independent (PF/G vs. PA/G, r = −0.012): build separate offensive and defensive signals rather than one blended rating.
  • Betting-leg correlations: same-game parlay leg correlations (e.g., QB passing yards vs. team total) are not published by any official source — numeric cells are left blank and flagged (F03). Policy instead: correlated legs are only combined inside sportsbook-offered SGPs (house rules, FanDuel ↗), with reduced stakes (STR06/STR07), and cross-strategy outcome correlation is capped at |r| < 0.50 by gate G05.
