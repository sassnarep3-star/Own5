The one sentence that matters

The model on this page forecasts the home margin at r=0.2901 out of sample. The line it is trying to beat forecasts the same games at r=0.453 on the identical fixture list. That is the finding, and it is why nothing here is promoted: a desk that cannot forecast better than the market has no business staking on the difference. Every gate below is written so that this result could not have been dressed up.

Read this before using any number

  • No price on this site is official. Odds come from an aggregator page that attributes them to one sportsbook. They are labelled OPERATOR everywhere they appear and are never presented as the league's numbers.
  • Scores are official-reconciled, not officially fetched one by one. 137 completed games are cross-checked against the pinned mirror and pinned again by the standings totals; 0 disagreements were found. Flag MK-F-08 records what was not done.
  • The historical window is an aggregator mirror with an undocumented line vintage. It is good enough to test a model against, and not good enough to call a closing price - so no closing-line claim is made anywhere.
  • 2 high-severity flags are open. They are on the review page, including the one that says the model loses to the market.
