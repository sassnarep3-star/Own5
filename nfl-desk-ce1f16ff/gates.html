Promotion gates

Every gate fails closed: a gate whose inputs do not exist reads BLOCKED or FAIL, never PASS. That is what makes the price store load-bearing rather than decorative - the closing-line gate is blocked until the store has closing rows, and the board names the file it is waiting for.

Against the dumbest rules that read the same posted line

Two rules graded on identical games, so the test is the exact McNemar test on the discordant pairs - not two independent hit rates, which is the usual way a small edge gets invented. S1 and S2 bet the home side by construction, so "always take the home side" is the same bet: the p-value of 1.0 is not a bug, it is the disclosure that those strategies are game-selectors and not side-detectors.

Demotion gate

Promotion asks "is there evidence?". Demotion asks the different and more urgent question: "has the evidence gone bad while money is on it?" A rule that needs a debate to execute is not a gate, so these fire on their own terms - and D1 deliberately requires no statistical significance, because waiting for proof of harm while live is how a bad month becomes a bad year.
