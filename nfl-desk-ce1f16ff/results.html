Why a results feed is first, not last

Until a result exists as data, nothing on this desk can be graded: not the backtest, not ROI, not closing-line value, not the demotion gate. This feed is the only source of outcome truth in the build, and it is built three ways at once - the official 2026 slate transcribed game by game, the same 137 scores cross-checked against a digest-pinned mirror, and the totals reconciled against the official standings for all 32 clubs - so that no single source has to be trusted on faith.
