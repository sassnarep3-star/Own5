A 59% "Questionable played" rate is a statement about hundreds of player-weeks. Applying it to one player's hamstring is a category error, and it is the error that makes most injury models unfalsifiable. This page therefore never predicts whether a named player plays; it reports what the report says, and what the absence removes.
