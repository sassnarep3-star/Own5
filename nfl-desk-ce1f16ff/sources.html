Capture record, verbatim

The transcriptions themselves are pinned in nfl-desk-ce1f16ff/pinned/capture.py (CAP-01 to CAP-06) as data, with the original page strings kept in raw_display inside the store, so a claim of "transcribed verbatim" can be checked against both.

Verification ladder

  • OFFICIAL nfl.com standings, schedule and injury-report pages, read and transcribed by this session (or vendored from a session that did, marked as such in flag MK-F-07).
  • AGGREGATOR nflverse mirrors of the league's own records, digest-pinned; scores reconciled three ways, prices labelled with an undocumented vintage.
  • OPERATOR one sportsbook's prices as displayed by an aggregator page; never presented as official, never used as an outcome.
  • DERIVED everything on every other page of this site, all of which rebuild from the rows above with python3 run.py.
