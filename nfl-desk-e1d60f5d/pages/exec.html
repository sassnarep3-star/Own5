Executive Summary

A transparent, source-linked research desk for originating NFL lines, testing strategies, and separating evidence from noise. Every table row links to an official source; values that cannot be confirmed are left blank and flagged. No bets are placed, no performance is claimed, and no strategy is certified until it passes validation gates.

What we have (verified from official sources)

  • 32-team 2026 standings (W-L-T, PF, PA, PCT) reconciled to nfl.com standings, captured 2026-11-17.
  • Week 11 schedule: 13 games + 6 byes (ATL, CLE, GB, LAR, NE, SEA), with kickoffs, networks and venues from nfl.com schedule. Vikings@49ers confirmed as a neutral-site game in Mexico City.
  • 10 questions registered (7 blocking), each with what we have, what we lack, and a discovery route.
  • 12 suggestions registered (11 awaiting decision), each with options, a recommended path, and the test that would change the call.
  • 12 flags published (7 blocking), each with a source link; nothing inferred.

What we still need before a decision can be made

  • Price-data policy: nfl.com publishes no betting prices, so opening/closing market lines are blank and flagged (F-01). A price source must be chosen before any backtest or ROI is published.
  • Injury/availability depth: only one injury-report read exists; no transactions, reserve list, official inactive list, or snap-count source (F-03 to F-06). Houston's depth chart is image-only (F-02).
  • Strategy validation: 0 strategies certified. All start PAPER behind fail-closed gates; backtest numbers are barred from the leaderboard.

Bottom line

The desk is evidence-first: blank cells mean "unverified," never "zero." Review the Questions and Suggestions registers before implementing any change, and the Flagged page for irregularities needing resolution.
