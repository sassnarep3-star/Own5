# Executive summary — NFL line-origination portfolio

**Integration review:** 2026-11-17 UTC

**Modeling/price/availability snapshots in the principal market desk:** through 2026-11-15 UTC; the separate official standings/schedule desk was read 2026-11-17 UTC.

**Purpose:** an entry point and an independent, fail-closed review of the repository's existing NFL evidence desks. This does not replace their underlying source data, and it does not create a live betting feed.

## Bottom line

The repository contains useful, source-linked work on line origination, verified results, availability, depth, strategy pre-registration, validation gates, questions, suggestions and flagged items. It is **not ready to make a live betting decision**. There is no verified operator price feed, no market opening/closing quote for each originated line, no qualified strategy, and no authorized, point-in-time official availability dataset complete across all clubs.

No strategy is certified by this hub. No reward is active. Do not treat any existing historical leaderboard statistic as prospective evidence or as a basis for a stake. The user explicitly prohibited backtest figures on the leaderboard; existing desk pages still expose historical performance values and are flagged for correction in their owning namespaces. The market desk's [readiness register](../nfl-market-desk/readiness.html) records a 145-game official-result corpus that contains the 136-game pre-existing log, marks that backtest corpus excluded from the leaderboard, and records ten price-policy rows fixed before any ROI publication. Those are repository-register claims, not live prices or leaderboard evidence; its market-opening and closing operator fields remain unpopulated.

## Important repository findings

1. **Leaderboard quarantine is necessary.** The e46 strategy outputs contain historical Weeks 1–10 outcome metrics. The 83a7 leaderboard labels Weeks 5–10 "prospective only" while its registration ledger records a registration timestamp of 2026-11-15T23:40Z. Weeks 5–9 were already played before registration; Week 10 spans November 12–15 and its game-level overlap has not been audited. The displayed window is not verified as wholly prospective and its values are not accepted as evidence of skill here. The new [`leaderboard.html`](leaderboard.html) publishes no performance values. The affected pages are preserved and linked on the [Flagged for Review page](flagged.html); changes to those other session-owned files are requested in `COORDINATION.md`, not made here.
2. **Market pricing is not verified.** The [price snapshot register](../nfl-market-desk/data/price_snapshots.csv) contains aggregator-tier references without a verified operator, jurisdiction, quote time or closing time; all 39 originated markets have blank operator open/close fields (0 of 78). The [price policy](../nfl-market-desk/data/price_policy.csv) does not identify a qualifying official or licensed source. Consequently, market residual, CLV and real-price ROI are undefined. Do not fill them with an aggregator line.
3. **Injury tables are not reconciled.** Separate Week 10 datasets contain 326 and 347 rows. The a991 capture differs by 268 added/changed/removed records from an older 276-row table; a later a699 row-level comparison of 347 official rows against the 326-row e46 table found 134 identical, 192 differing and 21 absent rows, with 49 of the 347 independently re-read against the November 15 capture. These are different comparisons, not interchangeable snapshots. Review the [`a699 row-level comparison`](../nfl-audit-a699fc53/comparison.html) and the [`a991 recapture`](../nfl-capture-a991e3fb/index.html) before using any injury count.
4. **Availability is not yet a replayable time series.** The [capture notes](../nfl-capture-a991e3fb/README.md) and [desk manifest](../nfl-desk-e46fb855/data/manifest.csv) show dated reads, not an immutable daily Wednesday-to-kickoff archive. A scheduled fetch that is not retained with its timestamp, raw hash and conflict flags cannot support point-in-time backtests or an injury-surprise feature.
5. **The 32-club roster/depth gate remains blocked.** The [32-row roster sweep](../nfl-market-desk/data/roster_sweep_32.csv) is largely a shell: roster URL/count fields are populated for only one row. [Market depth coverage](../nfl-market-desk/data/depth_coverage.csv) reports eleven parsed clubs; the [e46 team-availability table](../nfl-desk-e46fb855/data/team_availability.csv) labels thirty parsed, one Houston image-only and one New England partial. These are different coverage definitions, not a completed all-club sweep. The [official Texans chart](https://www.houstontexans.com/team/depth-chart) is image-only in the read; no team-level personnel model should be trusted until roster/IR transactions and a verified depth or authorized snap-count path cover all 32 clubs.
6. **Three readiness/review registers are not one register.** The [market register](../nfl-market-desk/data/decision_readiness.csv) has 78 rows, the [e46 register](../nfl-desk-e46fb855/data/requirements.csv) has 72, and the newer [decision-review hub](../nfl-review-3bba0722/) has 14 priority gates in its [readiness register](../nfl-review-3bba0722/data/readiness.json). No row-by-row mapping is verified. This hub points to the 78-row decision-readiness view as its primary navigation target but does not sum the three registers.
7. **Venue provenance is still partial.** The [Week 11 venue table](../nfl-market-desk/data/venues.csv) records a schedule/ticket-or-hotel-destination method rather than a per-fixture Game Center URL; it has 13 rows, 12 named values and every row carries `OWN-56`. One value is blank. The hub links the official release and [live schedule](https://www.nfl.com/schedules/2026/by-week/week-11), but every fixture still needs its own Game Center source.
8. **Raw-data terms need a first-class policy.** The integration hub publishes only derived repository-audit findings and official links. Existing older desks still publish row-level data; their retention and display rights need review before treating those datasets as a public product.
9. **The Week 10 bye register is inconsistent.** The [e46 Week 10 bye file](../nfl-desk-e46fb855/data/bye_weeks_2026.csv) lists 14 clubs, while the official [Week 10 schedule](https://www.nfl.com/schedules/2026/by-week/week-10), [injury report](https://www.nfl.com/injuries/league/2026/reg10) and independent [a699 source audit](../nfl-audit-a699fc53/data/brief_claims_check.csv) identify four byes: Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia and Tampa Bay. Until the register is corrected and its verifier checks the official slate, do not use it for availability coverage. See the [Flagged for Review page](flagged.html).
10. **Three newer evidence/review desks were merged after the original integration claim.** The [a699 Week 10 injury/transactions audit](../nfl-audit-a699fc53/index.html), the [e1d60f5d verified standings and Week 11 schedule desk](../nfl-desk-e1d60f5d/index.html), and the [3bba0722 decision-review hub](../nfl-review-3bba0722/) are now linked from the dashboard. Their underlying namespaces remain read-only here.

## Recommended decision path

1. Keep all performance rankings empty until a strategy is registered **before** a future evaluation window, and score only that prospective window. Use benchmark-relative proper scores, game-cluster uncertainty and multiplicity adjustment for signal qualification; show staking/CLV separately and only after a permitted price source exists.
2. Choose and document a price-data provider, permissions, timestamp semantics and settlement rules before publishing any price-relative backtest or ROI.
3. Resolve source terms for official Game Books before any capture or reuse. If permission is unavailable, find another authorized official source or leave starters/inactives/snaps blank.
4. Implement immutable, access-controlled daily availability captures with timestamp, source URL, content hash, parser/schema version and conflict flags; publish only permitted derived summaries.
5. Complete a 32-club roster/IR/depth sweep and preserve shared, empty and grouped depth slots. Do not infer replacements from jersey number, experience, salary or a single depth-chart column.
6. Link each fixture to its official Game Center for venue; keep the dated schedule release beside the live schedule.
7. Resolve the 78/72 requirement crosswalk, injury-row discrepancies and legacy leaderboard publication in the source-owning desks before describing the repository as fully integrated.
