Rules common to every strategy

  • Edge threshold and stake rule are fixed before the first bet; changing either creates a new strategy ID (counts toward the multiple-testing penalty).
  • Parlays and prop combos must be priced with a measured joint probability (see Correlations). Legs with a blank correlation are not allowed.
  • Own lines are published before tip and frozen in the ledger; results come only from official game pages.
