These are exact binomial calculations (pure mathematics, flag F09). Break-even at -110 odds is 110/210 = 52.38%; that odds convention is a market convention, not an official NBA fact.

What this means for the leaderboard

  • A bettor who is right 55% of the time needs roughly 2270 settled bets before a break-even test is reliable. Hundreds of bets are not enough; dozens are noise.
  • The best of many unskilled entrants always looks skilled over short samples, so rankings use closing-line value, minimum sample sizes and a multiple-testing penalty.
  • Applied to this desk: the prior-only model called 13 of 22 games straight-up. Under a fair coin P(at least 13 of 22) = 26.2%, i.e. not distinguishable from luck.
