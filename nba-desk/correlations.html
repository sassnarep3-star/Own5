The earlier 10-game Anthony Edwards correlations in nba-2026-lab are exploratory only (n = 10) and are not reused here.

Parlay arithmetic: joint probability of two legs

P(A and B) = pq + phi x sqrt(p(1-p)q(1-q)). A price built on independent legs overstates or understates the true joint probability by the ratio shown. Pure mathematics, no official source (flag F09).
