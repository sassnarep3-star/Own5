Honest reading. The model's edge over a naive baseline on 22 games is within noise (margin z = 1.22; total z = 0.10). The 30-team test is the stronger evidence: last season's margin carries real but partial information (r = 0.43), and shrinking it by beta beat both no-shrinkage and no-information predictors, by a small margin.

Method in five lines

  1. Team rating = posterior margin: in-season margin weighted by games played G against a prior of beta x last season's margin; weight G/(G+K).
  2. Home margin = rating(home) - rating(away) + home-court advantage. Home win probability = Phi(margin / sigma).
  3. Game total = league level (2026-27 to date) + both teams' shrunk total deviations.
  4. beta, beta_T and the home-court advantage come from official team tables (training pair 2024-25 to 2025-26, 2025-26 splits). sigma and K use the error size on the 22-game sample (in-sample, flag F04). Nothing is tuned to maximise accuracy.
  5. Lines are frozen in the ledger before tip and settled against official final scores.
