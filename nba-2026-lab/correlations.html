Calculation & scope

r = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) / √[Σ(x−x̄)² Σ(y−ȳ)²]. “Win” is 1 for W and 0 for L; Pearson with binary win is a point-biserial correlation. No rounding before calculation. Recompute with python3 nba-2026-lab/compute.py. A 3PM–PTS link is partly arithmetic (made threes contribute points); this is not an independent signal. Only same-player/game continuous outcome dependence is shown; correlations between binary leg hits at a quoted line, across games, or conditional on injury remain blank / unverified.
