Model, inputs & ratings — probability plus inputs

Everything the model used is stored and hashed; nothing is asserted that the corpus cannot support.

Pre-registered specification (v1, frozen)

  • Corpus: 150 verified finals — weeks 1-9 from the pinned, sha256-verified nflverse snapshot; week 10 from the official NFL.com scores page read 2026-11-18.
  • Two opponent-adjustment iterations (recentered); one-pass HFA on location=Home games only (n=142); HFA = 0 at neutral sites (MIN@SF Mexico City confirmed via the official game centre).
  • σ = residual std of margins over all 150 games; p(home) = Φ(m/σ) scaled by (1 − empirical tie rate).
  • Week 11 availability is NOT a feature (F-P02). Injury/depth ablation is future work once the report publishes (S-P04/Q-P04).

Full spec: model_spec.md — frozen before any Week 11 result exists. Changing it requires a new version and a new snapshot.
