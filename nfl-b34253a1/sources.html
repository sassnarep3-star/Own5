Provenance rules

What a source link does and does not prove.

Primary evidence

NFL schedules, game pages, standings, team stats and injury reports anchor the current NFL facts. Club pages are the publisher for their listed depth order; their labels and freshness vary.

Separate operator evidence

DraftKings and FanDuel are sportsbook publishers, not the NFL. Each book quote is retained separately, with date-only timing and no claim of user-specific availability.

Derived outputs

Line formulas and correlations are generated by the isolated engine. Their output provenance includes inputs and formula, not an external claim that the result is accurate or useful for betting.

Legacy material

Earlier secondary market/history datasets stay flagged in the audit. This session did not rewrite or certify other sessions’ files.
