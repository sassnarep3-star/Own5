FLG-08Flag: FLG-08Status: OPEN_NONBLOCKINGHIGH

Walk-forward scope

Finding
The simple no-HFA PF/G + opponent PA/G estimator was evaluated over 120 Week 2-9 games from one 2026 season. MAE=10.4048, r=0.135006, p=0.141514, naive 95% Fisher interval [-0.045336,0.306826].
Impact
One-season within-sample accuracy does not establish profitability or skill; interval does not adjust for shared team/week dependence.
Action
Treat as descriptive only; require prospective multiple-season holdout, calibrated probabilities and clustered uncertainty.
