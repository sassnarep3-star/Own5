These are the audit’s concise repository-level findings; see each per-line note for context.

No source URL recorded.

RA-F02Finding ID: RA-F02

Legacy ESPN market/futures values and older Spreadspoke/nflverse-derived backtests are not official NFL/bookmaker evidence; not reused and explicitly flagged.

No source URL recorded.

RA-F03Finding ID: RA-F03

Multiple older standings, injury and odds files are frozen at 2026-11-02. They are not presented as a live Week 10 state; current official values are separate.

No source URL recorded.

RA-F04Finding ID: RA-F04

Some official club depth charts are marked unofficial and do not establish active status, snap share or actual replacement; Houston's fetched chart is image-only and string assignments remain blank.

No source URL recorded.

RA-F05Finding ID: RA-F05
