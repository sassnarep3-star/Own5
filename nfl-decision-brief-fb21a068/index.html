Recommended decision nowKeep the project in paper / shadow mode. Start with singles. Do not rank parlays, use unverified availability as a feature, publish market residuals from an uncleared source, or award cash until the corresponding gates pass.

This is a conservative project-governance recommendation, not a claim that any strategy has or has not made money. Luck cannot be removed from outcomes; only measured uncertainty and prospective replication can limit false confidence.

Go / no-go sequence

Gates that must be passed before scale-up

  1. Define data rights and retention. Identify allowed sources, markets, jurisdiction, historical coverage, open/close definition and timestamp policy. Until then: no market residual / CLV claim.
  2. Capture immutable information before kickoff. Preserve dated official reports and source hashes, roster / IR changes, actual inactive and starter evidence. Unknown remains unknown; no jersey-order or salary shortcut.
  3. Freeze a singles-only model and evaluation protocol. Save each probability with its inputs and registration hash before the event. Use chronological train/validation/holdout windows and explicit random/favorite controls.
  4. Qualify signal with proper scores and uncertainty. Keep the signal score distinct from staking and realized ROI; account for game clustering and the number of tested strategies. Backtests never qualify a public leaderboard.
  5. Escrow prospective evidence, then consider products separately. Require prospective replication and an available permitted close-price benchmark before live consideration. Parlays / same-game / prop combinations remain behind direct joint-probability and operator settlement-rule gates.
