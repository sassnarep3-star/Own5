Read this first

This console answers one question: what would have to be true before any number here could justify a bet? The honest answer today is that it cannot. There is no market price anywhere in this repository, so no residual, no closing-line value and no return can be computed. Our own line is published for every game; the market column is blank and flagged. Nothing is certified, nothing is ranked, no reward is issued.

What this session added

  • A dedicated Suggestions section: 18 reviewable paths, each with options, a recommendation and an explicit "what would change the call". Nothing is implemented without a decision, and the review tray records your choice locally.
  • A Questions register: 22 questions split into what we have, what we do not have, and the cheapest route to discover each one.
  • A fresh 32-club depth sweep (2026-11-17) with 889 rows, a source URL and a capture date on every row, diffed against the 2026-11-15 read.
  • Proof that two blocking gaps are closable: official inactives and official game books are published on club sites - links on the questions page.
