The 8 blockers

  • F-01 Markets - No market price of any kind exists anywhere in this repository: no spread, no total, no moneyline, from any source Published instead: the market column is blank on every row and the difference column is explicitly undefined
  • F-02 Depth - Houston publishes its depth chart as an image, so it contributes zero parsed rows Published instead: Houston is listed with an explicit image-only flag and is excluded from every pairing
  • F-03 Injuries - Only two reads of the official injury report exist, two days apart, covering 5 of 32 clubs Published instead: the delta is published only for the 44 rows where both reads exist; every other row is level-only and marked
  • F-04 Roster - No transactions log and no reserve list has been read, so absent is ambiguous between healthy and ineligible Published instead: absence is recorded as unknown, never as available
  • F-05 Outcomes - No official inactive list and no game book has been read Published instead: the announced-starter and replacement columns stay blank; the discovery route is recorded as suggestion S-01 and S-02
  • F-06 Depth - No official snap-count source has been read, so playing time is unmeasured Published instead: depth is modelled from club charts only, and the caveat is published on the depth page
  • F-12 Data - The results feed holds one season, so the chronological holdout in R-70 and the backtest corpus in R-19 are both unreachable Published instead: 149 finals published; no holdout declared
  • F-13 Combos - No joint probabilities and no operator void/push rules, so no parlay or prop combo can be ranked Published instead: all three combo divisions are defined and empty

Two gaps we proved are closable

For two full sessions these were recorded as "no official source exists". This session found the source: club sites publish both.

  • Official inactives - Atlanta Falcons Week 10 inactives vs Kansas City Chiefs source
  • Official game book - Patriots vs Lions Week 10 game book source

Neither is captured yet. Suggestions S-01 and S-02 propose a three-club pilot before scaling to 32.

What we recommend, in order

  2. Pilot official inactives on three clubs (S-01).
  3. Automate the injury capture on the Wednesday-Friday cadence (S-03).
  4. Add parser drift alarms before automating anything (S-18).
  5. Resolve Houston with a structured source, never by transcribing the image (S-04).

Limitations, stated plainly

  • Clubs label their own depth charts unofficial. Depth order is a club statement, not a league one.
  • Snap counts are not captured, so playing time is unmeasured and the brief's preferred depth signal is unavailable.
  • Ratings are fitted on one season and ridge-penalised; that is necessary for identifiability and it shrinks every rating toward zero.
  • The Pythagorean exponent (2.37) is a published convention, not a league value, and it is labelled as such everywhere it appears.
  • Two depth reads two days apart differ on 62 of 730 like-for-like rows. A third read is needed before that is called a club-side change rather than a parser difference.
