Originated lines

Our own line for every week-11 game, originated side then total then moneyline.

The market column is empty on purpose. No market price exists anywhere in this repository, so no residual, no closing-line value and no return can be computed. Where a market value would sit, the cell is blank and marked UNVERIFIED rather than filled in.

Origination order (R-03)

  1. Side - fitted ratings plus home-field advantage give the spread and p(home win).
  2. Total - two estimators, published side by side with the gap, until a chronological walk-forward separates them (R-62).
  3. Moneyline - derived from the step-1 probability only. It never feeds back into the side.

Spread sigma 13.5 is a published convention, not a league value.
