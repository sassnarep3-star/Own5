Luck vs Skill

Per-pick nulls, exact Poisson-binomial tests, Holm-Bonferroni, Deflated Sharpe and the selectivity test.

How luck is removed here. Each pick is scored against its OWN null: the home win rate observed in the weeks strictly before that game. A strategy that mostly picks away teams is therefore not credited with a fake edge. Every p-value is an exact Poisson-binomial test across those heterogeneous per-pick nulls, then Holm-Bonferroni is applied across the whole strategy family, and the Deflated Sharpe Ratio accounts for how many strategies were tried.
