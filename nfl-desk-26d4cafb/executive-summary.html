Findings that should change decisions

  1. Do not use a +2.0 home-field assumption for 2026. The empirical value on 131 non-neutral games is -0.05 points with a 95% interval of [-2.3367, 2.2291].
  2. Do not read the official weekly injury table as a health signal for Monday night games. Baltimore's QB1 and QB2 are both on IR while the table shows “No Injuries Reported”.
  3. Do not rank strategies on raw win rate. With ten candidates, one is expected to look significant at p<0.05 by chance alone; only Holm-adjusted p and the Deflated Sharpe Ratio survive that.
  4. Do not price parlays as independent. Measured leg correlation on this sample is -0.3333; the observed joint rate is 0.5 against an independence-implied 0.5625.
  5. Two-leg parlays at -110 per leg need a 27.4% hit rate to break even. This sample hit 0.5 on 16 non-overlapping parlays, so on paper they clear it — but n < 20, so gate G-03 blocks the strategy until more games accumulate.
