Headline findings

  1. The repository's NFL boards are 16 games stale. Four boards are frozen at 2026-11-02 and were missing Week 8 Monday Night Football and all 15 Week 9 games. All 16 were re-fetched verbatim from NFL.com and merged; the resulting 136-game ledger reconciles 32/32 teams to nfl.com/standings with zero mismatches on W, L, T, PF and PA.
  2. 2026 home-field advantage is statistically indistinguishable from zero (-0.05 pts, p=0.96). Prior boards assumed +2.0. That assumption is the single largest systematic error in the repo: on the matched sample it produced a margin bias of 5.346 points.
  3. Baltimore is starting its third-string quarterback on Monday night while the official Week 10 injury table still reads “No Injuries Reported” for that game, because IR players are excluded and the report is in its pre-Thursday window. See Injuries & Depth and flag FLG-06.
  4. Two of ten strategies clear all ten validation gates after Holm-Bonferroni family-wise control and Deflated Sharpe Ratio: see the Leaderboard. The other eight are blocked or unranked because they fail a gate, not because they were filtered silently.
  5. Selective skill is NOT established. Accuracy across confidence terciles is 0.414 / 0.828 / 0.724 — not monotone, so the highest-confidence bucket does not beat the middle one. The engine reports this rather than claiming selectivity. See Luck vs Skill.
  6. No official market or player-prop lines exist. NFL.com publishes none, so every market, price, edge and CLV column on this site is deliberately left blank and flagged (FLG-01, FLG-02) rather than sourced from a third-party scrape.
