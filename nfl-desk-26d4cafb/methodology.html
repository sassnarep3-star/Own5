Methodology

Exactly how every number on this site was produced, and what was deliberately not done.

Declared model

  • Team offence = shrunk, opponent-adjusted points per game above league mean.
  • Team defence = shrunk, opponent-adjusted points allowed per game above league mean.
  • Expected points: pts_A = league_mean + off_A + def_B, with the empirical home-field advantage split half to each side and zeroed for confirmed neutral-site games.
  • Score distribution: independent Poisson on the two expected point totals, giving win, loss and tie probabilities, the fair spread and the fair total by direct enumeration.
  • Injury/depth toll: itemised per team per unit, weighted by the official availability tier, subtracted from the affected unit's own scoring expectation. These are bounded model priors, never official statistics.

Zero-look-ahead protocol

  • Walk-forward: a bet graded in week w is produced by a model fitted only on weeks < w.
  • The null probability for each pick also comes only from weeks < w.
  • Shrinkage k is declared before the fact; the sensitivity table shows the choice is not load-bearing (MAE varies only from 9.40 to 9.86 across k = 3 to 24).
  • No official injury register exists for Weeks 1-9, so no strategy using injuries is backtested; those entries are marked not evaluable rather than graded on invented data.
