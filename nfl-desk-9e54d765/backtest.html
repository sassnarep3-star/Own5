Backtest and removal of luck

Walk-forward scoring against the close and against controls, per season, with the sample sizes a ranking would need.

Nothing here is a certified strategy. These are families scored walk-forward on 5186 games from 2006 to 2026, each game priced with parameters fitted only on earlier weeks. The target is P(home win); the benchmark is the de-vigged closing moneyline. A control family is included on purpose: if the model cannot beat the random control, the model is adding nothing.
