What the residuals say, honestly

The residuals are large — several points on the side — and that is not a strength. The same model loses to the closing line in the backtest (see the skill intervals), so a big gap between our line and the market is far more likely to be our error than an edge. The residual is published because a residual series accumulates evidence; it is not a pick.
