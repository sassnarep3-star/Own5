3. What it did not measure

  • No closing prices. All 13 fixtures kick off after this build. The price store holds opens only, and the closing columns are empty by policy — not estimated. Clearing condition on R01.
  • No prospective results. Every leaderboard row shows n_settled = 0. Nothing can be ranked until games settle.
  • No combo pricing. Three divisions are BLOCKED, with the clearing condition published on each row and the reason in Correlations.
  • No single venue read. Only one of thirteen venues' game pages was read (F07), so venue-level detail is thin.
  • No third-party price confirmation. Two operators are read, but no independent third source — F15.

4. What the "removal of luck" panel says

The desk reports home-rate 0.559247 over 6591 games, against a break-even of 0.5. Separating a genuine 55% from a coin flip needs 783 settled bets; 54% needs 1224. With zero prospective bets settled, no ranking on this site can yet distinguish skill from luck — which is why the leaderboard is deliberately empty of results rather than filled with backtest numbers.

Two independent scores are reported for every strategy — Brier skill for the signal, flat ROI for the staking — and never merged. The 2026 tail already shows them disagreeing, which is exactly the case the split exists to expose.

5. The next decision, and who makes it

The desk recommends one thing: run the paper period, do not promote anything to live, and treat the origination residuals as a research series rather than as picks. The gate that would change that is written on every leaderboard row. The questions that would unblock the missing pieces are Q01 (closing prices), Q02 (operator terms), Q03 (official inactive lists). The suggestions that would change the desk most are S05 and S13, both deliberately left as drafts for the owner.

Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation, and no strategy has been certified.
