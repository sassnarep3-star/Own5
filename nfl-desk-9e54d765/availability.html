Injuries, designations and depth

The official Week 11 set, the two-clock delta, the empirical meaning of each designation, and depth measured from playing time.

Three rules this page keeps. The injury signal is a delta between two stamped reads, never a level. Depth is playing time, never jersey order, experience or salary. And a replacement is never named: without an official inactive list, a replacement can be bounded by a measured usage gap but not asserted.
