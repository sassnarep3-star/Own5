Why this closes the combo gate

Parlay and combo pricing by multiplication assumes independence. The same-game side/total pair is only weakly related (0.02), but "weakly" is not "zero", and weak dependence is enough to make a multiplied marginal probability wrong in a way that compounds with every extra leg. The gate therefore stays closed until a joint distribution is fitted and validated for the exact leg set — a condition published on every blocked leaderboard row.

What would let a combo open

A fitted bivariate distribution per leg pair, evaluated on held-out games, that beats the independent product by more than one percentage point, with n published beside the gap — plus the operator's own void and push rules, quoted from the operator's own rules page rather than from memory.
