Leaderboard

Signal and staking in separate columns, a demotion gate on every row, and no backtest number anywhere on the board.

Read the two score columns as different things. Signal is Brier skill against the close. Staking is a flat-unit return at the market price. They are published side by side and never combined, because a lucky staking run must not be able to mask a bad signal — and on this desk the 2026 tail already shows a strategy whose staking ROI interval covers zero while its signal interval sits below zero.
No strategy carries a backtest number on this board. n_settled is 0 on every ranked row because nothing has been settled prospectively. The backtest lives in a separate table (Backtest & luck) and the verifier fails the build if any backtest column appears here. That is the pre-registration rule, enforced mechanically rather than by good intentions.

Why the combo divisions are blocked

A parlay or combo probability built by multiplying marginal probabilities is only correct when the legs are independent. They are not: same-game margin and total correlate 0.02 [-0.0308, 0.0706] at n=1495. Until a joint distribution is fitted and validated for the exact leg set, and until the operator's void and push rules are quoted from its own rules page, no combo can be ranked. The clearing condition is on every blocked row above.
