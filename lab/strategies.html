Strategies tested

Seven deterministic rules covering single bets, cross-game parlays and same-game combinations. Thresholds were fixed before grading; nothing was tuned on the test window. Pushes refund the stake. Player-prop combinations are analysed on the combo pricing page instead of being graded, because no verified sportsbook prop price exists to grade against.
