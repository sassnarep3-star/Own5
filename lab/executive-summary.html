The positive finding

QB passing-yards over + same-team WR1 receiving-yards over: φ = 0.249, joint rate 6.1pp above the naive product, independent pricing rejected, and the lift holds out-of-sample in every season tested (mean theoretical EV +0.332/unit). Real books shade these legs, so this measures the pricing error — not an obtainable price.

Why nothing was promoted

The market beat our model on spread RMSE (13.1988 vs 13.6276) and Brier score (0.21009 vs 0.22232). The standard hold is 4.76%. Every strategy also fails gate G9, because official verification covers a sample rather than the full universe. Unverified inputs cannot produce verified conclusions.

Next steps

  1. Expand official verification toward a census (unblocks G9).
  2. Add injury, quarterback and weather features.
  3. Extend player data past 2021 and re-test the prop correlation.
  4. Obtain timestamped prop prices to test capturability.
  5. Pre-register new strategies before grading.
