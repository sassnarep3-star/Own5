Flagged for review

Every limitation, third-party dependency and unverified value in this project. These items are published rather than hidden, and none of them were resolved by guessing a value. Severity reflects how much the item constrains the conclusions.

Flagged items do not block publication. They block promotion: no strategy is called validated while an integrity gate is open.
