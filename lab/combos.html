Combination pricing

If two legs are correlated, the fair price of the combination is set by the observed joint probability, not by multiplying the two single-leg probabilities. This page quantifies that gap and tests whether it survives out-of-sample.

No sportsbook player-prop lines are published by any official source. We therefore do not invent a book price or claim a prop ROI. Instead each player's line is originated in-repo as his trailing season-to-date median (minimum three prior games), which makes "prop hits" a well-defined event. The deliverable is the measured mispricing, not a fabricated return.

Consistent across every season tested. Mean out-of-sample EV against a naively-priced book is +0.332 units per unit staked. Capturing it requires a book that has not adjusted — which is an empirical question this dataset cannot answer.
