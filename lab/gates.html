Why the integrity gates matter

G9 (official verification) fails for every strategy in this run. Only 80 games have been re-checked line-by-line against official NFL.com scores — a stratified sample, not a census of the 5,199-game universe. Until more of the backtest is officially verified, no strategy can be promoted, even if its statistics were strong. This is deliberate: the project's rule is that unverified inputs cannot produce verified conclusions.
G8 (price integrity) fails where the upstream table lacked a specific price and a −110 assumption was applied. Assumed prices are counted per bet, never hidden.
