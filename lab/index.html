What this is

An evidence-first attempt to originate our own NFL lines and test whether any signal survives contact with the closing market. 4,991 regular-season games (2006–2025) are priced strictly walk-forward, then seven strategies spanning singles, parlays and same-game combinations are graded at real prices and pushed through ten hard validation gates.

Headline result: 0 of 7 strategies passed. No strategy cleared all hard gates. No reward tier is awarded and no profitable edge is claimed. The closing line beat our model on both spread accuracy and win probability. That is the finding, and it is reported rather than buried.

The one robust positive finding

Same-game player props are genuinely correlated. A quarterback's passing-yards over and his top receiver's receiving-yards over hit together far more often than independence implies: φ = 0.249, and the joint rate exceeds the naive product by roughly six percentage points. The lift persists out-of-sample in every season tested. Multiplying the two legs' probabilities — the standard parlay shortcut — materially misprices this combo. See Correlations and Combo Pricing.
