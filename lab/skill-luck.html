Removing luck from the equation

Luck cannot be eliminated from a football outcome. What can be done is to refuse to call a result skill until it survives tests designed to kill lucky backtests. Six independent checks are applied to every strategy.

Luck share is the fraction of the observed deviation from break-even that one standard deviation of pure binomial noise could explain. A value of 1.0 means the result is entirely consistent with luck.

Luck cannot be removed from outcomes; it can only be bounded. No strategy is promoted on point estimate alone.
