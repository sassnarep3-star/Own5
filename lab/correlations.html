Measured correlations

For each pair of bet legs we compare the observed joint hit rate to the product of the marginals (what naive parlay pricing assumes). φ is the correct correlation for two binary legs. Confidence intervals come from a 4,000-replicate bootstrap that resamples whole weeks, so same-week dependence is preserved. "Independence rejected" means the naive product falls outside the bootstrap interval of the true joint rate.
