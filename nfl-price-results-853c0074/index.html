Decision status

Do not interpret the current data as a validated betting edge. We have a row-linked historic final-score feed, but no qualifying open/close quote history and no sourced market settlements. ROI, closing-line value, prop-combo results and leaderboard rewards are therefore not reported.

For an auditable future pipeline: originate side first, then total, then moneyline; assign stable market/selection IDs; capture opening and a predeclared close in UTC; attach the official source and rule profile; keep signal metrics separate from staking outcomes.

What is available

  • Official NFL game-result rows copied from the repository’s Week 1–9 result ledger; exact game source URL preserved on each row.
  • Existing DraftKings/FanDuel quote metadata is preserved separately. Historical price cells are blank because source captures/timestamps are unavailable; none are classified as opening/closing.
  • Append-only schemas and validation for future price snapshots and verified settlements.
  • Questions that block decisions, reviewable implementation suggestions and a nonblocking flagged register.
