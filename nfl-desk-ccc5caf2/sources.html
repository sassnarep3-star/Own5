Tier rules

  • OFFICIAL: published directly by the NFL (nfl.com, operations.nfl.com). Treated as authoritative.
  • AGGREGATOR: a third-party mirror of official data (nflverse, spreadspoke). Used only when no official page exists; pinned by SHA-256 and labelled AGGREGATOR in every table.
  • No value is ever inferred. If a value cannot be confirmed from an official/primary source, it is left blank and flagged.
