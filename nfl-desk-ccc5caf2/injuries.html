Injuries & depth

Official nfl.com injury report read 2026-11-18 (Week 10 snapshot), with per-club rows and practice/game status. Depth is inferred from snap share within team and position group — not from jersey number, experience or salary (explicitly rejected). The NFL publishes no machine-readable official depth chart (F16/F05).

Depth is inference, not official. Depth strings (1st–4th string) are computed from season-to-date snap share within team and position group. No active list or snap share is captured directly, and no machine-readable depth chart exists from the league. Treating column 1 as 'the starter' would fabricate starters the club never named. Lineup deficiency is used as a line-model input, not a bet trigger (suggestion S05). The market already prices about half of it; the residual ATS record shows no edge. First-pass sign error (57.4% → 51.4%) documented and retained as a methodology lesson (F11).

Signal = surprise, never a level

  • Practice-status weights used are judgement calls, not published probabilities (F14): Out 1.0 / Doubtful 0.75 / Questionable 0.5; DNP 0.5 / Limited 0.25 / Full 0.0.
  • The injury signal should be the delta (Questionable→Out, DNP→LP→FP progression), never the level.
  • Designation reliability (play rate per designation with confidence intervals) has not yet been scored against official inactive lists — required before any designation becomes a model feature.
