Policy rules (from the brief)

  • Pre-registration: rule text, sample window and gates committed to git before the evaluation window opens; the commit hash is the registration. Backtest numbers never appear on the leaderboard.
  • Joint-evidence gate (parlays / prop combos): a combo may only be ranked when a directly validated joint probability and the operator's void/push rules are both available. Multiplying marginal probabilities under dependence is invalid.
  • Proper-score leaderboard: singles, game parlays, same-game parlays and prop combos are separate divisions. Rank by benchmark-relative Brier skill with a game-cluster lower bound and a multiplicity adjustment for the number of trials — never raw win rate or season-long ROI.
  • Two-stage skill leaderboard: proper-score qualification first, then prospective CLV/ROI escrow.
  • Demotion gate: a live strategy that fails any hard gate (G4 CLV, G8 market integrity) is sent back to paper.
  • Signal vs staking: signal and staking are separated on the leaderboard so a lucky staking run cannot mask a bad signal.
Source policy: every row links to its gate or a flagged gap. No performance is claimed; no bets are placed. 1-800-GAMBLER.
