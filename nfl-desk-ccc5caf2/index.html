3. What is missing — and the decision that unblocks it

  • Operator price history (Q01/Q02, F01/F02). Without a timestamped closing price, no strategy can be certified and no CLV can be computed. Decision needed: capture operator snapshots weekly or accept that certification stays blocked.
  • Player-prop lines (F03). No official prop-line archive exists, so prop strategies cannot be priced economically. Decision needed: commercial odds archive or forward-collect from today.
  • Machine-readable official depth chart (F16). The NFL does not publish one; snap-share is an inference. Decision needed: accept snap-share inference labelled as such, or obtain club depth charts club-by-club.
  • Jurisdiction & reward funding (Q04/Q05). Rewards stay points-only until answered.
