Validation gates

Every strategy must pass all hard gates before leaderboard entry. A strategy that passes and goes live, then fails any hard gate, is demoted back to paper. Gates are applied with proper scores and uncertainty — never win rate or season-long ROI.

Leaderboard metricBrier skill score + uncertaintyNever raw win rate; never season-long ROI

A 55% season over 200 bets happens 25.1% of the time to a break-even bettor — win rate cannot distinguish skill from variance. That is why the leaderboard uses Brier skill with uncertainty, and why certification needs ~2,241 bets.

Why no strategy is certified yet

  • G4 and G8 cannot pass without a timestamped operator closing-price history. No official NFL or operator source publishes historical closing lines with timestamps (F01, F02).
  • G9 cannot pass without player-prop lines; no official prop-line archive exists (F03).
  • G6 is blocked until the owner sets bankroll, staking and drawdown tolerance (Q03).
