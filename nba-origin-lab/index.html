Executive decision

Do not publish a wager or claim skill. This lab defines how to originate a probability distribution and price from official NBA evidence, but the current sample does not meet the prospective validation, injury, settlement or market-quote gates. The 2025–26 ten-game sample is descriptive, selected and far too small to establish an edge.

Singles, cross-game parlays and same-game prop combinations each need separate joint-probability and settlement checks. A higher historical hit rate alone is not proof of skill: selection, multiple testing and variance remain.
