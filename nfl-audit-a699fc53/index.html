How the row check was done

  1. All eight pages of the live Week 10 report were read through the sandbox page fetcher (nfl.com cannot be reached with curl from the sandbox). The page text was transcribed row by row into data/official_injury_report_wk10_2026.csv.
  2. Club counts were tallied by hand before the transcription was compared with the published table. The build asserts that the counts match the tally club by club.
  3. The 49 rows the previous desk re-read on 2026-11-15 were compared against this read: 49 of 49 are identical. This is the cross-check on the transcription.
  4. Each published row was matched to the official row on club and player name, then compared on injury, practice status, game status and position. Result per row is on the Week 10 row check page.
  5. Transactions were read from the official trades and reserve-list pages for November 2026 and October 2026 (the October capture shows 10/24–10/31 only). See Official transactions.

What this desk cannot verify yet

  • No market prices, no game books, no official inactive lists, no snap counts. These are outside what was read here.
  • No dated depth-chart capture for any club in this session. Houston and Packers depth items are not checked.
  • Reserve-list history is only partly visible (see Flagged F05–F06). A "not listed" entry is not treated as "available".

Nothing on this site is a betting recommendation. The purpose is data quality: which published values match the official source, and which do not.
