A01 · freshness The published model-line slate is dated 2026-11-04 and the source snapshot is labeled 2026-11-03. The audit date is 2026-11-05, so those listed games are historical, not current actionable lines. Keep the snapshot clearly labeled historical; do not present it as a live slate. Refresh inputs and lines only after a dated official schedule and statistics snapshot is independently checked. NBA game calendar, Nov 4 · published lines
A02 · statistical The cross-game independence probe uses lag-1 points correlations for two players only: Luka Dončić, 63 pairs, r = −0.041, 95% CI [−0.285, 0.209]; Anthony Edwards, 60 pairs, r = 0.056, 95% CI [−0.200, 0.306]. These broad intervals include nontrivial positive and negative autocorrelation. A lag-1 statistic for two individual players does not establish independence of team-game legs or parlay returns. The correlations page and README also phrase the result as supporting / holding the independence assumption, which is stronger than these data warrant. Treat gate G05 as unverified, not passed, until game-leg joint outcomes are measured across a materially larger, pre-registered sample (with schedule, team, and temporal structure addressed). Avoid claiming cross-game independence from this probe. Luka official box scores · Edwards official box scores · computed correlations
A03 · statistical Pooled Pearson correlations and Fisher-z intervals use 125 game rows from only two players. The page reports per-player point estimates, but the pooled confidence intervals treat rows as independent and do not account for repeated observations within players or player-specific distributions. The 3-leg thresholds are also measured on the same small two-player sample. Keep results explicitly exploratory; do not carry the pooled interval or combo hit rates into pricing. Re-estimate with broader player coverage and uncertainty methods that respect clustering/serial dependence, then validate out of sample.
