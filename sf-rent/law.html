Different laws can apply simultaneously. This is a research summary, not legal advice. Verify current rules and the property’s actual classification before acting.

San Francisco annual increaseThe Rent Board sets the rate; eligible increases generally occur no more than once every 12 months. Use the rate effective when the increase takes effect.Official source ↗
2026–27 rate1.6%, March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027. A later effective date requires checking the newly published rate.Official source ↗
CoverageA pre-1979 building is a relevant indicator, not conclusive proof of coverage; exemptions and property history require review.Official source ↗
California AB 1482The Rent Board states its 8.8% San Francisco rate for August 1, 2026–July 31, 2027 does not apply to units under SF rent-increase limitations. Do not substitute it for the local annual rate.Official source ↗
Federal fair housingHUD identifies protected classes and prohibits retaliation for fair housing activity. Federal program-specific restrictions may also apply; no federal general rent cap is established by this source.Official source ↗
State fair housingCalifornia rules address protected classes and retaliation, including protected activity; neutral-looking decisions can still warrant legal scrutiny.Official source ↗
Roommate changesA landlord cannot raise rent simply because an additional roommate joins an existing tenancy. Last-original-occupant and successor issues are fact-specific; do not assume a reset.Official source ↗
