Executive summary

A cautious, tenant-respecting approach for two reported rent-controlled homes in the Outer Sunset. Research reviewed November 6, 2026.

Do not serve a notice yet. Coverage, lawful base rent, rent histories, tenancy continuity, lease restrictions and license status have not been independently verified. No lawyer has reviewed or approved a proposed increase.

For an eligible annual increase effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027, San Francisco’s published rate is 1.6% of lawful base rent. This is a ceiling for the annual component, not proof that either home is eligible. A smaller increase or no increase is an option. Banked increases require separate review.

The conservative business choice is to consider the same neutral policy for both units, while checking each tenancy independently. Different timing or amounts are not automatically discriminatory; equal treatment does not guarantee legal compliance. Seek independent San Francisco landlord–tenant counsel before service.
