The short version

WHAT IS TRUE, WHAT IS COMPUTED, WHAT IS MISSING
VERIFIED

What was read first-hand

The 2026 league standings (32 clubs), the Week-11 official injury report (253 rows across 24 clubs), the Week-11 schedule (13 games), all 14 Week-10 finals, the 2026 Schedule Release page, the Colts at Texans game centre, and the complete official Game Book for Washington at New York, which publishes the actual starters, the Not Active list and the game-day weather.

COMPUTED

What was derived

An opponent-adjusted rating model fitted on weeks 1-8 only, a fitted home field of 1.488 points, a residual sigma of 14.079 points, 39 originated lines across 13 games, 8 correlations with n and intervals, a 150-game corpus reconciled to the standings with 0 mismatches, and a two-split shadow backtest.

MISSING

What is blocked and why

No admitted price source, so no market residual, no closing-line value and no return. No verified depth order for the Houston Texans and no verified official snap-count source, so no replacement depth and no matchup matrix. No prospective game played, so the leaderboard carries no score.
