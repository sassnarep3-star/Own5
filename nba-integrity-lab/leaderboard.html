Proposed scoring and controls

Within each division, compare pre-event probabilistic forecasts with a synchronized, documented benchmark using a proper log score. Use an independently audited lower confidence bound with game-level clustering; require all gates and a separate replication before ranking. This is a proposal: no baseline quotes, confidence bounds or rankings are currently available.

Fix the strategy family and stake rules in advance, prevent duplicate entries and shared-data leakage, log voids and exclusions, and declare ties and reward eligibility before any competition begins. The offline prototype can check supplied attestations and rank eligible examples, but it cannot authenticate sources or produce a clustered confidence bound. No automated entry or payout service exists.
