Calculation, in plain language

For the total, average the teams’ published pace and blend each offense with the opposing defense. For the home margin, shrink each team’s published net rating toward zero using games / (games + 20), take their difference, and scale by half the average pace per 100 possessions. Home spread is the negative of that margin. Spread and total are separate crude baselines, not calibrated forecasts.

These ratings use a short early-season sample. Displayed NBA ratings are individually rounded; the exact underlying unrounded values are unavailable. No bookmaker comparison, uncertainty interval or player availability adjustment is supplied.
