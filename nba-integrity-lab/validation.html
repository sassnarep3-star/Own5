Assumptions and limits

The exact binomial example assumes independent bets, the same fixed success probability, a constant −110 price, no pushes, no commission and no selection after the fact. Actual prices and dependence usually vary; use a valid predeclared method and clustered uncertainty for the real ledger instead.

At n=2,243, the exact power in this specific one-candidate example is about 79.2%, not a guarantee of 80%. The older desk’s approximately 2,243 figure is a normal approximation. Statistical significance is not a guarantee of future profitability, and luck cannot be removed from outcomes.

The gate checker validates supplied evidence fields; it cannot independently authenticate a sportsbook quote or create missing data.
