Reading the comparison

In this sample, a lift greater than one means the observed joint rate is above the product of its sample marginals. Multiplying those marginals therefore understates the observed joint rate for that particular comparison; it does not establish the direction in other samples. A sportsbook’s actual combined quote is still needed.

The previously published claim that near-zero lag-1 player autocorrelation establishes cross-game parlay independence is not supported. The actual wager-leg correlation remains blank until matched wager outcomes are available.
