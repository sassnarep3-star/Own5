Signal design

Promising inputs to investigate include pace, offensive and defensive efficiency, expected minutes, injuries, rest and market movement. Each candidate must be frozen before a future test, use only information available before the event, and be compared against a transparent baseline.

A parlay needs the probability and settlement of the whole ticket, not just promising-looking legs. Shared game conditions and correlated outcomes must be modeled or empirically tested.
