The eight requirements, and what each produced

  • Price-snapshot store. 390 rows — one per market per snapshot type across 78 originated markets. 156 carry a value; 234 are empty and carry a flag id. The slots exist for the desk's own open and close and for the operator's open and close; only the ones we can source are populated.
  • Verified results feed. 137 games for 2026, transcribed from the official week-by-week schedule pages. Rebuilding every club's win-loss-tie, points for and points against from those 137 games reproduces the official standings page with 0 mismatches across 32 clubs. The pinned mirror agrees on 136 of them and lags on 1.
  • Origination order. Every game records three steps in order: side, total, moneyline. The moneyline row states that it is derived from the side and the total, never fitted separately.
  • Three columns. model_line, market_line and difference_model_minus_market, for all three market types. market_line is labelled AGGREGATOR because no official price exists.
  • Signal versus staking. Two ranks per family from disjoint inputs. Today's clearest case: a strategy that is first on realised money from four bets sits fifth of eight on signal.
  • Depth of replacement. 705 published position slots across 31 clubs; 7 are being filled by a backup, each naming which string steps in, whether that man is himself on the injury report, and whether the loss compounds to a third name.
  • Correlations. 9 pairs, every one computed from official numbers, each published with r, p, a 95% interval and n. 7 values previously published elsewhere in this repo are moved to a withheld register because their inputs were not official.
  • Demotion gate. Six triggers, evaluated against every family. All are armed; none has fired, because nothing has ever been live.

What the model can and cannot do

Out of sample on 2026 weeks 5-10 (73 games), the side model's margin forecast correlates with the actual margin at r = 0.3187 (p = 0.0060, n = 73) and calls the straight-up winner 49 of 72 (0.6806). Its margin error is 10.0242 points against 10.8082 for the naive "predict a tie" baseline, so the side model is worth about 0.78 points a game.

The totals model is the weak half: 11.0038 points of error against 11.0235 for simply predicting the league average every time. It adds nothing yet, and because the moneyline is derived from the side and the total, that weakness flows into the moneyline. Fixing it is suggestion S-02.

Why nothing is certified

Two gates do the work. G2 requires every graded bet to link to a verified opening and closing price snapshot; there is no official publisher of NFL prices, so G2 fails for every family. G8 requires the certification sample to come from an official results feed; the official feed holds one season, so the certification board gives each family between 4 and 51 graded bets against a 300-bet minimum. On the larger mirror sample the best family hits against a break-even, which is below the price it would have to beat.

That is the honest output, not a failure to find one: on the evidence available, no family here has demonstrated an edge.
