Every rule below, and its parameters, was written down before any result was inspected. Graded walk-forward: parameters are always fitted on periods strictly earlier than the period being graded. Two universes are shown. The certification universe is the official results feed: small, but it is the only one that can ever certify anything. The research universe is the pinned mirror from 1999 to 2025: thousands of bets, useful for asking whether a signal exists at all, but it cannot certify or reward anything.

Bets are graded at a flat -110, a 52.38% break-even, because the mirror carries no per-leg prices for 2026 (F-07).

Reading the results honestly

  • The moneyline family (S3) is significantly worse than break-even, not merely inconclusive. That is a real finding about the rule, and the signal score penalises it rather than rewarding the strength of the evidence.
  • The research board's largest samples land around 51% against a 52.38% break-even. That is the shape of a market that has already priced the signal.
  • The same-game parlay needs 27.44% and delivered 26.32% over 1,953 bets. Independent leg pricing is not free money; it is an assumption that costs about 4% of turnover here.
  • Prop combinations (S9) produced no graded bets at all, because no prop lines exist from any source in this repo. The row is kept deliberately empty rather than modelled (F-08, S-09).
