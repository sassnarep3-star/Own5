Tracking the depth of the replacement, not the fact of one

Knowing that a starter is out is the easy half. What matters is who replaces him and how far down the chart that is. This tracker answers, for every published position slot on every club:

  • which string is actually expected to start — 2nd, 3rd or 4th;
  • whether that replacement is himself carrying a designation on the official injury report, so a loss compounds into a second;
  • whether the loss therefore cascades to a third name, and whether a third name is even published;
  • how many rungs down the chart the club has been forced to go.

Offence and defence are tracked as separate rows with their own unit column, so a defence playing its third-string cornerback is never pooled with an offence starting its backup left tackle.
