What this desk is

This session was asked for eight specific things: a price-snapshot store that records opening and closing numbers for every market we originate; a verified results feed so backtests and ROI can be computed at all; an origination order of side → total → moneyline; model, market and difference as three separate columns; a leaderboard that scores signal and staking separately; the depth of a replacement rather than the fact of one; correlations from officially sourced numbers only, each with its n; and a demotion gate that sends a live strategy back to paper. All eight are built and published here.

The headline finding is unchanged from the honest position of every desk before it: nfl.com publishes no betting prices. That single fact is why the price store's opening and closing rows are empty, why gate G2 fails for every strategy, and why nothing on the board is certified. The store now records exactly where the gap is, instead of leaving it implicit.
