Price snapshot store

Opening and closing numbers for every market we originate, with empty slots labelled rather than filled.

What the store records

One row per market per snapshot type. There are 78 markets (side, total and moneyline for each of 26 scheduled games), so 390 rows in total. 156 carry a value. The other 234 are empty, and each empty row names the flag that explains it. The store never fills a cell to make a table look complete.

MODEL_ORIGIN
the price this desk first originates, with a UTC stamp
MODEL_CLOSE
the desk's last price before kickoff — empty until a second run (F-03)
MARKET_OPEN
the operator's opening price — empty, no official publisher exists (F-01)
MARKET_CLOSE
the operator's closing price — empty, same reason (F-01)
MARKET_REFERENCE
an aggregator line of unknown timestamp, kept for context and never called an opening or a closing price (F-14)
