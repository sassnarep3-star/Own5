The demotion gate (D1)

A strategy that has been promoted to live is returned to paper the moment any trigger fires. Demotion is automatic: it does not wait for a review and it cannot be overridden by a good week. Re-promotion needs a fresh pass through G0–G8 on bets logged after the demotion.

D1a
Rolling 200-bet closing-line value at or below zero
D1b
Drawdown from peak of 20% or more of the reference bankroll
D1c
The last 100 graded bets fail to beat break-even at p &lt; 0.10 one-sided
D1d
Any parameter or rule changed from the pre-registered commit
D1e
Any graded bet without a verified opening or closing snapshot
D1f
The official results feed falls more than a week behind

Current state: armed. Nothing has ever been promoted to live, so no trigger has fired and no family is eligible for demotion. The table below evaluates each trigger against every family anyway, so the day a promotion happens the gate is already wired.
