Origination order: side, then total, then moneyline

The order is not cosmetic. The side is priced from a margin model; the total from a separate totals model; and the moneyline is then derived from the two of them as P(home win) = Φ(side / σ), with σ = 11.106 points estimated from out-of-sample residuals. The moneyline therefore cannot disagree with the side and total it came from, and every row records that dependency.

All three steps are fitted on the 137 official 2026 results only. Home-field advantage comes out at 0.213 points, which is not separable from zero on this sample — flag F-11.

Model line, market line, difference

model_line is ours. market_line is the aggregator's untimestamped line, labelled AGGREGATOR throughout (F-14). difference is model minus market. For the moneyline the columns are probabilities, and the market figure is de-vigged.
