Demotion gate (S-DG01, active from the first live strategy)

A live strategy returns to paper when either trigger fires: (a) the game-cluster 95% lower bound of its Brier skill vs benchmark is below 0 over the most recent 50 scored games, or (b) three consecutive losing weeks (net − units over any rolling 3 weeks). Hard stop: any single week below −5 units. Both triggers are proper-score/uncertainty based; they cannot be gamed by staking because staking is scored separately.
