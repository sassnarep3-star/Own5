Validation gates

What a strategy must pass, and what sends it back to paper.

Built 2026-11-19
Paper research

Gates are fail-closed: a strategy that has not met every gate is on paper. The demotion rules are the other half — a live strategy that loses its edge is sent back to paper on a stated trigger, not on a hunch.
