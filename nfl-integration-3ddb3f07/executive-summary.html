Executive summary

What is established, what is computed, what is missing, and what must be decided.

Built 2026-11-19
Paper research

1 · Where the project stands

The repository originates NFL lines, tracks official availability, computes correlations from official inputs only and ranks strategies behind fail-closed gates. It has 78 requirements in the canonical register: 46 met, 21 partial, 9 not met. No strategy is certified.

The corpus holds 145 settled games read from the official schedule pages. The desk that owns them reconciles all 32 clubs against the official standings.

2 · What is published, and what is deliberately blank

  • Origination. 39 markets are originated in the required order — side first, then the total, then the moneyline — each with the model's fair line, the market line and the difference in three separate columns. The difference is published against an aggregator reference, which is labelled as such and is not an operator quote.
  • Prices. 0 of 195 price snapshots are verified. No operator price is admitted, so market residual, closing-line value and real-price ROI are blank by policy (FLAG-P1, question Q-01, suggestion SG-01).
  • Depth. 11 of 12 club depth charts were parsed from the clubs' own published pages, with the raw text and its hash kept. The depth of the replacement is recorded per replacement: 2nd string (14).
  • Correlations. 5 coefficients are published from official inputs, each with its sample size and definition; the pairs that would need a non-official input are withheld and listed as withheld.

3 · Removing luck from the ranking

2 prospective panels are published with the observed hit rate, the interval implied by the model's own probabilities, the Brier score against a coin flip and the cluster-bootstrapped skill interval. 7 of 7 strategy families are on paper. 6 of 12 gates are met across the catalogue; a strategy needs every one of them, plus a price, before it can be ranked.

Control baselines (random picks, favourites only) are graded on the same games, so a lucky staking run cannot be mistaken for a good signal.

4 · What blocks a decision today

  • No admitted price source — blocks market residual, closing-line value, ROI and every price-relative score.
  • Availability is a snapshot, not a series — the injury report is read and captured with a clock time and a hash, but a full Wednesday-to-kickoff series for every club is not yet retained.
  • Depth is not universal — the clubs whose depth chart is an image cannot be parsed, and their rows are blank rather than guessed.
  • One season — holdout and ablation studies need more history than a single 2026 season.

All 34 flagged items (9 blocking) are published on Flagged for Review with their sources; the open questions are on Questions and the reviewable options are on Suggestions.

5 · How this site is built

Every table is read at build time from the desk that owns it, and the file that was read is recorded with its sha256 on the verification report. Every row carries an official source link or an explicit unverified marker; a value that cannot be confirmed is printed blank. The repository is re-verified end to end on every build: the desk directory lists every desk, and each desk's own verifier is re-run and its result published.
