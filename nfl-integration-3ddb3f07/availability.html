Injuries & depth

Who is out, who replaces them, and how deep the replacement really is.

Built 2026-11-19
Paper research

Availability is read from the official NFL injury report and kept with the clock time of the read. Two rules matter for anything downstream:

  • An injury designation is a surprise, not a level — what matters is the change between reads, and the delta table on the capture ledger is what gets published.
  • Depth of a replacement is measured by playing time on the club's own published depth chart, never by jersey order, experience or salary. A slot shared by two players, or left empty, is recorded as shared or empty rather than invented.

Replacement columns are blank where the club publishes its depth chart as an image: the blank is the evidence, and the flag says so.
