Originated lines

Side, then total, then moneyline — with the fair line, the market line and the difference apart.

Built 2026-11-19
Paper research

Lines are originated in one order: the side first, then the total, then the moneyline, with each later step derived from the step before it. The model's fair line, the market line and the difference are published as three separate columns, so a reader can see the size of the gap and its direction without doing arithmetic.

The market line here is an aggregator reference of unknown timestamp, kept for context and labelled as such. It is not an operator quote, so no residual, closing-line value or ROI is computed from it. The price policy is on the record and undecided.
