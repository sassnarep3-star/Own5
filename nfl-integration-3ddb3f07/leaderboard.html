Leaderboard & strategies

Signal separated from staking, gated, pre-registered, and controlled.

Built 2026-11-19
Paper research

Signal and staking are scored separately. A signal score is a proper score against the model, never a return; a staking figure is only published when a real price exists. A family cannot be certified on a staking run, and the rank gap column shows where the two orders disagree.

Control baselines are graded on the same games, so a lucky run is visible as a lucky run.
