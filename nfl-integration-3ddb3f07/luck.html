Removal of luck

Proper scores, clustered intervals, and an ablation instead of a fixed penalty.

Built 2026-11-19
Paper research

Luck is bounded, not removed. For every prospective panel the site publishes the observed hit rate, the interval implied by the model's own probabilities, the Brier score against a coin flip and the game-clustered bootstrap interval of the skill. A hit rate that sits inside its own interval is not evidence of skill, however good it looks.

The ablation compares the same model with and without the personnel features instead of subtracting a fixed number of points for a missing starter, so the effect of availability is measured with its uncertainty rather than assumed.
