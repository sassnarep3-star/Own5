Executive summary — NFL Origin Desk

What this desk is. A research workspace for originating NFL lines and running a leaderboard competition in which strategies must pass validation gates before being ranked. It is additive to the existing session desks in this repo (root NFL Research Desk, NFL Strategy Lab, NFL Line Lab, NBA labs and several NFL decision desks); it touches no file owned by another session.
