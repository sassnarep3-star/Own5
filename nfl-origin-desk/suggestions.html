S01Archive a timestamped operator price snapshot every week
Problem
Every CLV and "market vs model" statement rests on an aggregator line whose timestamp is unknown. Without a price history, gate G4 can never be evaluated and no strategy can be certified as skill.
Recommendation
Capture the operator's own NFL page (spread, total, moneyline) at two fixed times per week (Wednesday post-injury-report and Sunday pre-kickoff) and store the raw snapshot with HTTP headers and hash.

S11Price correlated legs with an empirical copula, and refuse independent pricing
Problem
Same-team QB + WR1 legs are correlated (r = 0.50 on 2024-2026); at median lines the joint hit rate is 32.5% versus 24.8% if treated as independent, a +7.7 pp gap that changes the fair price materially.
Recommendation
Require an empirical joint estimate for any same-game combination; publish the implied fair price next to the offered price and reject the bet when the offered price assumes independence.
