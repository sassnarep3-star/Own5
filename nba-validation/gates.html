Separating evidence of skill from luck

Retain every attempted strategy, including failures. Tune only inside chronological training windows; keep same-game legs together. Assess forecast quality against simple baselines before profitability. Correct the primary test family, preserve dependence when estimating uncertainty, and demand replication. Closing-line comparison is diagnostic, not proof of profit. Passing statistical tests cannot eliminate chance or guarantee future returns.
