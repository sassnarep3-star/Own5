Pricing constraints

For two binary events, max(0, pA+pB−1) ≤ pAB ≤ min(pA,pB). With nondegenerate marginals, pAB = pA·pB + phi·sqrt(pA(1−pA)pB(1−pB)). The engine rejects infeasible inputs. Do not substitute raw-stat Pearson r for binary phi. No independence assumption is certified here.
