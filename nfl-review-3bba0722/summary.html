BLOCKERS

Do not infer these values

  • No current operator opening/closing price was captured in this hub; price-dependent residual, CLV and ROI fields remain blank.
  • No complete, immutable, multi-day injury/depth/inactive history or 32-club parser certification was built here.
  • No official snap-count source was confirmed by this review; a failed URL is not proof that none exists.
  • The Game Book use restriction is not cleared for automated retention, modeling or public display.
  • No strategy is certified by this hub; backtests, win rate and staking outcomes are not evidence of prospective skill.

RECOMMENDED ORDER

Build the evidence chain first

  1. Resolve source rights and data policy first, especially raw Game Book and operator price data.
  2. Automate dated, hashed official source captures and fail closed on wrong-week pages, missing fields or stale reads.
  3. Complete the 32-club depth-source sweep while preserving raw cells, grouped/empty slots and the distinction between unofficial depth expectations and actual game evidence.
  4. Build the expected-versus-actual availability graph from dated club sources, official Game Books/inactives and reserve-list events; keep snap counts separate until an official source is verified.
  5. Pre-register a singles-only shadow baseline and a chronological train/validation/final holdout; publish each probability with its inputs and keep signal scoring separate from staking.
  6. Keep parlays and prop combos blocked until direct joint probabilities, operator prices and the applicable void/push rules are validated.
  7. Only then add venue/weather context, publish weekly diffs and show a removal-of-luck panel with sample sizes and uncertainty.
