Local law — the San Francisco Rent Ordinance

SF’s Rent Ordinance (codified at Admin. Code Ch. 37) governs both units: it caps annual increases, controls just-cause evictions, and defines the only lawful routes above the annual amount.

The mechanics — every step is mandatory

How an allowable increase actually works (sf.gov “Learn about rent increases”, “Banked rent increases”)
StepRuleSource
1. TimingFirst increase only after 12 months of tenancy; thereafter once every 12 months; later effective date = new anniversary date.sf.gov ↗
2. Base rent onlyIncrease computed on base rent (incl. housing services such as parking), excluding temporary passthroughs/fluctuating charges. No rounding up.sf.gov ↗
3. Rent increase licenseSince 2022-07-01 (2023-03-01 for condos and buildings with 1–9 units), annual/banked increases require a license generated by the Housing Inventory report; imposing one without a license is null and void (§37.15).sf.gov ↗ · Housing Inventory FAQ ↗
4. Notice contentsDollar amount; percentage; effective date; banked portion (if any).sf.gov ↗
5. Notice period30 days if the combined increase in any 12-month window is ≤ 10%; 90 days if > 10%; +5 days if mailed (CCP §1013).sf.gov ↗ · CC §827 ↗
6. BankingSkipped allowable increases may be saved and imposed later together with the current annual increase. Banked amounts add (never compound, are never prorated) and multiply the current base rent; no cap on the amount banked; does not transfer to a new tenancy; new owner may use prior owner’s valid banked amounts; effective only with a license.sf.gov banking ↗
7. Banking eligibility (!!)Official texts conflict. “A skipped increase is only banked if at least 24 full months pass without any rent increase” (banked page) vs worked examples banking the prior two skipped increases at the next anniversary while the third (12 months old) is lost (learn page). See F-02.banked ↗ · learn ↗
8. Above the allowable rateOnly via Rent Board petition & certification (capital improvements / rehab / energy / renewables / operating & maintenance). OM-portion increases capped at 7% (§37.3(c)(2)). Capital passthroughs: 100% of certified cost, amortized at the rate in effect when filed (2026-03-01→2027-02-28: 4.1%/7y, 4.3%/10y, 4.6%/15y, 4.8%/20y), excluded from base rent, must be discontinued at the end of amortization; hardship relief available; petition must be filed BEFORE the notice; no charge for pre-tenancy work.sf.gov capital ↗ · §37.7 ↗ · imputed rates ↗ · Ordinance §37.3 ↗
9. Security deposit interestRequired on deposits held ≥ 1 year: 2026-03-01→2027-02-28 = 4.2%. Not part of the increase itself; flagged out of scope F-11.sf.gov rates ↗
10. Additional occupantsNo additional rent may be charged merely because a roommate moved in (or left). Surcharge clauses are unenforceable.sf.gov ↗

Why this defines “reasonable and normal”

The Rent Board describes its mission as “assuring landlords fair and adequate rents” — the City’s definition of fair is the annual allowable increase and certified passthroughs, not the open market. Anything beyond that is only lawful at a genuine vacancy (Costa-Hawkins), by petition, or void. sf.gov — Rent Board ↗ · CC §1954.53 ↗
