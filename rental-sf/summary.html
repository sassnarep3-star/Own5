Bottom line

  1. Do not reprice to market. Both tenancies are continuing; market is only for new tenancies (Costa-Hawkins vacancy decontrol), CC §1954.53 ↗.
  2. Reasonable & normal = the City’s CPI figure (1.6%) + properly banked years, once per 12 months, with license and proper notice. This is what the Rent Ordinance defines as fair (sf.gov ↗; Rent Ordinance §37.3 ↗).
  3. Above that, only Rent Board-certified passthroughs (capital improvements / operating & maintenance) — never voluntary, never in base rent, never without a petition before the notice. sf.gov capital improvements ↗
  4. Unit 1: serve a clean 1.6% notice ($63.92) if anything at all; chasing the skipped 1.4% riskily moves the anniversary and loses money. Scenarios ↗
  5. Unit 2: decide banking doctrine with the counselor first; the difference between the two official readings is $53.55–$148.20/month. F-02
  6. One vs both units: apply the identical lawful formula to both; separate, person-specific logic is what triggers discrimination and retaliation inquiries. Full analysis ↗
