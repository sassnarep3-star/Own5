What the numbers mean

  • Groups — the number of position rows the club prints for offense and defense. Special teams rows are not transcribed.
  • Names — the number of player names printed across those groups.
  • Shared slots — slots where the club prints more than one name.
  • Empty slots — slots the club prints with no name. They are preserved, not closed up.
