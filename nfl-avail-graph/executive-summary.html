Executive summary

What was delivered

An official-source availability ledger for the 2026 Week 10 slate, published with every value linked to the page it was read from, and every unverifiable value left blank and flagged rather than filled.

  • Results and venues for all 14 fixtures from the official game centres, including the official Location and Stadium fields and the official Game Book link for each game.
  • Official depth coverage for all 32 clubs — every club's own depth page read in one namespace, 31 of them parsing into rows. The Texans publish an image and are deliberately not transcribed by eye.
  • An availability graph of 704 groups joining the official charts, the official injury report and the league's official announcements, with the expected starter stated and the actual starter left blank.
  • 55 replacement rows for every Out designation that appears in a chart, each naming the next available name in the same official group and its depth string.
  • An injury capture of 347 official rows with 171 designations, plus the 6-field delta against the committed 2026-11-15 read and an explicit audit of the 198 rows that the comparison normalises away.
  • The official inactive list for all 14 fixtures (172 players). A player on it did not play, so every chart name on it is excluded from the expected-starter calculation.
  • 64 official availability events with a publication date, an official source URL and an evidence note on every row.
  • The official Week 11 slate — 13 fixtures with the game centre's own location and stadium fields, and 6 official byes.
  • 24 flags, of which 2 are blocking.

The two decisions that mattered

  1. Depth is a group, never a single starter. Several clubs print shared slots (more than one name in the same slot) and several print empty slots. Both are preserved exactly as printed. Presenting a shared slot as one starter would fabricate a starter the club never named.
  2. An unconfirmable value stays blank. Two official game centres hide their scores and the official Game Books could not be opened. The scores were taken from the official by-week schedule page instead, and every actual-starter cell is blank with the reason and the PDF link. No cell anywhere on this site was filled by inference.
  3. A diff that normalises is not a complete diff. Comparing two dated reads of the official injury page requires two normalisations: an empty cell and the literal token BLANK mean the same thing, and the comma separating two injury names is canonicalised. Both are published row by row in the normalisation audit, so a reader can see exactly which rows the six-row delta is silent about and why.

How the desk should be used

Read it as a dated snapshot, not as a live board: the capture stamp is 2026-11-18 and the injury report on it is the Week 10 report. The delta table is the part that carries information — what changed between two dated reads of the same official page — because the absolute designation level is already public. Nothing here is a model output, a price or a backtest number, and nothing on this site has been used to select a bet.
