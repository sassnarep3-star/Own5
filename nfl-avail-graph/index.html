What this desk is

This desk publishes the official, point-in-time record of who was available and who was expected to start for the 2026 NFL Week 10 slate, together with the official results and venues for the same 14 fixtures. Every row on every page carries a link to the official page it was read from. Where an official source does not print a value the cell is left blank and flagged — nothing on this site is inferred, and no depth order is modelled from jersey number, salary, experience or snap count.

What is deliberately blank

Three things this desk could not obtain, each published with the reason and the official link that would close it:

  • Actual starters. The official NFL Game Book PDF could not be read in this environment (static.www.nfl.com refuses a TLS handshake). Every availability row therefore shows an empty actual-starter cell, the reason in words, and the official PDF link. Flagged →
  • Houston Texans depth. The club publishes its depth chart as a single image. Transcribing it by eye would create an inferred depth order, so the Texans have no depth rows and their replacement cells are blank and flagged.
  • The Week 11 league injury report. The league's own Week 11 URL still serves the Week 10 report, so no Week 11 designation is published here.

How to read a depth group

A depth chart row is a group, not a single starter. The club prints slots in order; a slot may hold more than one name (a shared slot, printed with a semicolon) and may be empty. Both are preserved exactly as printed, and the position family is only ever used as a join key between two official records — never as a depth model.
