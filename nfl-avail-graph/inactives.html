Official inactive lists — all 14 Week 10 fixtures

The NFL publishes each club's game-day inactive list. A player on it did not play, so the club's own depth chart cannot have started him. This is the official record that the unreadable Game Book would otherwise supply, and it is joined into the availability graph: every chart name that appears here is excluded from the expected-starter calculation, and the affected row says so.

These are the clubs' own declarations, published by the league. A player can be inactive without being injured (a coaching decision) and a player can be active while carrying a designation, so an inactive list and an injury report are not the same thing and both are published.
