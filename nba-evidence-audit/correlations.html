Exploratory correlations

A deliberately narrow, independently checked example: Anthony Edwards' first 11 listed games of the 2025–26 regular season, from the official filtered NBA game log. These are in-sample arithmetic, not official NBA estimates or validated parlay prices.

Only 11 games, one player, and a nonrandom date window. Thresholds are illustrative choices made for this retrospective audit; no holdout, odds, uncertainty interval, or generalization is established. A near-zero correlation never proves independence.
