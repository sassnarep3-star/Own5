F01HighHome-margin sign mismatchThe formula and raw JSON use positive for an expected home win. The original model page calls the same value a spread and says negative means home favorite. Read values as expected home margin; the conventional home spread is its negative. Do not place a bet from that label.official source · reviewed artifact
F02HighParlay arithmetic reversedThe original executive summary says the independence product overstates the joint probability when observed lift exceeds one. For the same sample, joint = lift × product, so lift above one means the product understates the observed joint probability. No prospective edge follows.official source · reviewed artifact
