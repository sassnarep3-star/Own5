6 · Depth of the replacement

How far down the published chart a club has to reach, not whether it had to reach at all.

The inherited metric this replaces

705 chart slots were published with a depth field that took the value 0 for 698 slots and 1 for 7. It could not express a second absence, could not separate a backup from a fourth string, and treated an injured fourth receiver exactly like an injured starting quarterback.

Three exposed slots is not a league-wide measurement

Four of 32 club charts were read for this session, so only 3 slots are exposed. The metric is correct and the sample is far too small to rank clubs. That is recorded as F-05 and as suggestion S04.
