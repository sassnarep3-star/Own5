The bye-week problem

A published correlation that is mostly a schedule artefact

Rest differential vs home margin is published elsewhere in this repository at r = −0.4257 with n = 121. Sixteen of those games involve a team coming off a bye, and byes are scheduled, not random. Removing only those games gives the table below: the coefficient collapses to +0.04.
