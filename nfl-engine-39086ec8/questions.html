The one gap that cannot be closed

Q02: the Week 10 opening prices are gone. No public source will publish what a book opened at, and reconstructing it from a later price would be invention. The only fix is forward-looking — start capturing now.

Two owner decisions gate everything downstream

Q04 (are rewards points or cash) and Q05 (bankroll and tolerable drawdown) cannot be researched. They are the owner's call, and until they are answered the demotion rule D3 has no threshold and the leaderboard cannot be launched.
