1 · Price-snapshot store

Opening and closing numbers for every market we originate, with enough provenance to make each one checkable. Append-only: a number is never edited, and a disagreeing re-capture is reported.

What the store contains today

13 games × 6 markets = 78 snapshots, all at one capture point (2026-11-14), stored as event=close under the definition “the last capture before kickoff”. The event=open slice is empty for Week 10 and cannot be filled: no capture was made when the market opened. WAS @ NYG is absent — it kicked off on 2026-11-12.

The price page does not publish which state's rules apply to the served numbers; its footer carried New Jersey responsible-gaming assets. The store records source_region as US-UNSPECIFIED for every row and this is flagged (F-08) because settlement rules and void rights are state-specific.

Prop market NAMES render (e.g. 'Patrick Mahomes - Passing Yds') but no line or price does. Prop combos therefore cannot be priced from this source and are excluded from any ROI calculation rather than modelled at assumed lines.

Data captured 2026-11-14 (UTC). Official sources only. Anything a source does not publish is shown as blank and listed on Flagged for review.

Results verified against nfl.com standings. Prices are a single capture point per game: there is no opening price, so nothing here is a closing-line value.
