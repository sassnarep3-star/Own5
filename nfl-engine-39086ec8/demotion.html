Each scenario was built so exactly one rule fires, which is how a gate is proven to be wired to the right evidence. None of them is a claim about any strategy.

Two rules cannot run yet

D2 (CLV reversal) and D4 (price evidence unavailable) both need price history. They are implemented and demonstrated, and they are inert until a second capture point exists. Suggestion S08 recommends adopting D1, D3 and D5 now and enabling D2 and D4 when the history exists.

Why an SPRT rather than a fixed sample

“Wait for a bigger sample” is how a losing strategy stays alive. The sequential probability ratio test asks, after every graded bet, whether the record is still consistent with a break-even strategy. When the cumulative log-likelihood ratio crosses the lower boundary the answer is no, and the strategy is demoted without waiting for a sample target that can always be raised.
