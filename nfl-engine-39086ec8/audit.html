Notes on the irregularities

B2 — Club codes used for joins. This is the defect that made this session's own build fail until a canonical map (official.CLUB_ALIASES) was added. Any desk joining the ledger to nfl.com by raw code is silently losing two teams.

B6 — C-03 is interpreted as 'is a rest advantage a real signal?'. The published r is dominated by bye weeks. A 16-game sub-sample with clear selection (byes are scheduled, not random) is being read as a rest gradient.

B7 — C-03 source is nfl.com standings. The link resolves, but it is the wrong page for the quantity. C-05 to C-09 share the same blanket source.

B10 — Own lines cover the Week 10 slate. The Thursday final was excluded, leaving one game of the slate unoriginated without an explicit note.

B11 — Market columns are blank because no official price exists. The conclusion 'no official market price exists' is too strong. What is missing is an OPENING price and any history; a current price is obtainable and is now stored.

C5 — The recorded quotes match the operator today. Not an error by that desk: the market moved. It is evidence that a DATE-ONLY timestamp is not enough — the same numbers mean different things 48 hours apart. This is the core argument for the price store's required captured_at_utc.

D2 — `deficiency_points` measures how far down the chart a replacement is. The column is a near-boolean. It cannot rank a game, separate a 1-rung from a 2-rung loss, or see a cascading absence. Requirement 6 of this brief is therefore not met by the inherited data even though the underlying charts support it.

A withdrawn finding is left in the table

C6 flagged a spread sign error, then C7 disproved it by checking the sign against the moneyline on the same row. The row stays, marked NOT CHECKED, because a finding that is deleted leaves no evidence that it was ever considered.
