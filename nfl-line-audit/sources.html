Data policy P-01

Decided before any backtest was published; the full text is in the registration file.

  • No row-level official NFL data is republished on this site. The site publishes derived aggregates and links to the official page.
  • Raw official bytes are hashed and counted but not committed in this namespace.
  • Operator prices are stored with the operator name, the printed price, the read clock and the page URL, and are attributed to that operator.
  • Aggregator data is labelled PINNED-DATASET or AGGREGATOR on every row and never described as official.

The basis is NFL.com Terms and Conditions s.1.3 (read 2026-11-19): individual non-commercial informational use only, and systematic retrieval prohibited without written consent.
