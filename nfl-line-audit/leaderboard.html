How a strategy gets on this page

  1. Its definition is frozen in a registration commit before any evaluation. The commit hash is the registration.
  2. It is forecast pre-kickoff and settles against the operator price recorded at capture time.
  3. It is ranked by benchmark-relative Brier skill with a game-cluster interval, then Holm-adjusted across families.
  4. Staking is a separate column. A lucky staking run cannot lift a bad signal.
  5. It returns to paper automatically if the skill lower bound drops below zero, if ROI over the last 50 bets is at or below -10%, or if its inputs change without a new registration.
