The one positive number, and why it is not published as a strategy

Betting every over at the closing total returns +0.33% per unit across 741 decided games. The reason is in the bias row: the closing total sits about 1.26 points below the total actually scored (cluster interval 0.40 to 2.05). That is a market-level effect, not a modelling edge, and it shrinks each season.

The screen below asks where the effect lives. It found no concentration by total size, so it is a league-wide level shift rather than a subset effect. Because this screen looked at the holdout, the holdout is no longer a holdout for this hypothesis.
