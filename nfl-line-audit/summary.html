Executive summary

The desk does what the brief asks: it originates its own NFL lines, records them against named operator prices, tracks official availability with clocks, ranks strategies on proper scores and publishes a decision-ready register. It also reports the outcome honestly: the line beats the model.

Origination

  • 13 Week 11 fixtures, three markets in the required order, 52 market rows.
  • Fair, market and difference in separate columns; market cells carry the median of DraftKings and FanDuel, de-vigged where needed.
  • Every row stores a hash of the exact input object; the verifier recomputes it.
  • Model: opponent-aware points per drive, recency-weighted, home field +0.31 points (indistinguishable from zero).

Results and corpus

  • 952 usable games, 32/32 clubs reconciling to the official standings on W-L-T, PF and PA.
  • 14 Week 10 finals appended from the official page because the pinned snapshot stops at week 9.
  • The repo's 136-game log: 117 agree, 16 are blank table rows, 3 fixtures are absent. It cannot be a corpus for 19 rows.

Skill

  • Model Brier skill against the closing line: -7.8% over 744 walk-forward games.
  • No pre-registered family certifies; two are significantly worse than the market after correction.
  • Bets needed to separate a 55% bettor from break-even at -110: 359.
  • Zero strategies on the leaderboard because zero bets have settled.

Availability

  • Official Week 11 report read today: 253 rows, 24 clubs; CIN and WAS print no injuries.
  • 225 player-level changes against the 18 Nov read, both clocks on every row.
  • Questionable plays 25.0% of the time (n=4,970). It is not a probability and is never a points penalty.
  • Depth comes from playing time: 12 position/rank rows with intervals, no jersey numbers anywhere.

Governance

  • Pre-registration committed before evaluation; the commit hash is the registration and the verifier recomputes it.
  • Backtest numbers are forbidden on the leaderboard by rule and by build.
  • Demotion gate: three automatic triggers, published before use.
  • 18 flagged items with evidence links, including three defects this desk found in its own pipeline.

What to decide next

  • Whether to keep chasing the closing line with public data, or to reposition the model as a residual generator (S03).
  • Whether to seek written consent for automated capture, which unlocks the whole availability series (Q02, S01).
  • Whether official inactives are the next data purchase (Q03, S02).
  • Whether usage rank replaces the depth-chart idea permanently (S06).
