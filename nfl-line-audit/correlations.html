What the correlations decide

  • Same-game parlay legs are dependent. QB passing yards and the team's top-WR receiving yards move together at r=0.513 over 1,523 team-games. Multiplying marginal probabilities would overstate the joint and is forbidden.
  • Side and total are nearly independent. Home margin and game total correlate at 0.019 over 952 games, so a same-game side+total combination is admissible only with a joint that respects that near-independence.
  • Scoring and winning move together but not identically (PF/G vs win pct 0.716, 95% interval 0.505–0.863, n=32 clubs). A points-based rating and a win-based rating will disagree, which is why the model does not use win percentage at all.
