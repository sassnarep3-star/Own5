Why the benchmark is 0.25 and not the market's own Brier

A side bet at a closing line is 50/50 by construction: the line is the market's point forecast, so a forecast that takes the line scores a Brier of 0.25. Anything a model adds has to beat that. The moneyline, being a two-way price, is benchmarked against the de-vigged close instead, because a 50/50 coin is not the right comparator for a priced market.

A skill above +0.05 is treated as a defect. An earlier build reported +0.30 and the cause was a leak: the "model" probability was centred on the closing line itself. The build now refuses to publish a side skill above +0.05 and the test suite fails if one appears.
