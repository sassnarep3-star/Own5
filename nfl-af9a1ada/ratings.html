Three defects this table caught, all now tested. (1) A fit with a plus-signed defence returned a "defence" that correlated positively with points allowed (+0.76) — it was a second offence wearing a different name. (2) Eliminating one club by substitution to pin the sum-to-zero invariant parked the whole invariant on the last club alphabetically, which came out at −18.8. (3) Fitting on all 28 seasons produced a rating barely related to this season's scoring (correlation 0.027): Jacksonville scored 29.9 a game and was handed a negative offence. A two-season window fixes it, and tests/test_model.py now fails on each of the three.
