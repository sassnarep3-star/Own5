Origination: side, then total, then moneyline

Numbers are computed in that order because that is the order in which a line is built: the side sets the game's balance, the total sets its size, the moneyline is the price of the side. Every row carries the hash of the exact inputs that produced it.

Units matter, so they are in the table. A side is a printed home line, where a negative number means the home club is favoured. A total is a points line. A moneyline is the probability the home club wins, between 0 and 1. An earlier version of this desk published the side fair line as a home margin while the market column was a printed line; every sign inverted and it manufactured 23-point edges on games the model and the market agreed about. Both columns now share one convention, and tests/test_model.py fails if they ever stop.

How the fair line is produced

StepWhat happensWhere it is asserted
1 ratingsoffence and defence per club, ridge-fitted on the two most recent seasons, walk-forward, so a game is only ever priced by a fit that predates it tests/test_model.py::TestRatings
2 marginhfa + net(home) − net(away), with net = offence + defence and a defence that is positive when goodsign of the defence parameter is tested
3 total2·mu + hfa + (off−def) for each club the mean predicted total equals the mean actual total
4 probabilityhome win probability from a normal margin with the measured residual spread the side skill is bounded, because an unbounded one means leakage
5 de-vigeach book's two-way moneyline removed of its margin, then averaged devig_two_way sums to one
What would change my mind: a walk-forward Brier skill above zero with a clustered interval that excludes zero, on at least 4,721 bets. Nothing on this page comes close, and the size of the gap is published rather than hidden.
