F03 · nfl.com terms prohibit systematic retrieval without written consent BLOCKING

Section 1.3 of the NFL.com terms permits personal, non-commercial, informational use and prohibits systematic retrieval of data to compile a collection or database absent express prior written consent. The desk therefore stores hashed, dated manual reads and does not run an unattended scraper against nfl.com.

What it leaves blank: every multi-day automated capture requirement (R20, R29, R35, R36, R50) is recorded as BLOCKED rather than attempted

F13 · Seven defects were found in this desk's own build, fixed, and are recorded BLOCKING

(1) A league average left inside a margin expression inflated every predicted margin by about 22 points. (2) A plus-signed defence produced a defence column that correlated positively with points allowed. (3) Sum-to-zero by elimination parked the invariant on the last club alphabetically at -18.8. (4) Fitting on all 28 seasons produced ratings unrelated to this season's scoring. (5) A benchmark probability centred on the closing line produced an impossible +0.30 skill. (6) The fair line and the market line were in opposite sign conventions, inventing 23-point edges. (7) The corpus spread sign was read the wrong way round, giving a closing-line error no real line produces. All seven are now covered by tests.

What it leaves blank: nothing

F06 · The solved home-field edge depends heavily on the window REVIEW

On the two-season window the home edge is +1.17 points. Fitted on all 28 seasons it is +2.18, and on the 2026 season alone it is -0.56. The shipped model uses the two-season window because that is the window whose ratings actually track scoring, but the home edge is clearly sensitive to the choice and is reported rather than smoothed.

What it leaves blank: nothing

F08 · The pinned historical corpus is not an official NFL source REVIEW

The walk-forward and the closing-line benchmark use the committed nflverse corpus, a third-party dataset. It is used because no official machine-readable closing-line history exists. Its Week 10 2026 rows were reconciled 14 of 14 against today's official finals, which is the only cross-check available and is not a guarantee.

What it leaves blank: nothing; the source class is stated on every page that quotes it

F12 · League average points per club is higher this season INFO

The shipped two-season fit puts mu at 22.44 points per club per game, while a 2026-only fit puts it at 23.35 and the 1999-2025 corpus averages about 21. Combined with a small home edge this makes the model's totals run above the market on several Week 11 games, which is visible in the difference column and is not corrected by hand.
