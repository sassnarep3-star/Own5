Validation gates

Thirteen gates, published thresholds, and the per-strategy matrix.

Every strategy is scored against the same thirteen gates. A strategy is CERTIFIED only when every hard gate passes; anything else stays provisional no matter how good the win rate looks.

GateNameCriterionWhy it existsSource
G0Pre-registrationThe rule, threshold and sample window are committed before results are inspected.Research integrity; stops post-hoc threshold selection.source
G1Minimum samplen >= 300 graded bets before a strategy may be listed, and a power check for certification.A 55% true rate and a 52.38% break-even need ~2,241 bets at 80% power.source
G2Significance vs multiplicityExact two-sided binomial p < 0.05 / k, where k is the number of variants tested.Keeps false discoveries bounded when many families are tried.source
G3Chronological holdoutThe final 30% of bets, by date, must clear break-even on its own.Guards against fitting the past.source
G4Closing-line valueAverage CLV >= 0 against a verified closing price.The industry skill metric; requires a price history (F02).source
G5Correlation cap|r| < 0.50 against every live strategy on overlapping bets.Prevents disguised duplicate exposure.source
G6DrawdownMaximum drawdown < 20 units on a 100-unit bankroll.Risk control, not just selection.source
G7Staking<= quarter Kelly, capped at 2% of bankroll per bet.Stops one bet from dominating the record.source
G8Market integrityAll prices traceable to a verified operator or official source.Aggregator lines do not qualify (F01).source
G9Parlay correlationNo correlated legs without the operator's same-game price.Correlated legs priced as independent are a guaranteed loss.source
G10Grading integrityOfficial final scores; pushes void; player props void if the player is inactive.Prevents mis-graded results.source
G11LiquidityStake within posted limits; books may refuse or limit any bet.Operational, not statistical.source
G12Responsible gamblingAdults only, legal jurisdiction only, support at 1-800-GAMBLER.Applies to every page and every strategy.source

The honest consequence

Because G4 needs a verified closing price and G8 needs a verified operator price, the correct current statement is: no strategy in this project is certified as skill-based. That is not a failure of the analysis; it is the analysis working.
