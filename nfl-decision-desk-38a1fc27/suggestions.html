Recommendation

S04 Publish a market-efficiency scorecard per signal Recommended now

S11 Price correlated legs with an empirical copula, and refuse independent pricing Recommended now
Problem
Same-team QB + WR1 legs are correlated (r = 0.50 on 2024-2026); at median lines the joint hit rate is 32.5% versus 24.8% if treated as independent, a +7.7 pp gap that changes the fair price materially.
Recommendation
Require an empirical joint estimate for any same-game combination; publish the implied fair price next to the offered price and reject the bet when the offered price assumes independence.
