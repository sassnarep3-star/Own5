Removing luck

  • Break-even at the price, not at 50%. -110 requires 52.38%, so every test is against 52.38%.
  • Multiplicity is counted. 7 variants tested, so α = 0.00714. Buckets found after inspection are labelled exploratory.
  • Chronological holdout. The last 30% of bets, by date, must clear break-even independently.
  • Permutation null. 500 shuffles of the deficiency labels give an empirical null distribution for every slope we publish.
  • Power, not vibes. A 55% true rate needs about 2,241 bets to be distinguished from 52.38% at 80% power.

Parlay and same-game construction

A two-leg -110 parlay needs a joint 27.44% (0.5238²). Independent break-even legs deliver exactly that, so the parlay price itself carries no edge — only correlation does. Same-team legs are strongly correlated (see correlations), which is why the operator's same-game price, not the parlay multiplier, is the only fair test.
