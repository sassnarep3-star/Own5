What this means for combination bets

Cross-market legs (spread and total) are effectively independent, so a two-leg parlay across them is fairly priced by the multiplier. Same-team legs are not: passing volume drives both the quarterback's yards and his receiver's, so pricing them as independent overstates the payout. Same-team combinations should only be taken at the operator's same-game price, where the book applies its own correlation model.
