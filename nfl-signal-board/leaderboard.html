Leaderboard &amp; pre-registration

The leaderboard is a competition with gates, not a display of past performance. Two rules make it defensible: signal and staking live in separate columns, so a lucky staking run cannot lift a bad signal; and no backtest number ever appears here — a strategy earns a figure only by settling bets after its registration.

Everyone starts at zero. There are no settled bets since registration, so every strategy is UNCERTIFIED. The ranking key, when numbers exist, is the lower bound of the skill interval, not the point estimate, and never win rate or season-long ROI.

Pre-registration register

A strategy is registered by freezing its rule text, its sample window and its gates, hashing the frozen artifact, and committing that hash before the evaluation window opens. The commit is the registration. Changing a rule requires a new registration id.
