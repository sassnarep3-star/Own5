Two corrections this build made to itself

The spread sign. A first pass treated the pinned corpus's closing_spread_home as home-favoured-negative and produced a side skill of +0.038 with an interval excluding zero. The data says the opposite: scored as home-favoured-positive the mean absolute error is 10.25 points against 14.62 the other way. With the sign fixed, the skill is negative. The first number was a sign error, and it is recorded here rather than quietly replaced.
The benchmark. A second pass scored the side against the de-vigged moneyline probability. A constant 0.5 predictor beat that benchmark by 0.139, which is impossible if the benchmark were a cover probability — the control exposed the error. The side is now scored against a coin flip at the closing line, and the same control returns exactly 0.0000, which is what a correct benchmark must do.
