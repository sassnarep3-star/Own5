What I would do first, if the choice were mine

1. Capture the official inactive list for one week before building anything else (SUG04). It is the single input that converts "a player is out" into "this backup is playing", which is the whole depth feature. 2. Use the game book as the starter record instead of a depth chart (SUG05): it is published, dated and does not need a per-club parser. 3. Do not touch the model until those two inputs exist — with a calibration slope of 0.11 the binding constraint is the model, not the data volume.

Policy P-01: the public site carries derived aggregates, our own model outputs and operator-attributed prices only; row-level official data stays private. No bets are placed, no performance is claimed, and no strategy on this site is certified. This is research, not betting advice.
