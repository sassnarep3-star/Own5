Overview

The headline, unsoftened
The model does not beat the market.

On 2,951 priced games from 2015 to 2026, our side probability scores a Brier skill of -0.0354 against a coin flip at the closing line (95% cluster interval -0.0508 to -0.0202). The moneyline scores -0.0579 against the de-vigged close. The calibration slope on train is 0.110: the raw probabilities are badly over-confident, so the raw edges on the origination page (up to +0.76 EV per unit) are an artefact and no stake is taken from any of them.

That is the useful result. It is published first because a site that hides it is worse than no site. All 78 market rows, 13 fixtures and 78 operator prices are below with clocks and links.

Scope note. This repository already contains 50+ prior desks built by parallel sessions. This board is additive and does not edit their files. Where an earlier desk's claim and an official page disagree, the official page wins and the disagreement is listed on Flagged for review. Where two desks disagree with each other, both are recorded and neither is adopted here.

Policy P-01: the public site carries derived aggregates, our own model outputs and operator-attributed prices only; row-level official data stays private. No bets are placed, no performance is claimed, and no strategy on this site is certified. This is research, not betting advice.
