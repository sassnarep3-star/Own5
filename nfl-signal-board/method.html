3. How every number was produced

NumberMethodFailure mode it is guarded against
The ratingsa walk-forward Elo updated game by game with a season regression and a home-field termlookahead: a game can only move a rating after it is played
The projected margina linear map from the rating difference, fitted on 2015–2024 onlytuning on the holdout
The projected totalrolling offence and defence scoring rates with shrinkage toward the league meansmall-sample rates driving fake edges
The cover probabilitya normal dispersion model around the projected margin, with sigma chosen on trainchoosing sigma on the evaluation window
The skill1 − (model Brier ÷ benchmark Brier), with a week-cluster bootstraptreating correlated games as independent
The multiplicity controlHolm adjustment across the registered familiesfinding a winner by trying many variants
The calibration slopea logistic fit of outcome on the log-odds of the model probability, train onlystaking inflated probabilities
The reconciliation32 club records recomputed from games and compared to the official page; the build fails on any mismatcha corpus that silently drifts from the official record

4. Determinism

The data build uses a fixed seed ({summary['model'].get('seed', 20261119)}) and no wall-clock value, so two consecutive runs produce byte-identical CSVs. The bootstrap uses a linear congruential generator implemented in the build rather than a library, so the intervals reproduce on any machine with Python 3.11 and no third-party packages. verify.py re-runs the build and fails if the committed files differ.
