Correlations

Correlations are computed only from numbers on an official page, and n is printed with every value. The inputs here are today's official league standings: 32 clubs, each with a record and points for/against. Nothing is imported from a third-party dataset.

What these are not. They are team-level, season-to-date relationships. They are not parlay-leg correlations, and using them as if they were is exactly the error the brief warns about: multiplying marginal probabilities is invalid when the legs are dependent. The leg-level coefficient needs play-by-play, which is not held here (Q09).
