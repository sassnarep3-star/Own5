Availability & depth

The brief asks for a live picture of who is out, who replaces them, and how deep the replacement is. The official Week 11 report gives us designations. It does not give us the inactive list, a named replacement, a depth order or a snap count. So the table below publishes exactly what the official page prints, and leaves the replacement columns blank with the gate that would fill them.

Why the replacement column is empty. A designation tells us a player is doubtful or out. It does not tell us who takes the snaps. Naming a backup from a depth chart would be an inference the brief forbids, and the club depth pages are not a verified structured source for all 32 clubs. The gate is therefore explicit: an official inactive list plus announced-starter evidence (or a game book).
