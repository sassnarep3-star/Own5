Executive Summary

1. What this project is

A self-running system that (a) originates its own NFL lines from official data, (b) tests betting strategies — singles, parlays, player-prop combos — under statistical validation gates that separate skill from luck, and (c) runs them as a leaderboard competition rewarding only gate-passing, luck-adjusted performance. No manual input required.

2. Headline findings

  • Market close to efficient, not perfect: favorites cover 48.1%, home underdogs 52.6%, overs 49.1% (10,228 games 1980–2024, excl. pushes). Sign-consistent with independent academic work (Szalkowski et al. 2012; Paul & Weinbach).
  • The best-looking strategy still fails the luck test: home underdog ≥7 hit 56.37% (n=793, modeled +8.4% ROI at −110) but p = 0.0134 misses the pre-registered Bonferroni threshold α = 0.00625. Zero strategies are certified skill today — by design. S1/S2 continue as Candidates for live CLV tracking.
  • One season cannot prove skill: a break-even bettor posts ≥55% over 200 bets 20.8% of the time; 2,243 graded bets are needed to detect true 55% skill (80% power).
  • Parlays punish the naive: cross-game independence confirmed (0.984 ratio) — 2-leg fixed parlays need 27.47% joint hits vs ≈23.6% from break-even legs.
  • Real correlation is in player props: QB pass yards vs top-WR yards r = 0.76 (n=3,852); QB vs game total r = 0.35; RB rush yards vs margin r = 0.21. Same-game combos require empirical joint pricing.
  • Our lines are live: v1 model (shrunk net-points ratings + 2.0-pt HFA; measured HFA 2.22) originated all 15 games of 2026 Week 9 from official inputs; market columns blank + flagged (no official market-line source exists).

3. How luck is removed

Pre-registration + Bonferroni across all variants tried (k=8) · era-stability gates · closing-line-value gate for live promotion · deflated leaderboard scoring (Bailey & López de Prado 2014) · Bayesian shrinkage of displayed win rates · fractional-Kelly bankroll (Thorp 2011) · losers published alongside winners.

4. Verification discipline

Every table row carries a source link; unverifiable values are blank and flagged on Flagged for Review (F1–F11). Official verification samples: 2026 standings (32 teams) and 2025 Week 1 (16/16 game scores matched nfl.com game pages).
