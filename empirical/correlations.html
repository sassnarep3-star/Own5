i
Continuous and thresholded outcomes are different estimands.

A positive yardage correlation does not require positive correlation between particular over/under indicators. No pairwise coefficient can identify an arbitrary three-leg joint distribution. Condition on matchup, roles, availability, exact threshold and settlement before attempting a prospective model.
