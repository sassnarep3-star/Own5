How to verify the case data

  1. Compare every lease/addendum and move-in/roommate notice against the reported dates, exact rent, and named parties. Keep these originals private.
  2. Reconstruct rent actually demanded and paid, effective dates of all rent notices, and any pass-throughs separately from base rent.
  3. Check each unit's Rent Board Housing Inventory / license status and official rent history using the exact address only in the secure Rent Board portal. Do not publish an address, license document, or tenant record.
  4. For Unit A, ask a tenant-side San Francisco housing attorney to decide whether the 2026 occupant event changed the rent-control anniversary or any initial/base rent rule.
