Executive summary

A defensible rent increase is one that is legal, calculated from the correct unit-specific records, served correctly, and supported by consistent non-discriminatory decision-making—not merely one that appears small or matches the neighboring unit.

What is a reasonable and normal increase for covered rent-controlled units?Use the SF Rent Board annual allowance in effect on the proposed effective date as the routine ceiling; 1.6% for Mar. 1, 2026–Feb. 28, 2027. Taking less or nothing is allowed. “Normal” neighborhood practice cannot be established from an official rate schedule. Do not infer that a market rent comparison authorizes an increase.SF annual-increase rules ↗
Does the 1940 building date prove rent control?No. It supports likely coverage under the City's general pre-June 14, 1979 category, but property form and exceptions matter; year 1940 is not independently verified here.SF coverage guide ↗
What do large corporations vs. individual owners usually do?No official comparative evidence was identified to substantiate a claim about what either group typically does. Both are subject to the same applicable rent/notice/fair-housing rules. A documented checklist and consistent, unit-by-unit review is a prudent process recommendation—not a claim about industry-wide behavior.SF Rent Board requirements ↗
What is a proper reason for increasing rent?A routine annual allowable increase does not require a cost-based justification, but the limit, timing, notice and license apply. Above-allowance cost recovery must fit a specific petition/pass-through rule and its proof/process; ordinary maintenance is not automatically recoverable as a capital-improvement charge.Rent Board petitions ↗
Should both units receive an increase?There is no rule identified requiring increases to both units. Nor is raising both a way to guarantee no discrimination inquiry. Use one neutral written policy but determine eligibility, date, base rent, amount and notice separately for each tenancy.Fair Housing Act terms / conditions ↗
Can tenants get free lawyer review?Free tenant counseling and some legal help exist, but provider scope, availability and eligibility vary. The Rent Board provides free counseling about its ordinance, not legal representation; some services have income, geography, disability/age or case-type limits. Do not promise that every tenant can obtain a free attorney to pre-review an annual notice.SF Rent Board referral list ↗

Conservative recommendation

First verify exact unit coverage and Rent Board license; assemble a complete rent ledger; establish the applicable anniversary and any legitimately banked/petitioned amounts; apply the rate effective on the intended date; provide legally sufficient notice; retain the workpaper and neutral policy. Because Unit A involves a described household change and Unit B lacks rent history, an independent tenant-side attorney review before serving a notice is prudent. It cannot guarantee that a lawyer will find it lawful or that free representation is available.

Quick practical guide

  1. Do not rush: no immediate notice is recommended on these incomplete records.
  2. Do not mirror amounts mechanically: even under one percent policy, rent amounts and dates differ.
  3. Do not add a separate amount for an additional roommate: SF's ordinance generally prohibits charging merely because a tenant adds an occupant.
  4. Do not label expenses as the reason for an above-allowance increase without a valid petition: follow the exact petition route and retain proof.
  5. Do not frame an increase as retaliation: avoid linking it to a tenant complaint, repair request, accommodation, or protected activity; use documented neutral criteria and check fair-housing rights.
