Privacy: tenant names and initials are not published. Units use neutral labels; dates and rent amounts supplied for the analysis are retained and clearly marked as owner-reported, not independently verified.

1.6%
SF controlled-unit annual increase

Official allowable rate for effective dates March 1, 2026–February 28, 2027. It is not a mandatory increase or a prediction of next year's rate. Official rate table ↗

12 mo.
Minimum annual interval

For a covered local rent-controlled tenancy, timing is tied to the last increase or the lease start if no increase was made; facts about a changing household may affect the date. Rent Board rules ↗

30 / 90
Days written notice

At least 30 days for increases of 10% or less; at least 90 days if greater than 10%, plus 5 days when mailed under the city's guidance. California Civil Code § 827 ↗

Key practical conclusion

Do not send a rent increase notice yet on the information in this file. The current lawful base rent, last effective increase, rent-increase license, exact unit coverage, and (for Unit A) treatment of the occupant change have not been verified. The annual percentage for a proposed April 2027 increase is not established by today's 2026–27 rate table.

Does an older construction year alone prove coverage?No. The city says units before June 14, 1979 may be covered, with unit-type and other exceptions; it directs users to confirm coverage.SF rental law guide ↗
Does raising both units remove discrimination risk?No official source creates that safe harbor. Matching a percentage does not cure an unlawful increase or prove nondiscrimination; use neutral criteria and individually correct legal calculations.42 U.S.C. § 3604 ↗
