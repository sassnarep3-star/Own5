Notice content check

Identify

  • Tenant(s) and unit correctly (in private notice, not on this public website)
  • Current lawful base rent and proposed increase component(s)
  • Dollar increase and percentage increase
  • Effective date and payment date

Explain

  • Separate current annual allowance and any banked portion
  • State any petitioned pass-through and reference the official decision/process where appropriate
  • Include extra state-required language where AB 1482 applies
  • Provide accurate contact information and keep proof of service

Official San Francisco materials say a rent increase notice should include the increase amount, percentage and effective date; banked increases require identifying the banked component. Local ordinance § 37.3 also specifies written disclosures for portions of increases. Confirm the exact notice form/content for the increase type and tenancy before service.

Tenant legal help and independent review

A tenant may ask the Rent Board for free counseling about the Rent Ordinance or approach a referral organization for possible legal services. This does not mean every tenant has a guaranteed free attorney. For attorney review of a proposed increase, share an accurate, itemized calculation and relevant lease/rent history without pressuring the tenant to forego counsel.

Unit A added-occupant / successor issue

The Rent Board's official guidance says additional rent may not be imposed solely because a new roommate is added to an existing tenancy. If the original occupant(s) have left, Costa-Hawkins can permit a market-rent change in some situations, but the City warns these cases are complicated. The exact occupancy facts must be reviewed before treating the April 1, 2026 event as a new rent base.
